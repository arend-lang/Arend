### Logic.Rewriting.TRS.Substitutions

Substitution operations for higher-order terms.

- **`plain-identity`**: Identity substitution.
- **`plain-identity-effect`**: Applying identity substitution is the identity.
- **`weakening`**: Weakens a term from a narrow context to a wider context via a `SubList`.
- **`Substitution.apply`**: Applies a substitution to a term.
- **`MetaSubstitution.apply`**: Applies a meta-substitution to a term.
