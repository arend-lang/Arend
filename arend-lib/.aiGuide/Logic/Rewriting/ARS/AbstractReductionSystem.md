### Logic.Rewriting.ARS.AbstractReductionSystem

Defines abstract reduction systems (ARS) and the basic relations derived from a reduction relation.

An `AbstractReductionSystem` is a set `A` equipped with a reduction relation `~>` and a separate symmetric relation `|--|` that represents an extra "equivalence" layer (e.g., for rewriting modulo a congruence). Specializing `|--|` to propositional equality yields a `SimpleARS`. From the primitive `~>` and `|--|`, the module systematically builds reflexive, transitive, symmetric, and reflexive-transitive closures, plus joinability and conversion relations, which are the standard tools for stating confluence, normalization, and Church–Rosser-style properties.

#### Core Class

- **`AbstractReductionSystem`**: A set `A` with a reduction relation `~> : Rel A` and a symmetric relation `|--| : Rel A` (with `|--|_symmetric` witnessing symmetry). The intended reading is "one-step reduction" plus an auxiliary equivalence layer.
- **`SimpleARS`**: Extends `AbstractReductionSystem` by fixing `|--|` to be propositional equality `=`, with symmetry given by path inversion. Use when no extra equivalence layer is needed.

#### Reduction Relation Variants

- **`~>_0`**: Equality `=` viewed as a relation on an ARS (zero reduction steps as identity).
- **`~>_1`**: The base one-step reduction `A.~>`.
- **`~>_=`**: Reflexive closure of `~>` (zero or one step).
- **`~>_+`**: Transitive closure of `~>` (one or more steps).
- **`~>_*`**: Reflexive-transitive closure of `~>` (zero or more steps); the standard "reduces to" relation.
- **`<~`**: Reverse reduction: `a <~ b` iff `b ~> a`.
- **`<~>`**: Symmetric closure of `~>` (one step in either direction).
- **`<~>*`**: Reflexive-transitive closure of `<~>`; the conversion relation generated by `~>`.

#### Conversion Lemmas

- **`<~>-reverse`**: Symmetry of `<~>`: swaps `sc-left` and `sc-right`.
- **`<~>*-compose`**: Transitivity of `<~>*`: composes two conversion sequences via an intermediate point.
- **`<~>*-reverse`**: Symmetry of `<~>*`: reverses a conversion sequence.
- **`~>=><~>*`**: Embeds a single reduction step into the conversion relation.
- **`~>*=><~>*`**: Embeds a multi-step reduction into the conversion relation.
- **`~>*-concat`**: Transitivity of `~>_*`: concatenates two reduction sequences.
- **`~>_=-append`**: Prepends an at-most-one-step reduction onto a multi-step reduction.

#### Joinability

- **`Join`**: The proposition that `a` and `b` have a common reduct: `\exists c, a ~>_* c \wedge b ~>_* c`. Constructor `join` packages the witness and the two reduction paths. Joinability is the standard notion underlying confluence.
- **`Join.append-left`**: If `c ~>_* a` and `Join a b`, then `Join c b` (extend the joining on the left).
- **`Join.append-right`**: If `c ~>_* b` and `Join a b`, then `Join a c` (extend the joining on the right).

#### Equivalence-Layer Relations

- **`~`**: Reflexive-transitive closure of `|--|`; the equivalence generated by the auxiliary symmetric relation.
- **`|==|`**: One-step "convertibility modulo `~`": either a forward reduction (`red-to`), a backward reduction (`red-from`), or a `~`-equivalence step (`eq`).
- **`Conversion~`**: Reflexive-transitive closure of `|==|`; full conversion that mixes reductions in both directions with `~`-equivalences.
- **`Join~`**: Joinability up to `~`: there exist `c`, `d` with `a ~>_* c`, `c ~ d`, and `b ~>_* d`. Constructor `join~` packages the witnesses; this is the joinability notion appropriate for rewriting modulo `~`.
- **`~>~`**: One-step reduction modulo `~`: `a ~>~ b` iff there exist `c`, `d` with `a ~ c`, `c ~> d`, `d ~ b`. Constructor `red~` packages the witnesses; this lifts `~>` to the quotient by `~`.
