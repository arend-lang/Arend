### Logic.Rewriting.ARS.Confluence

Confluence properties for abstract reduction systems.

- **`StrongJoin`**: Data type witnessing a strong joinability of two elements.
- **`isStronglyConfluent`**, **`isChurchRosser`**, **`isConfluent`**, **`isLocallyConfluent`**: Propositions expressing various confluence notions.
- **`hasUniqueNormalFormsWrtReduction`**, **`hasUniqueNormalForms`**, **`hasNormalFormProperty`**: Normal form uniqueness properties.
- **`ChurchRosser=>Confluence`**, **`Confluence=>ChurchRosser`**: Equivalence of Church-Rosser and confluence.
- **`StrongConfluence=>Confluence`**: Strong confluence implies confluence.
- **`Newman`**: Newman's lemma — local confluence + termination implies confluence.
