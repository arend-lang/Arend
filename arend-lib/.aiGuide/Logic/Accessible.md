### Logic.Accessible

- **`Acc`**: Standard accessibility predicate on a relation `R`, generated by `acc` from accessibility of all `R`-predecessors `b` with `R b a`.
- **`nat-acc`**: Every natural number `n` is accessible with respect to `<`.
- **`map-acc`**: Accessibility pulls back along a map `f : A -> B` of sets: if `f a` is `R`-accessible, then `a` is accessible for the relation `\lam a1 a2 => R (f a1) (f a2)`.
