### Set.SetCategory

The category of sets, its (co)limits, and a structure identity principle specialized to `\Set`.

This module instantiates `SetCat`, the category whose objects are h-sets and whose morphisms are functions, and proves it is bicomplete by exhibiting concrete limits (cones into the unit type) and colimits (built via coproducts and coequalizers). Coproducts use the set truncation of a dependent pair, coequalizers use a quotient by the relation generated by the parallel pair, and general filtered/diagram colimits are presented as `SetColimit`, a quotient of `\Sigma j, F j`. A `SetCoproduct` and `SetCoequalizer` are provided in `SetCat.op` so that colimits in `SetCat` reduce to limits in the opposite category. A specialization of the SIP to sets and a helper for building functors out of `Nat` round out the module.

#### Category Instance

- **`SetCat`**: The category of h-sets, with `Hom X Y = X -> Y`, identity, composition, and proven univalent.
- **`SIP_Set`**: Structure identity principle specialized to `SetCat`: from an iso `e : X ≃ Y` and structures `S1, S2` with mutual homomorphism witnesses, produces `p : X = Y` together with a dependent path of structures and a pointwise computation `transport (\lam Z => Z) p x = e.f x`.

#### Limits and Colimits

- **`SetCoproduct`**: For `F : J -> \Set`, the coproduct in `SetCat` (i.e. product in `SetCat.op`) given by `Trunc0 (\Sigma (j : J) (F j))` with injections `\lam j x => in0 (j, x)`.
- **`SetCoequalizer`**: For parallel `f g : X -> Y`, the coequalizer in `SetCat` realized as the quotient of `Y` by the relation `\Sigma (x : X) (f x = y) (g x = y')`.
- **`SetBicat`**: Witnesses that `SetCat` is bicomplete (`BicompleteCat`). Limits are computed as cones `Cone F (\Sigma)` from the unit set; colimits are obtained via `limits<=pr+eq` from `SetCoproduct` and `SetCoequalizer` in the opposite category.
- **`SetBicat.cone-isLim`**: Helper showing that a cone whose canonical map to the cone-pullback over `\Sigma` is an equivalence is in fact a limit.

#### Set Colimits over Small Precategories

- **`SetColimit`**: Concrete colimit of a functor `F : Functor J SetCat` for `J : SmallPrecat`, defined as the quotient of `\Sigma (j : J) (F j)` by the relation `\Sigma (p : Hom s.1 s'.1) (F.Func p s.2 = s'.2)`.
- **`SetColimit.inC`**: Inclusion of a pair `(j, a)` into the colimit.
- **`SetColimit.~-cequiv`**: From a morphism `f : Hom s.1 s'.1` with `F.Func f s.2 = s'.2`, derive equality `in~ s = in~ s'` in `SetColimit F`.
- **`SetColimit.unext-ub`**: For a join-semilattice `J`, equality `inC s = inC s'` in the colimit yields a common upper bound `j` together with witnesses that `s` and `s'` are sent to the same element of `F j`.
- **`SetColimit.Func-app`**: Functoriality applied pointwise: `F.Func (g ∘ f) a = F.Func g (F.Func f a)`.
- **`SetColimit.poset-app`**: For a poset-shaped diagram, any two morphisms `f g : j <= j'` act identically: `F.Func f a = F.Func g a`.
- **`SetColimit.poset-id`**: For a poset, any reflexivity `p : j <= j` acts as the identity: `F.Func p a = a`.
- **`SetColimit.inMap`**: Curried inclusion `(j : J) -> F j -> SetColimit F`.
- **`SetColimit.inMap-coh`**: Coherence: `inMap j a = inMap j' (F.Func f a)` along any `f : Hom j j'`.

#### Functors out of `Nat`

- **`NatFunctor`**: Builds a functor `NatSemiring -> C` from a sequence `F : Nat -> C` and successor morphisms `f : Hom (F n) (F (suc n))`, by composing the successor maps along the unique witness of `n <= m`.
- **`NatFunctor.sigma-isProp`**: The witness type `\Sigma (k : Nat) (n + k = m)` is a proposition, justifying canonical choice of bridging morphism.
- **`NatFunctor.natHom`**: Constructs `Hom (F n) (F m)` by induction on the gap `k` with `n + k = m`, iterating `f`.
- **`NatFunctor.natHom_id`**: `natHom (0, idp)` equals the identity morphism.
- **`NatFunctor.natHom-comp`**: Composition law: `natHom` of a sum of gaps equals the composite of the individual `natHom`s.
