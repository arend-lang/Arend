### Set.Filter

Filters on meet-semilattices and their specializations to subsets of a set, including completeness and properness conditions.

A `Filter` is an upward-closed, finite-meet-stable subset of a top meet-semilattice, generalizing the classical notion of a filter on a poset or set. The module specializes this to `SetFilter` (filters on the powerset lattice `SetLattice X`), then layers properness conditions: `WeaklyProperFilter` excludes the empty set, while `ProperFilter` strengthens this by requiring every member to be inhabited. The design provides functorial action via preimage (`SetFilter-map` and variants) and equips proper/weakly-proper filters with a meet-semilattice structure where the meet is intersection of membership predicates.

#### Core Filter Structure

- **`Filter`**: Record on a `TopMeetSemilattice A` carrying a predicate `F : A -> \Prop` closed under upward-monotonicity (`filter-mono`), containing `top` (`filter-top`), and closed under binary meets (`filter-meet`).
- **`Filter.principal`**: The principal filter generated by an element `a : A`, defined as `{b | a <= b}`.

#### Complete Filters

- **`CompleteFilter`**: Extends `Filter` over a `CompleteLattice`, additionally requiring that membership of an arbitrary `Join f` implies membership of some `f j` (`filter-Join`).
- **`CompleteFilter.isProper`**: Lemma that a complete filter containing `bottom` is empty (i.e. yields `Empty`), expressing the standard properness criterion.

#### Set Filters

- **`SetFilter`**: A `Filter` specialized to `A := SetLattice X`, i.e. a filter of subsets of a set `X`.
- **`SetFilter-map`**: Pushforward of a `SetFilter` along `f : X -> Y`, defined by preimage: `V` belongs to the image filter iff `f ^-1 V` belongs to the source.

#### Properness Conditions

- **`WeaklyProperFilter`**: Extends `SetFilter` with `isWeaklyProper : Not (F bottom)`, i.e. the empty subset is not in the filter.
- **`ProperFilter`**: Extends `WeaklyProperFilter` with `isProper`: every `U` in the filter is inhabited (`∃ U`); weak properness is derived automatically.
- **`pointFilter`**: The principal proper filter at a point `x : X`, consisting of all subsets `U` with `U x`.

#### Functoriality of Proper Filters

- **`WeaklyProperFilter-map`**: Pushforward along `f : X -> Y` preserving weak properness; reuses `SetFilter-map`.
- **`ProperFilter-map`**: Pushforward preserving properness; reuses `SetFilter-map`.

#### Lattice Structure on Filters

- **`WeaklyProperFilterSemilattice`**: `MeetSemilattice` instance on `WeaklyProperFilter X` with order given by inclusion of membership predicates and meet given by intersection (`F U ∧ G U` as a Σ-type).
- **`ProperFilterSemilattice`**: Analogous `MeetSemilattice` instance on `ProperFilter X`, with the meet inheriting properness from the left component.
