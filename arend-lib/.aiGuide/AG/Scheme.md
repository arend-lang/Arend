### AG.Scheme

Affine schemes and general schemes formalized via locales of prime ideals and locally ringed structures.

This module builds the spectrum of a commutative ring as a locale presented by a frame presentation `SpecPres`, where basic opens are ring elements and the cover relation captures membership in the radical of an ideal generated by a family. Schemes are defined as locally ringed locales — locales equipped with a sheaf of rings whose stalks are local in the sense that every section either it or its `+1` shift is locally invertible — that are locally isomorphic to affine schemes. The construction goes through frame presentations (`RingedFramePres`, `LocallyRingedFramePres`) so that a sheaf can be specified on basic opens (powers localizations of the ring) and then extended to the full presented frame, with descent verified by gluing localizations via partition-of-unity arguments on power identities like `e^n = a·c + b·d`.

#### Spectrum as a Frame Presentation

- **`SpecPres`**: Frame presentation of `Spec R` for a commutative ring `R`. Generators are ring elements, conjunction is multiplication, and basic covers encode that `a + b` is covered by `{a, b}` and that `0` is covered by the empty family.
- **`SpecPres.cover_ideal`**: A cover `Cover x g` in `SpecPres R` implies `x` lies in the radical of the ideal generated by `g`.
- **`SpecPres.ideal_cover`**: Converse — radical membership yields a cover.
- **`SpecPres.cover_ide`**: Every element is covered by `1`.
- **`SpecPres.cover_pow`**: `x` is covered by any of its powers `pow x n`.
- **`SpecPres.cover_bigSum`**: A sum `BigSum l` is covered by the array `l` of its summands.
- **`Spec`**: The Zariski locale of a commutative ring, defined as `PresentedFrame (SpecPres R)`.

#### Ringed Locales

- **`RingedLocale`**: Record bundling a locale `L` with a sheaf of commutative rings `R : VSheaf CRingCat L`. Coercible to its underlying locale.
- **`RingedLocale.fromSheaf`**: Coerces a `VSheaf CRingCat L` to a `RingedLocale`.
- **`RingedLocale.equality1` / `equality2` / `equality3`**: A chain of equivalences characterizing equality of ringed locales — first as a pair (locale path, transported-sheaf path), then via direct image along the path-induced iso, and finally as a pair of isos (locale iso, sheaf iso along its direct image). Provides univalence-style identification.

#### Morphisms of Ringed Locales

- **`RingedLocaleHom`**: A morphism `Dom -> Cod` consists of a frame map `f : FrameHom Cod Dom` together with a natural transformation `f# : Cod.R -> direct_image f Dom.R` of sheaves.
- **`RingedLocaleHom.fromHomEq`**: From `f = g` derives the inequality `f a <= g a` used to compare sheaf actions.
- **`RingedLocaleHom.ext`**: Extensionality — equality of two ringed-locale morphisms reduces to equality of underlying frame maps plus pointwise compatibility on the sheaf component.

#### The Category of Ringed Locales

- **`RingedLocalePrecat`**: Precategory of ringed locales. Composition combines frame composition with the appropriate composition of the sheaf-component natural transformations.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.forget`**: Forgetful functor `RingedLocalePrecat -> LocaleCat`.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.homMap`**: Given `p : f = g` between ringed-locale homs and `a : L`, produces the induced ring isomorphism `M.R (f a) -> M.R (g a)` via the sheaf's transport of `p`.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.homMap-lem` / `homMap-lem2`**: Identify `homMap` with the transport of `RingHom.id` and show that `homMap p ∘ homMap (inv p) = id`.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.homMap-natural`**: Naturality of `homMap` as a natural transformation between the two sheaf restrictions along `f` and `g`.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.natTrans-inv`**: Inverts the sheaf-component natural transformation along an iso of ringed locales.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.homMap_o` / `homMap_o-right` / `homMap_o-left`**: Coherence lemmas relating `f#` and `g#` for inverse pairs to `homMap` of the unit/counit equations.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.fromIso`**: An iso of ringed locales induces an iso of sheaves between the direct image of the source sheaf and the target sheaf.
- **`RingedLocalePrecat.isotoid`**: Univalence for the precategory of ringed locales — every iso yields an equality.

#### Locally Ringed Locales

- **`LocallyRingedLocale`**: Extends `RingedLocale` with two conditions: `isNonTrivial` (if `0 = 1` over `a`, then `a` is bottom) and `isLocallyRinged` (every section is, on a covering family, either invertible or has invertible `+1`-shift, encoding the local-ring stalk condition).
- **`LocallyRingedLocale.EitherInv`**: The disjunction `Inv (R.F.Func p x) || Inv (R.F.Func p x + 1)` capturing locality of the stalk.

#### Frame Presentation Variants

- **`RingedFramePres`**: A frame presentation `P` together with a sheaf of rings on the basis site `framePresSite P`.
- **`LocallyRingedFramePres`**: Extends `RingedFramePres` with presentation-level analogues `isNonTrivialPres` and `isLocallyRingedPres` of the local-ring conditions, expressed via `Cover` and `SCover` instead of locale joins.
- **`LocallyRingedFramePres.EitherInv`**: Presentation-level version of the locality disjunction.
- **`LocallyRingedFramePres.limInv`**: If a section's value at the basepoint is invertible, then the whole limit element is invertible — used to lift local invertibility from a basis element to the full glued section.
- **`LocallyRingedFramePres.toPresented`**: Transfers a presentation-level `EitherInv` witness to the corresponding `LocallyRingedLocale.EitherInv` after passing through `PresentedFrame` and `extend.lim`.

#### From Presentations to (Locally) Ringed Locales

- **`ringedLocaleFromPres`**: Converts a `RingedFramePres` to a `RingedLocale` by taking the presented frame and the extended sheaf `sheafOnPresentedFrame`.
- **`locallyRingedLocaleFromPres`**: Same conversion, lifted to `LocallyRingedLocale` using the `Pres` versions of the locality conditions.

#### Affine Schemes

- **`affineRingedPres`**: Builds a `LocallyRingedFramePres` on `SpecPres R` whose sheaf sends a basic open `a` to the localization `LocRing (powers a)`. The sheaf condition is verified on the indexing basic covers using `localization-lem`.
- **`affineRingedPres.functor`**: The presheaf of localizations `a |-> LocRing (powers a)` together with functorial maps induced by `cover_ideal`.
- **`affineRingedPres.functorial`**: Given `b ∈ radical (closure1 a)`, constructs the unique ring homomorphism `LocRing (powers a) -> LocRing (powers b)` extending `locMap`, by inverting `a` via the witness that some power of `b` is a multiple of `a`.
- **`affineRingedPres.functorial-lem` / `functorial-lem-div`**: Explicit formulas for the functorial map on a generator, both in the general radical case (`pow b n = a * c`) and the divisibility case (`b = a * d.inv`).
- **`affineRingedPres.emptyMatchingFamily`**: Empty matching families form a contractible type — used in the cover-by-`0` case.
- **`affineRingedPres.common-denom`**: Two elements of `LocRing (powers a)` and `LocRing (powers b)` can be put over common-denominator powers `pow a m`, `pow b m`.
- **`affineRingedPres.pow-lin-comb`**: From `pow e k = a * c + b * d` derive `pow e l = t * pow a n + s * pow b m` for arbitrary `n, m`. Key lemma for promoting partition-of-unity identities to arbitrary powers.
- **`affineRingedPres.localization-lem`**: The matching-family map for a two-element basic cover `{a, b}` of `e` (witnessed by `pow e n = a * c + b * d`) is an equivalence — i.e., the localization presheaf is a sheaf on basic covers.
- **`affineRingedLocale`**: The `LocallyRingedLocale` underlying `Spec R`, namely `locallyRingedLocaleFromPres (affineRingedPres R)`.

#### Schemes

- **`Scheme`**: Extends `LocallyRingedLocale` with `isScheme`: the top element is covered by opens `U` whose restriction `VSheaf.restrict U R` is isomorphic, as a ringed locale, to some `affineRingedLocale R'`. The non-triviality and locality fields are derived automatically by transporting the corresponding properties from each affine restriction along the iso.
- **`affineScheme`**: Promotes `affineRingedLocale R` to a `Scheme`; the cover by affines is given by `R` itself.
- **`affineScheme.locale_iso`**: The canonical iso `L.restrict L.top ≅ L` in `LocaleCat`.
- **`affineScheme.restrict_map`**: The ringed-locale morphism from a restricted ringed locale `(L.restrict a, VSheaf.restrict a S)` back into `(L, S)`.
- **`affineScheme.restrict_iso`**: Upgrades `restrict_map` at `L.top` to an iso of ringed locales — restricting a sheaf to the top open recovers the original ringed locale up to iso.
