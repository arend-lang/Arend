### AG.Projective

Constructs the Proj scheme of a graded commutative ring as a presented locale equipped with a structure sheaf of rings.

This module realizes the classical Proj construction in the locale-theoretic / presented-frame style: basic opens correspond to homogeneous elements of positive degree, and the cover relation is generated by the rule that homogeneous elements `a, b` of the same degree cover `a + b`, plus the rule that the zero element of each degree is covered by the empty family. The structure sheaf is built by gluing local pieces of the form `HomogenLocRing(powers a)` — the degree-zero part of the localization at powers of a homogeneous element — and obtained by pulling back the affine structure sheaf along the canonical morphism `ProjPres R -> SpecPres R` of frame presentations. The resulting locally ringed locale is shown to be a scheme by exhibiting affine charts on each generator.

#### Frame Presentation of Proj

- **`ProjPres`**: The frame presentation `FramePres (Carrier R)` for `Proj R`. Generators are homogeneous elements of positive degree; basic covers encode `a + b ≤ a ∨ b` (same positive degree) and that `0` in each degree is covered by the empty family. Conjunction is multiplication of homogeneous elements.
- **`ProjPres.Carrier`**: Triple `(a : R, n : Nat, isHomogen a (suc n))` — a homogeneous element of strictly positive degree `suc n`. These are the generators of `ProjPres R`.
  - **`Carrier.ext`**: Two carriers with equal underlying ring elements are either equal or that element is zero (degrees match by `degree-unique`).
- **`ProjPres.h*`**: Conjunction on carriers — multiplies underlying elements and adds degrees (preserving positivity via `suc`).

#### Cover Lemmas in `ProjPres`

- **`cover_spec`**: Transfers a cover in `ProjPres R` to a cover in `SpecPres R` of the underlying ring elements.
- **`cover_factor`**: A single-element cover `Cover1 x y` yields some `n, c` with `x.1 ^ n = y.1 * c` (the localization-style factoring).
- **`cover_zro`**: Any family covers a carrier whose underlying element is `0`.
- **`cover-eq`**: Covers transfer along equality of underlying ring elements.
- **`cover-proj1`** / **`cover-proj2`**: If `x.1 = a.1 * b` (resp. `a * b.1`) and one element of `g` equals `a` (resp. `b`), then `g` covers `x`.
- **`toSpec`**: The frame-presentation prehomomorphism `ProjPres R -> SpecPres R` sending a homogeneous generator to its underlying ring element.
- **`cover_BigSum`** / **`cover_FinSum`**: A finite (array or `FinSet`-indexed) sum of homogeneous elements of the same positive degree is covered by its summands.
- **`ideal_cover`**: If `x.1` lies in the radical of the ideal generated by `(g j).1`, then `g` covers `x` in `ProjPres R`.
  - **`ideal_cover.hpow`**: Iterated `h*` self-multiplication of a carrier (homogeneous power preserving the carrier structure).
  - **`ideal_cover.hpow_pow`**: `(hpow x n).1 = pow x.1 (suc n)`.
  - **`ideal_cover.cover_hpow`**: Each `hpow x n` is `Cover1`-covered by `x`.

#### The Proj Locale

- **`Proj`**: The presented frame `PresentedFrame (ProjPres R)` — the underlying locale of `Proj R`.

#### Structure Sheaf and Scheme Structure

- **`projRingedPres`**: The full `Scheme (Proj R)` structure. The structure sheaf is constructed by:
  1. taking the affine structure sheaf on `SpecPres R`,
  2. pushing it forward along `ProjPres.toSpec R` to a sheaf of rings on `Proj R`,
  3. cutting out the subsheaf of sections whose stalks lie in `HomogenLocRing(powers a.1)` (the degree-zero localization).
  - **`projRingedPres.functor`**: The presheaf `a ↦ HomogenLocRing (powers a.1)` on `framePresSite (ProjPres R)`. On a cover `Cover1 b a` with witness `pow b^n = a*c`, it acts via the homomorphism `shom` that sends `y / a^k` (with `y` of degree `ad*k`) to `y * c^k / b^(n*k)`. `Func-id` and `Func-o` use `LocRing.unequals` / `LocRing.equals-lem` to reconcile different witness choices.
  - **`projRingedPres.natTrans`**: The natural inclusion of the homogeneous-localization presheaf into the underlying-ring localization presheaf, by taking the `.1` component.
  - **`projRingedPres.natTrans-isEmb`**: This inclusion is a (componentwise) monomorphism — equality of underlying localizations implies equality in `HomogenLocRing`.
  - **`projRingedPres.subSheaf-lem`**: Sheaf condition for the sub-presheaf — if a section in `LocRing (powers a.1)` restricts to homogeneous degree-zero pieces on each cover element, it is itself in `HomogenLocRing (powers a.1)`.

#### Functoriality of `HomogenLocRing` (helper module `functorial`)

- **`functorial`**: Given homogeneous `a` of degree `ad`, `b` of degree `bd ≠ 0`, and `Cover1 b a` in `SpecPres R`, produces (uniquely up to truncation) a ring hom `HomogenLocRing(powers a) -> HomogenLocRing(powers b)`.
  - **`functorial.sfunc`**: Sends a homogeneous fraction `y / a^k` (with `y` of degree `ad*k`) to `y * c^k / b^(n*k)` using the witness `pow b n = a*c`.
  - **`functorial.smap`**: Computes the action of `sfunc` on an arbitrary element of `HomogenLocRing(powers a)` together with a witness presentation.
  - **`functorial.shom`**: Packages `smap` as a `RingHom`, with `func-+`, `func-ide`, `func-*` provided.
  - **`functorial.shom-coh`**: Two different choices of witness data `(n, c, cd, …)` produce equal ring homomorphisms — used to obtain a hom independent of witness.

#### Auxiliary Sub-Ring / Gluing Lemmas

- **`fromSubRing`** / **`toSubRing`**: Convert between a localization element with a `subRing` proof and a packaged pair, used for navigating the subsheaf construction.
- **`sheaf-lem`**: Local-to-global step: if a fraction `d / a^n` restricts on two charts `g0, g1` (with witnesses `pow gi^ki = a*bi` and `pow a^u = g0*c0 + g1*c1`) to elements of the homogeneous sub-ring on each chart, then it lies in the homogeneous sub-ring on `a` itself.
- **`radical2-lem`**: Radical-membership lemma for the binary ideal `Ideal.closure (if __ b c)`, used in glueing two-element covers.
