### AG.SchemeSite

Scheme sites: a preorder of "opens" with a functor of rings, such that the associated site is a locally ringed site whose locale is a scheme; this is the 1.12 presentation of schemes.

A `SchemeSite` is a preorder `P` with a functor `R : P.op -> CRingBicat` of rings of sections, subject to a stability axiom: restricting a localization `R b` of `R a` along `a' <= a` gives, locally, a localization again (`loc-stable`). From this the module builds a preorder site (`SchemeSite.toSite`, whose covers are generated by the sections of localizations that jointly generate the unit ideal), its locale (`SchemeSite.toLocale`) and the sheaf of rings `SchemeSite.toSheaf`; the sheaf property rests on gluing lemmas for localizations at elements summing to 1. A `SatSchemeSite` is a scheme site in which every section `x : R a` has a localization `R b` among the opens below `a`; its locale `toScheme` is a genuine `Scheme` (`SatSchemeSite.toScheme`) whose basic opens are exactly the affine opens. `SatSite` saturates an arbitrary scheme site by adding all principal opens, `LRL->SchemeSite` and `maxSchemeSite` go the other way, producing a saturated scheme site from the affine opens of a ringed locale (so a scheme is recovered from a scheme site). Morphisms are in `AG.SchemeSite.SchemeSiteHom`, the comparison with locally ringed locales is in `AG.SchemeSite.LRLSchemeSiteHom` and `AG.SchemeSite.SchemeEquiv`.

#### Scheme sites

- **`SchemeSite`**: Record of a preorder `P` and a functor `R : P.op -> CRingBicat` of rings of sections, with the axiom `loc-stable`: for `a' <= a` and `b <= a` such that `R b` is a localization of `R a` at the powers of some `x`, there is `b' <= a'`, `b' <= b` such that `R b'` is the localization of `R a'` at the powers of the restriction of `x`.
- **`SchemeSite.IsLocalizationAt`**: `IsLocalizationAt (ba : b <= a) x` says that `R b` is the localization of `R a` at the powers of `x` via the restriction map.
- **`SchemeSite.loc-restrict`**: For every `b <= a` the ring `R b` is a localization of `R a` at some element: there is `x : R a` with `IsLocalizationAt ba x`.
- **`SchemeSite.loc-stable-forall`**: Strengthens `loc-stable` to every witness: if `R b` localizes `R a` at `x`, then for any `a' <= a` there is `b' <= a'`, `b' <= b` with `R b'` the localization of `R a'` at the restriction of `x`.

#### The site, locale and sheaf of a scheme site

- **`SchemeSite.toSite`**: The preorder site on `P` whose basic covers of `a` are the families such that the elements `x` with `R b` a localization of `R a` at `x` (`b <= c`, `c` in the family, `b <= a`) generate the unit ideal of `R a`.
- **`SchemeSite.basicCover-stable`**: Basic covers restrict along `a' <= a`: if the family covers `a` then it covers `a'`.
- **`SchemeSite.toLocale`**: The locale `SiteLocale toSite` of the site.
- **`SchemeSite.toSheaf`**: The functor `R` is a sheaf of commutative rings on `toSite`, a `VSheaf CRingCat toSite`.
- **`SchemeSite.toRLSite`**: The structure of a ringed site on `toSite` with sheaf of rings `R`.
- **`SchemeSite.Func-Cover1`**: A restriction map `R a -> R b` along a cover `Cover1 b a` (a single-element cover, more general than `b <= a`), obtained from the sheaf property.
- **`SchemeSite.Func-Cover1_<=`**: For an actual inequality `b <= a`, `Func-Cover1` agrees with the restriction `R.Func`.
- **`SchemeSite.Func-Cover1-o`**: `Func-Cover1` is compatible with composition of covers.

#### Covers and ideals

- **`SchemeSite.Cover_Ideal`**: If `a` is covered by `U` in the site, then `a` is covered by `U` by a basic cover: the sections of localizations onto opens below members of `U` generate the unit ideal of `R a`.
- **`SchemeSite.Cover_Ideal_<=`**: If moreover all members of `U` lie below `a`, the unit ideal of `R a` is generated by elements `x` such that `R b` is the localization of `R a` at `x` for some `b` in `U`.
- **`SchemeSite.equiv-cover`**: If the restriction `R a -> R b` along `b <= a` is an equivalence, `b` covers `a`.
- **`SchemeSite.inv-cover`**: If `R c` localizes `R a` at `x` and `x` becomes invertible on `b <= a`, then `b` is covered by `c`.
- **`SchemeSite.inv-cover1`**: The same as `inv-cover` for a single-element cover `Cover1 b a` in place of `b <= a`.
- **`SchemeSite.cover-div`**: If `y` divides `x` in `R a`, `R b` localizes at `x` and `R c` at `y`, then `b` is covered by `c`.
- **`SchemeSite.cover-BigSum`**: If sections `x_i` of `R a` sum to 1 and each lies in a localization `R b_i` with `b_i` covered by `U`, then `a` is covered by `U`.

#### Gluing and algebra of localizations

- **`SchemeSite.pow-lin-comb-pair`**: For `x y` in a commutative ring and `n m`, some power of `x + y` is a combination `t * x^n + s * y^m`.
- **`SchemeSite.pow-lin-comb`**: A power of a finite sum is a linear combination of prescribed powers of the summands.
- **`SchemeSite.pow-lin-comb1`**: If `Σ s_j t_j = 1`, then for any exponents `n_j` there are coefficients `c_j` with `Σ c_j t_j^(n_j) = 1`.
- **`SchemeSite.localization-merge-unique`**: If `Σ s_j t_j = 1` and `x`, `y` have equal images in all localizations at the `t_j`, then `x = y`.
- **`SchemeSite.localization-merge`**: Gluing for localizations: compatible elements `v_j` of the localizations at `t_j` (with `Σ s_j t_j = 1`) come from a single element `r` of the ring.

#### Saturated scheme sites

- **`SatSchemeSite`**: A scheme site in which every section `x : R a` has a localization: there is `b <= a` with `R b` the localization of `R a` at `x` (`loc-exists`).
- **`SatSchemeSite.toLRLSite`**: A saturated scheme site is a locally ringed site (`x` is locally either invertible or has invertible `x + 1`, via the covers by the localizations at `-x` and `x + 1`; and if `1 = 0` in `R a` then `a` is covered by the empty family).
- **`SatSchemeSite.loc-principal`**: For `b <= a` and `x : R a`, `b` is the principal open of `x` in the locally ringed site iff `R b` is the localization of `R a` at `x`.
- **`SatSchemeSite.basis-affineOpen`**: Every element `a` of the site, embedded in the locale, is an affine open.
- **`SatSchemeSite.top-affine`**: If the preorder has a greatest element, the locale is an affine scheme.
- **`SatSchemeSite.toScheme`**: The scheme (a `Scheme`) whose locally ringed locale is the locale of the site, locally affine by `basis-affineOpen`.

#### Saturation

- **`SatSite`**: The saturation of a scheme site `S`: opens are pairs `(a, s)` with `s : S.R a`, ordered by `(a, s) <= (b, t)` when `a <= b` and `s` divides the restriction of `t`, with rings the localizations `R a` at powers of `s`. The result is a `SatSchemeSite`.

#### Scheme sites from ringed locales

- **`LRL->SchemeSite`**: Given a ringed locale `L` and a family `C` of affine opens closed under passing to principal opens, the saturated scheme site whose opens are the members of `C`, ordered by principal-open inclusion, with rings `L.R`.
- **`maxSchemeSite`**: The scheme site of a ringed locale given by all its affine opens, a `SatSchemeSite`.
