### Data.SeqColimit

Sequential colimits of `Nat`-indexed type sequences, with their key universal-property machinery.

A `Seq` is a sequence of types `A : Nat -> \Type` connected by maps `f : A n -> A (suc n)`, and `SeqColimit s` is its higher inductive colimit, generated by inclusion `inSC` and path-constructor `quotSC` identifying each element with its image under `f`. The module's main technical content is a *flattening lemma* expressing the total space of a type family `P` over `SeqColimit s` as itself a sequential colimit (of the sigma-types `(x : A n) × P (inSC x)`); this is proved via an intermediate `TotalSeqColimit` HIT and two `QEquiv`s. Two corollaries follow: surjectivity of `inSC` from level-0 when every transition map is surjective, and contractibility when each level admits a uniform target absorbing all of `A n`.

#### The Sequence Record and Colimit HIT

- **`Seq`**: Record packaging a sequence of types `A : Nat -> \Type` together with transition maps `f : A n -> A (suc n)`.
- **`SeqColimit`**: Higher inductive colimit of a `Seq`, with point constructor `inSC : A n -> SeqColimit s` and path constructor `quotSC x : inSC x = inSC (f x)`.

#### Flattening

- **`SeqColimit.flattening`**: For any family `P : SeqColimit s -> \Type`, exhibits the total space `\Sigma (w : SeqColimit s) (P w)` as itself the sequential colimit `total` of the sigma-sequence `(x : A n) × P (inSC x)` (with transition transporting `P` along `quotSC`). Proved as a composite of two `QEquiv`s through an intermediate HIT.
- **`SeqColimit.flattening.total`**: The candidate sequential colimit on the pointwise sigma-types, with `total.seq` providing the `Seq` whose levels are `(x : A n) × P (inSC x)` and whose `f` transports the `P`-component along `quotSC`.
- **`SeqColimit.flattening.TotalSeqColimit`**: Auxiliary HIT parametrized by `j : I` interpolating between `total` (at `left`) and `\Sigma (w : SeqColimit s) (P w)` (at `right`), with constructors `tinSC` and `tquotSC` mirroring those of `SeqColimit` but extended in the `P`-direction.
- **`SeqColimit.flattening.totalSeqColimitLeft`**: `QEquiv` between `total` and `TotalSeqColimit left`, witnessing that at the left endpoint the auxiliary HIT recovers the iterated colimit.
- **`SeqColimit.flattening.totalSeqColimitRight`**: `QEquiv` between `TotalSeqColimit right` and `\Sigma (w : SeqColimit s) (P w)`, witnessing that at the right endpoint it recovers the total space.

#### Surjectivity

- **`seqColimit-surj`**: If every transition map `f {n}` is a `Surjection`, then `inSC {s} {0}` is also surjective onto `SeqColimit s`.
- **`seqColimit-surj.helper`**: Inductive helper showing each `inSC x` (at level `n`) is merely in the image of `inSC {s} {0}`.

#### Contractibility from Constant Maps

- **`constantMaps`**: If at each level `n` there is a target `(p n).1 : A (suc n)` absorbing every `a : A n` via `f a = (p n).1`, then `SeqColimit s` is `Contr`actible.
- **`constantMaps.points`**: Path `inSC (p n).1 = inSC (p 0).1` built by induction on `n`, threading the absorbing-point identifications back to level 0.
- **`constantMaps.contraction`**: The contraction itself, sending each `inSC x` to `inSC (p 0).1` along `quotSC x`, the absorption `(p n).2 x`, and `points n`; the `quotSC` case is handled with an explicit equational-reasoning proof of the required dependent path.
