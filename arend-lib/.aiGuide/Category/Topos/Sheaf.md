### Category.Topos.Sheaf

Presheaves and sheaves on small categories and sites, with category structures, restriction, direct image, and constructions of sheaves from bases and equalizers.

#### Presheaves

- **`VPresheaf`**: Class for a presheaf valued in a (large) category `D`: a small precategory `C` together with a functor `F : C.op -> D`.
- **`Presheaf`**: A `VPresheaf` specialized to `D = SetCat`.
- **`VPresheafCat`**: The category of `D`-valued presheaves on `C`, as a subcategory of the functor category `FunctorCat C.op D`.
- **`PresheafCat`**: The category of `Set`-valued presheaves on `C`.

#### Sheaves

- **`VSheaf`**: Extends `VPresheaf` with `C : Site`; requires `F` to send each cover sieve `s` on `x` to a limit cone, i.e. `isSheaf : isCover x s -> Limit (Cone.map F s.cone)`.
- **`Sheaf`**: A `Set`-valued sheaf, extending both `Presheaf` and `VSheaf`.
- **`VSheafCat`**: The category of `D`-valued sheaves on a site `C`, as a subcategory of `VPresheafCat`.
- **`SheafCat`**: The category of `Set`-valued sheaves on a site `C`.

#### Restriction and Direct Image

- **`VSheaf.restrict`**: For a locale `L` and `a : L`, restricts a sheaf on `L` to a sheaf on the slice locale `L.restrict a`, by precomposition with the restriction functor.
- **`VSheaf.direct_image`**: Direct image of a sheaf along a morphism `SiteWithBasisPrehom C C'` of sites with basis.
- **`VSheaf.direct_image_framePres`**: Direct image of a sheaf along a `FramePresPrehom`, between sites generated by frame presentations.
- **`VSheaf.direct_image_locale`**: Direct image of a sheaf on `L` along a frame homomorphism `f : FrameHom L' L`, producing a sheaf on `L'` via precomposition with `f.functor.op`.
- **`VSheaf.direct_image_locale.map`**: Functoriality of direct image on natural transformations between sheaves.
- **`VSheaf.direct_image_id`**: Direct image along the identity frame homomorphism is the identity on sheaves.
- **`VSheaf.transport_direct_image`**: Transport of a sheaf along an equality `L = L'` of locales equals the direct image along the induced isomorphism.
- **`VSheaf.transport_direct_image-iso`**: Transport along `Cat.isotoid e` of a locale isomorphism `e` equals direct image along `e.f`.

#### Preservation and Reflection of the Sheaf Property

- **`sheaf-preserve`**: A functor `G : D -> E` that preserves limits sends a `D`-valued sheaf to an `E`-valued sheaf via composition.
- **`sheaf-reflect`**: A functor `G : D -> E` that reflects limits reflects sheaves: if `Comp G F` is an `E`-valued sheaf, then `F` is a `D`-valued sheaf.

#### Sheaves on Sites with a Basis

- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis`**: Builds a sheaf from a presheaf `F` on a site with basis `C`, given that for every basic cover `g` of `x` and every `z : D` the map `matchingFamily x g z` is an equivalence.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis.MatchingFamily`**: Class of compatible families of morphisms `family j : Hom z (F (g j).1)` matching on pullback overlaps.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis.matchingFamily`**: Builds a matching family from a single morphism `h : Hom z (F x)` by composing with the basis morphisms.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis.cone-isMatching`**: A cone over the sieve diagram automatically satisfies the matching condition on pullbacks.
- **`vsheafMatchingFamily`**: Conversely, any sheaf on a site with basis induces an equivalence on matching families for each basic cover.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis-equalizer`**: Builds a sheaf in a complete category by checking that `F x` is the equalizer of `leftMap x g` and `rightMap x g` for each basic cover.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis-equalizer.product1` / `product2`**: The two products `∏ F (g j).1` and `∏ F (pullback (g j.1).2 (g j.2).2)` indexed by the cover and pairs of cover indices.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis-equalizer.leftMap` / `rightMap`**: The two parallel maps `product1 -> product2` induced by the two pullback projections.
- **`vsheafOnSiteWithBasis-equalizer.eqMap`**: The map `F x -> product1` induced by the cover morphisms `(g j).2`.
- **`sheafOnSiteWithBasis`**: Specialization to `Set`-valued sheaves: a presheaf `F : C.op -> Set` is a sheaf iff `matchingFamily` is an equivalence on each basic cover.
- **`sheafOnSiteWithBasis.MatchingFamily`**: Set-valued matching family: elements `family j : F (g j).1` agreeing on pullback restrictions.
- **`sheafOnSiteWithBasis.matchingFamily`**: Restriction of a global element `e : F x` to its family of restrictions `F.Func (g j).2 e`.
- **`sheafOnSiteWithBasis.piEquiv`**: Lifts an equivalence `e : A ≃ B` to an equivalence on function spaces `(Z -> A) ≃ (Z -> B)`.

#### Sheaves on Presented Frames

- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame`**: Extends a sheaf on the site `framePresSite P` of a frame presentation `P` to a sheaf on the presented frame `PresentedFrame P` (in a complete category), via the functor `extend`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.genSieve`**: The sieve on `a` generated by a family `g : J -> P`, consisting of those `b` with `Cover1 b (g j)` for some `j`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.cover1-lem`**: The diagram of `F` over the generated sieve forms a limit when `a` is covered by some single `g j`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.cover-lem`**: Limit property of the generated sieve under a `Cover a g`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.cover'-lem`**: Variant of `cover-lem` for `Cover' a g` together with `b<=a`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.extend`**: The extended functor `Precat.op {PresentedFrame P} -> D`, sending `b` to the limit of `F` over generators below `b` and acting on inclusions by the universal property.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.extend.limFunctor` / `lim` / `cone`**: The diagram of `F` restricted to generators below `b`, its limit, and the cone induced by an inclusion `b <= a`.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.extend-proj`**: The projection `extend (embed y) -> F y` from the extended sheaf at a representable down to `F` on a generator.
- **`sheafOnPresentedFrame.extend-proj-nat''` / `coneMap-nat` / `extend-proj-nat'` / `extend-proj-nat`**: Naturality lemmas relating the cone maps and projections of `extend` along inclusions and covers.
