### Topology.CoverSpace.Product

Binary products of cover spaces and precover spaces, with projections, pairing, and inheritance of regularity, separation, and completeness.

The product cover space on `\Sigma X Y` is built as the join of the two cover-transfer structures along the projections — i.e. the coarsest cover structure making both projections cover-maps. This `Prod X Y` presentation lets cauchy covers on the product be generated by rectangle covers `U × V` from cauchy covers of the factors, and gives a clean characterization of neighborhoods `single (x, y) <=< W` in terms of factor neighborhoods. Universal pairing (`tuple`) and functoriality (`prod`) are derived from the projection-based presentation, and structural properties (strong regularity, separatedness, completeness) lift from factors to the product.

#### Product Instances

- **`CoverSpaceHasProduct`**: Registers `ProductCoverSpace` as the categorical product on `CoverSpace`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace`**: The product cover space on `\Sigma X Y`, with topology from `ProductTopSpace` and cauchy covers from `Prod X Y`.
- **`ProductStronglyRegularCoverSpace`**: Product of strongly regular cover spaces is strongly regular.
- **`StronglyRegularCoverSpaceHasProduct`**: Product instance for strongly regular cover spaces.
- **`ProductSeparatedCoverSpace`**: Product of separated cover spaces is separated.
- **`ProductCompleteCoverSpace`**: Product of complete cover spaces is complete, established via `complete-char` and lifting projections through completion.
- **`PrecoverSpaceHasProduct`**: Registers `ProductPrecoverSpace` as the categorical product on `PrecoverSpace`.
- **`ProductPrecoverSpace`**: The product precover space on `\Sigma X Y`, defined analogously via `Prod` using `PrecoverTransfer`.

#### Internal Presentation (`Prod`)

- **`ProductCoverSpace.Prod`**: Meta-definition `CoverTransfer __.1 ∨ CoverTransfer __.2` — the join of cover structures pulled back along the two projections, used as the underlying cauchy structure of the product.
- **`ProductPrecoverSpace.Prod`**: Analogous meta for precover spaces using `PrecoverTransfer`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.proj1'`**, **`proj2'`**: Cover maps from the `Prod`-presentation to each factor.
- **`ProductPrecoverSpace.proj1`**, **`proj2`**: Precover-map projections from the `Prod`-presentation.

#### Projections and Pairing

- **`ProductCoverSpace.proj1`**, **`proj2`**: The canonical cover-map projections `X ⨯ Y → X` and `X ⨯ Y → Y`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.tuple`**: Pairing: given `f : CoverMap Z X` and `g : CoverMap Z Y`, produces `CoverMap Z (X ⨯ Y)` with `z ↦ (f z, g z)`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.tuple.precover`**: Same pairing at the precover-map level, with `Z` only required to be a `PrecoverSpace`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod`**: Functorial action on cover maps: `f : X → Y`, `g : X' → Y'` give `prod f g : X ⨯ X' → Y ⨯ Y'`, defined as `tuple (f ∘ proj1) (g ∘ proj2)`.

#### Embedding Preservation

- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod.isEmbedding`**: `prod f g` is an embedding when both `f` and `g` are.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod.isDenseEmbedding`**: `prod f g` is a dense embedding when both factors are.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod.isWeaklyDenseEmbedding`**: `prod f g` is a weakly dense embedding when both factors are.

#### Cover and Neighborhood Characterizations

- **`ProductCoverSpace.prodCover'`**: Rectangle cauchy covers in the `Prod`-presentation: from cauchy `C` on `X` and `D` on `Y`, the family `{U × V | U ∈ C, V ∈ D}` is cauchy.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prodCover`**: Same rectangle-cover statement transferred to `X ⨯ Y`.
- **`ProductPrecoverSpace.prodCover`**: Rectangle cauchy-cover construction at the precover level.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod-neighborhood'`**: In the `Prod`-presentation, `single (x, y) <=< W` implies the existence of factor neighborhoods `U ∋ x`, `V ∋ y` with `U × V ⊆ W`.
- **`ProductCoverSpace.prod-neighborhood`**: Bi-implication form of the neighborhood characterization on `X ⨯ Y`.

#### Cauchy Filters

- **`prodCF`**: Product of cauchy filters: given `F : CauchyFilter X` and `G : CauchyFilter Y`, yields a cauchy filter on `X ⨯ Y` whose sets are those containing some rectangle `U × V` with `F U` and `G V`.
