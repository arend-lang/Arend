### Topology.CoverSpace.Complete

Cauchy filters, separation, and completion of cover spaces.

This module develops the theory of completeness for cover spaces by introducing Cauchy filters (proper filters that meet every cover) and their equivalence relation `CF~`. Regular Cauchy filters — those generated by `<=<`-shrinking — serve as the canonical "ideal points" of a space: a cover space is complete when every regular Cauchy filter converges to an actual point. The `Completion` construction realizes the universal complete cover space as the space of regular Cauchy filters, with `dense-lift` extending dense-embedding maps into complete targets, giving the standard universal property of completion.

#### Cauchy Filters

- **`IsCauchyFilter`**: Predicate on a `SetFilter`: every cover `C` contains a member `U` with `F U`.
- **`filter-limit-cauchy`**: A filter that converges to a point is Cauchy.
- **`WeaklyCauchyFilter`**: Record extending `WeaklyProperFilter` with the Cauchy property.
- **`CauchyFilter`**: Record combining `WeaklyCauchyFilter` and `ProperFilter`.
- **`RegularCauchyFilter`**: Cauchy filter where every member `U ∈ F` contains some `V <=< U` also in `F`.
- **`pointCF`**: Regular Cauchy filter at a point `x`, consisting of sets `U` with `single x <=< U`.
- **`regCF`**: Regularization of a Cauchy filter, defined as the intersection over all sub-Cauchy-filters.
- **`regCF_<=`**: `regCF F ⊆ F`.

#### CF~ Equivalence

- **`CF~`**: Equivalence relation on Cauchy filters: every cover contains a member belonging to both.
- **`CF~-sym`**: Symmetry of `CF~`.
- **`CF~_meet`**: Meet of two `CF~`-equivalent Cauchy filters.
- **`CF~_<=`**: Inclusion `F ⊆ G` implies `F CF~ G`.
- **`CF~_<=<`**: If `F CF~ G`, `U <=< V`, and `F U`, then `G V`.
- **`CauchyFilterPoset`**: Poset on `CauchyFilter S` ordered by `⊆`.
- **`CauchyFilterEquivalence`**: `Equivalence` instance for `CF~`.
- **`RegularCauchyFilter.Reg_CF~_<=`**: For regular `F`, `F CF~ G` upgrades to `F ⊆ G`.
- **`RegularCauchyFilter.equality`**: Two regular Cauchy filters that are `CF~` are equal.
- **`RegularCauchyFilter.ratherBelow`**: Lifts a `<=<`-like relation `R` from covers to membership in regular Cauchy filters.

#### Cauchy and Cover Maps

- **`CauchyMap`**: Continuous map between cover spaces that preserves Cauchy filters; `func-cauchy` produces a `CauchyFilter` on the codomain.
- **`CauchyMap.func-CF~`**: Cauchy maps respect the `CF~` equivalence.
- **`CauchyMap.fromContMap`**: From a complete domain, every continuous map is automatically Cauchy.
- **`StronglyCauchyMap`**: Cauchy map that also preserves weakly Cauchy filters.
- **`CoverMap`**: Combines `PrecoverMap` and `StronglyCauchyMap` — the morphisms of cover spaces.
- **`CoverMap.embedding->topEmbedding`**: A `CoverMap` embedding is a topological embedding.
- **`CoverMap.id`**, **`CoverMap.compose` (`∘`)**, **`CoverMap.const`**: Identity, composition, and constant cover maps.
- **`CoverMap.closure-univ`**: Universal property for maps into closure cover spaces.
- **`CoverMap.id-denseEmbedding`**: The identity is a dense embedding.
- **`func-cauchy_<=`**: `CoverMap`-pushforward preserves filter inclusion.

#### Cover Transfer

- **`CoverTransfer-map`**: The canonical map `CoverTransfer f -> Y` is a `CoverMap`.
- **`CoverTransfer-univ`**: Universal property of `CoverTransfer`: factoring `g ∘ f` through it.
- **`CoverTransfer_<=<`**: `<=<` in `CoverTransfer f` is reflected by `<=<` along `f`.

#### Separation and Embeddings

- **`SeparatedCoverSpace`**: Class extending `CoverSpace` and `HausdorffTopSpace`; equal points are detected by Cauchy covers.
- **`SeparatedCoverSpace.separated-char`**: Eight-way TFAE characterizing separation via `pointCF`, `<=<`, opens, and Cauchy covers.
- **`embedding-inj`**: Embeddings into a separated space from a separated source are injective.
- **`Separated-char`**: TFAE for separation: embeddings are injective; `completion` is injective; embeds into some separated space.

#### Completeness

- **`IsCompleteCoverSpace`**: Every regular Cauchy filter is contained in some `pointCF x`.
- **`IsCompleteCoverSpace.cauchyFilterToPoint`**: Same conclusion for arbitrary Cauchy filters.
- **`CompleteCoverSpace`**: Class extending `SeparatedCoverSpace` with `isComplete`; provides the canonical limit point of any Cauchy filter.
- **`CompleteCoverSpace.filter-point`**: The unique point a Cauchy filter converges to.
- **`CompleteCoverSpace.filter-point-sub`**, **`filter-point-elem`**, **`filter-point-char`**, **`filter-point-limit`**: Characterizations of `filter-point` via `<=<`, neighborhoods, and the limit property.

#### Dense Lifting

- **`dense-filter-lift`**: Pulls a Cauchy filter on `Y` back to one on `X` along a dense embedding `f`.
- **`dense-filter-lift.map-equiv`**: The pushforward of the lift is `CF~`-equivalent to the original.
- **`cauchy-lift`**: For a complete `Z`, lifts a `CauchyMap X -> Z` along a dense embedding `f : X -> Y` to `Y -> Z` pointwise.
- **`dense-cauchy-lift`**: Packages `cauchy-lift` as a `CauchyMap`.
- **`dense-lift`**: Packages `cauchy-lift` as a `CoverMap`.
- **`dense-lift-char`**: `dense-lift` agrees with `g` on the dense subspace: `cauchy-lift f fd g (f x) = g x`.
- **`dense-lift-neighborhood`**: Neighborhood characterization of `cauchy-lift`.
- **`dense-lift-natural`**: Naturality of `dense-lift` with respect to filter limits.
- **`dense-complete`**: A space with a dense embedding from a "completing" source is itself complete.

#### Completion

- **`completion`**: The unit `S -> Completion S` sending `x` to `pointCF x`, packaged as a `CoverMap`.
- **`completion.isDenseEmbedding`**: `completion` is a dense embedding.
- **`Completion`**: The complete cover space `RegularCauchyFilter X` with cover structure `coverSpace`.
- **`Completion.mkSet`**: Lifts a set `U ⊆ X` to `{F | F U} ⊆ Completion X`.
- **`Completion.mkSet_<=`**, **`mkSet-open`**, **`mkSet_<=<-point`**: Functoriality and topological properties of `mkSet`.
- **`Completion.pointCF_^-1_<=<`**: A `<=<` neighborhood in `Completion` pulls back to filter membership.
- **`Completion.isCCauchy`**: Cover predicate on `Completion X`: refines a cover obtained via `mkSet` from a cover of `X`.
- **`Completion.makeCover`**: Every cover of `X` lifts to a cover of `Completion X`.
- **`Completion.coverSpace`**: The cover space structure on `RegularCauchyFilter X`.
- **`completion-lift`**: Universal extension of a `CoverMap X -> Z` (with `Z` complete) to `Completion X -> Z`.
- **`completion-lift-char`**: `completion-lift g (pointCF x) = g x`.
- **`completion-lift-unique`**: Two precover maps from `Completion X` to a separated space agreeing on `pointCF`-points are equal.
- **`completion-lift-neighborhood`**: Neighborhood characterization of `completion-lift`.
- **`completion-lift-natural`**: Naturality of `completion-lift` with filter limits.
- **`complete-char`**: TFAE characterizing completeness of `X` via the existence of a retraction `Completion X -> X`.

#### Regular Precover Spaces

- **`regPrecoverCauchyFilter`**: Every proper filter satisfying the Cauchy property in a precover space is a Cauchy filter in its regularization `RegPrecoverSpace X`.
- **`regPrecoverSpace-extend-coverMap`**: A `PrecoverMap X -> Y` to a cover space extends to a `CoverMap` from `RegPrecoverSpace X`.
