### Topology.CoverSpace.StronglyComplete

Strong completion of cover spaces via strongly regular Cauchy filters and the universal property for lifting maps along weakly dense embeddings.

This module develops a "strong" variant of completeness for cover spaces, where convergence is witnessed not by arbitrary Cauchy filters but by *strongly regular* ones — filters closed under the strongly-rather-below relation `s<=<`. A strongly complete cover space is one in which every strongly regular Cauchy filter has a (unique) point whose neighborhood filter `pointSCF x` refines it. The construction `StrongCompletion X` realizes this freely: its underlying type is `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter X`, with cover sets generated by lifting cauchy covers of `X` via `mkSet`. The main payoff is the universal property `weaklyDense-lift`: any cover map from `X` into a strongly complete space `Z` extends uniquely along a weakly dense embedding `f : X -> Y` of strongly regular spaces.

#### Filter Equivalence and Order

- **`WeaklyCauchyFilterPoset`**: `Poset` instance on `WeaklyCauchyFilter S` ordered by subset inclusion `⊆`.
- **`WCF~_meet`**: Given `F CF~ G`, constructs the meet `F ∧ G` as a `WeaklyCauchyFilter`, witnessing that equivalent filters have a common refinement.
- **`WCF~_<=<`**: If `F CF~ G` and `V s<=< U` with `F V`, then `G U`. Transports membership across the equivalence using the strongly-rather-below relation.
- **`WeaklyCauchyFilterEquivalence`**: `Equivalence` instance on `WeaklyCauchyFilter S` for a `StronglyRegularCoverSpace`, using `CF~` as the equivalence relation.

#### Strongly Regular Cauchy Filters

- **`StronglyRegularCauchyFilter`**: Record extending `WeaklyCauchyFilter` with `isStronglyRegularFilter`: every set in the filter contains a strongly-rather-below sub-set also in the filter.
- **`StronglyRegularCauchyFilter.Reg_CF~_<=`**: For a strongly regular `F`, the equivalence `F CF~ G` upgrades to actual inclusion `F ⊆ G`.
- **`StronglyRegularCauchyFilter.equality`**: Two equivalent strongly regular Cauchy filters are equal — i.e., `CF~` is the identity on `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter`.
- **`sregCF`**: Builds a strongly regular Cauchy filter from a weakly Cauchy `F` by taking sets that lie in *every* sub-filter of `F`.
- **`sregCF_<=`**: `sregCF F ⊆ F` — the strongly regular core is a sub-filter.

#### Point Filters and Continuous Maps

- **`pointSCF`**: The neighborhood filter of a point `x : S` consisting of sets `U` with `single x <=< U`. The canonical strongly regular Cauchy filter at a point.
- **`contMap-cauchy`**: Every continuous map out of a strongly complete cover space is automatically a `StronglyCauchyMap`.

#### Strongly Separated and Complete Cover Spaces

- **`StronglySeparatedCoverSpace`**: Class extending `SeparatedCoverSpace`, `StronglyRegularCoverSpace`, and `StronglyHausdorffTopSpace`; derives strong Hausdorffness from separation and strong regularity.
- **`IsStronglyCompleteCoverSpace`**: Predicate on a strongly regular cover space `S`: every `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter` has a point `x` with `pointSCF x ⊆ F`.
- **`IsStronglyCompleteCoverSpace.cauchyFilterToPoint`**: Strong completeness yields a point even for any (weakly) Cauchy filter, by routing through `sregCF`.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace`**: Class extending `StronglySeparatedCoverSpace` and `CompleteCoverSpace` with `isStronglyComplete`. Standard completeness is derived from strong completeness via `sregCF`.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace.weak-filter-point-unique`** / **`weak-filter-point-pair`**: The point of a weakly Cauchy filter is unique, packaged as a propositional `Sigma`.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace.weak-filter-point`**: `\sfunc` extracting the limit point of a weakly Cauchy filter.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace.weak-filter-point-sub`**: `pointSCF (weak-filter-point F) ⊆ F`.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace.weak-filter-point-elem`**: From `V s<=< U` and `F V`, deduces `single (weak-filter-point F) <=< U`.
- **`StronglyCompleteCoverSpace.weak-filter-point-char`**: Characterization: `single (weak-filter-point F) <=< U` iff some `V s<=< U` lies in `F`.

#### Lifting Along Weakly Dense Embeddings

- **`weaklyDense-filter-lift`**: Given a weakly dense embedding `f : X -> Y` and a weakly Cauchy filter on `Y`, lifts it to a weakly Cauchy filter on `X` whose sets are unions of preimages over `<=<`-shrunk filter sets.
- **`weaklyDense-filter-lift.map-equiv`**: The pushforward `f.func-weak-cauchy` of the lifted filter is `CF~`-equivalent to the original filter on `Y`.
- **`weaklyDense-cauchy-lift`**: Lifts a `StronglyCauchyMap g : X -> Z` to a `StronglyCauchyMap Y -> Z`, defined by sending `y` to the unique point of `g`-pushforward of the lifted point filter.
- **`weaklyDense-lift`**: Same construction at the level of `CoverMap` — the universal lift of `g : X -> Z` along a weakly dense embedding `f : X -> Y` into a strongly complete `Z`.
- **`weaklyDense-lift-char`**: Computes the lift on the image: `weaklyDense-lift f fd g (f x) = g x`.
- **`weaklyDense-lift-neighborhood`**: Characterizes when `single (weaklyDense-lift f fd g y) <=< W` in terms of preimages and `s<=<`-shrinkages.
- **`weaklyDense-lift-natural`**: Naturality: lifting commutes with taking points of pushforward filters across `f` and `g`.

#### Auxiliary Results

- **`func-weak-cauchy_<=`**: The pushforward of weakly Cauchy filters under a cover map is monotone in `⊆`.
- **`dense-stronglyComplete`**: Strong completeness transfers along a weakly dense embedding `f : X -> Y` if every strongly regular Cauchy filter on `X` has a point in `Y` for its pushforward.

#### The Strong Completion

- **`strongCompletion`**: The unit `S -> StrongCompletion S` of the completion, sending `x` to `pointSCF x`, packaged as a `CoverMap`.
- **`strongCompletion.dense-aux`**: Technical lemma: if `single F <=< V` in the completion, then `F` contains the preimage of `V` under `strongCompletion`.
- **`strongCompletion.isDenseEmbedding`**: The unit is a weakly dense embedding — the foundational fact making the lifting theorems applicable to `StrongCompletion`.
- **`StrongCompletion`**: `\instance` exhibiting `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter X` as a `StronglyCompleteCoverSpace`. The free strongly complete cover space on `X`.
- **`StrongCompletion.mkSet`**: `mkSet U F = F U` — turns a set in `X` into a set of strongly regular Cauchy filters by membership.
- **`StrongCompletion.mkSet_<=`**: `mkSet` is monotone in `⊆`.
- **`StrongCompletion.isCCauchy`**: Cauchy covers of the completion are those refined by the `mkSet`-image of a Cauchy cover of `X`.
- **`StrongCompletion.makeCover`**: Every Cauchy cover of `X` lifts to a Cauchy cover of `StrongCompletion X` via `mkSet`.
- **`StrongCompletion.coverSpace`**: The underlying `StronglyRegularCoverSpace` structure on `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter X`.
- **`StrongCompletion.pointSCF_^-1_<=<`**: If `single F <=< U` in the completion, then the preimage `pointSCF ^-1 U` lies in `F`.
- **`StrongCompletion.mkSet_<=<-point`**: `single F <=< mkSet U` iff `F U` — characterizes the topology of the completion at points.
