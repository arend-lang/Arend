### Topology.CoverSpace.Category

Category instances for precover spaces and cover spaces, with morphisms given by (pre)cover-preserving maps.

#### Category Instances

- **`PrecoverSpaceCat`**: The category `Cat PrecoverSpace` of precover spaces, with `Hom = PrecoverMap`, identity `PrecoverMap.id`, and composition `PrecoverMap.∘`.
- **`CoverSpaceCat`**: The category `Cat CoverSpace` of cover spaces, with `Hom = CoverMap`, identity `CoverMap.id`, and composition `CoverMap.∘`.
