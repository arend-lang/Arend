### Topology.CoverSpace.Locale

Establishes the adjunction between precover spaces and locales: every precover space presents a locale of "cauchy-covered" sets, and every locale gives rise to a precover space on its complete filters (points), with regularity matching on both sides.

#### From Cover Spaces to Locales

- **`CoverSpaceLocale`**: For a `PrecoverSpace X`, the locale `PresentedFrame framePres` whose basic covers come from cauchy covers of `X`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.framePres`**: The frame presentation on `Set X`: conjunction is intersection `∧`, and basic covers `U ◁ f` are generated by (i) `U = top` together with a cauchy family refined by `f`, (ii) the family `f` covering every `V s<=< U`, or (iii) `U = bottom`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-cauchy`**: Any cauchy cover `C` yields a frame-presentation cover of `top` by `C`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-reg`**: Regularity at the presentation level: `U` is covered by all `V s<=< U`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-empty`**: An empty subset (no points satisfy `U`) is covered by any family.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.embed_<=<`**: Strong neighborhood `V s<=< U` lifts to the locale's `<=<` between embedded basic opens.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.regular`**: `CoverSpaceLocale X` is always a regular locale.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-char`**: For a strongly regular `X`, any frame-presentation cover of `U` translates back into a cauchy cover refining `Compl U'` together with the family `g`, for `U' s<=< U`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-point-char`**: Pointwise refinement: if `single x <=< U` and `U` is covered by `g`, some `g j` already contains a neighborhood of `x`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.hasWeaklyDensePoints`**: Weakly dense points of `X` give dense points to `CoverSpaceLocale X`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-inh`** / **`CoverSpaceLocale.cover-reg-inh`**: When `X` has dense points, `U` is presentation-covered by its inhabitants (resp. by points `x` with `single x <=< U`).
- **`CoverSpaceLocale.hasStronglyDensePoints`**: Dense points of `X` yield strongly dense points of `CoverSpaceLocale X`.

#### From Locales to Cover Spaces

- **`LocalePrecoverSpace`**: For a locale `L`, the `PrecoverSpace` whose underlying set is `CompleteFilter L` and where `C` is cauchy iff `top <= ⋁ { points_* s | s ∈ C }`.
- **`LocalePrecoverSpace.points_<=<`**: The functor `points^*` sends `<=<` in `L` to `s<=<` in the induced precover space.
- **`LocalePrecoverSpace.cover-cauchy`**: A frame-cover `top <= SJoin C` of `L` gives a cauchy cover by the family `points^* a` for `a ∈ C`.
- **`LocalePrecoverSpace.single_<=<-char`**: Characterization of strong neighborhoods at a point: `single x <=< U` iff some `a` with `x a` has `points^* a ⊆ U`.
- **`LocalePrecoverSpace.hasWeaklyDensePoints`** / **`LocalePrecoverSpace.hasDensePoints`**: Density on the locale side transfers to (weak) density of points in the precover space.

#### Regular Locales as Cover Spaces

- **`LocaleCoverSpace`**: A regular locale `L` (with proof `Lr`) gives a `StronglyRegularCoverSpace` extending `LocalePrecoverSpace L`.
- **`LocaleCoverSpaceFunctor`**: The functor `LocaleCat -> PrecoverSpaceCat` taking `L` to `LocalePrecoverSpace L` and a frame homomorphism `f` to the precover map `PointsSpaceFunctor.filter-map f`.
- **`LocaleCoverSpaceFunctor-fullyFaithful`**: Under dense points on the source `L` and regularity of the target `M`, the action of the functor on hom-sets is an equivalence; injectivity is shown via a density-based pointwise comparison and surjectivity by reconstructing a frame homomorphism `frameHom` from a precover map.
- **`LocaleCoverSpaceFunctor-fullyFaithful.frameHom`**: The inverse construction: from a precover map `f : LocalePrecoverSpace L -> LocalePrecoverSpace M`, builds a `FrameHom M L` by `a ↦ ⋁_{b <=< a} points_* (f ^-1 points^* b)`.

#### Unit of the Adjunction

- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit`**: For a strongly regular `X` with weakly dense points, the canonical `PrecoverMap X -> LocaleCoverSpace (CoverSpaceLocale.regular)` sending each `x` to the framePres point `\lam U => single x <=< U`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isDense`**: The unit is weakly dense.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isEmbedding`**: The unit is an embedding of precover spaces.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isDenseEmbedding`**: Combined: the unit is a weakly dense embedding, exhibiting `LocaleCoverSpace (CoverSpaceLocale X)` as a completion-style envelope of `X`.

#### Completeness

- **`LocaleCompleteCoverSpace`**: For a regular locale `L`, `LocaleCoverSpace Lr` is in fact a `StronglyCompleteCoverSpace` (separated and strongly complete).
- **`LocaleCompleteCoverSpace.makeFilter`**: Witness of strong completeness: from a `WeaklyCauchyFilter` on `LocaleCoverSpace Lr`, builds the limiting `CompleteFilter L` whose elements are `a` such that some `b L.<=< a` has `points^* b` in the filter.
