### Topology.CoverSpace.Locale

Constructs the adjunction between cover spaces and locales, presenting a cover space's locale of opens via a frame presentation and recovering a cover space from the points of a locale.

This module formalizes the pointful/pointfree correspondence in constructive topology: every precover space `X` gives rise to a locale `CoverSpaceLocale X` whose frame is presented by subsets of `X` modulo cauchy-cover and strongly-below relations, while every locale `L` yields a precover space `LocalePrecoverSpace L` on its complete filters (points). The presentation uses three kinds of basic covers — top covered by a cauchy family, any open covered by sets strongly below it, and bottom covered by anything — which together force the resulting frame to be regular. The constructions form a functor `LocaleCat → PrecoverSpaceCat` with a unit `X → LocaleCoverSpace (CoverSpaceLocale X)` that is a weakly dense embedding when `X` has weakly dense points, providing the pieces of an adjunction between regular locales and strongly regular cover spaces.

#### Cover Space to Locale

- **`CoverSpaceLocale`**: The locale associated to a precover space `X`, defined as the `PresentedFrame` of `framePres`.
- **`framePres`**: The frame presentation on `Set X`: conjunction is set intersection, and basic covers are generated by cauchy families on top, strongly-below families on arbitrary opens, and arbitrary covers of bottom.
- **`cover-cauchy`**: Any cauchy cover yields a frame-presentation cover of top.
- **`cover-reg`**: Any open `U` is covered by all `V` with `V s<=< U` (regularity at the presentation level).
- **`cover-empty`**: An empty subset is covered by any family.
- **`embed_<=<`**: The embedding `Set X → CoverSpaceLocale X` preserves the strongly-below relation.
- **`regular`**: `CoverSpaceLocale X` is a regular locale.
- **`cover-char`**: From a presentation cover `Cover U g` and `U' s<=< U`, extracts a cauchy cover by `Compl U'` together with the `g j`.
- **`cover-point-char`**: If `single x <=< U` and `U` is covered by `g`, then some `g j` already contains a neighborhood of `x`.
- **`hasWeaklyDensePoints`**: Weakly dense points of `X` give weakly dense points of `CoverSpaceLocale X`.
- **`cover-inh`**, **`cover-reg-inh`**: Under dense points, `U` is covered by itself indexed by inhabitants (resp. by points strongly below `U`).
- **`hasStronglyDensePoints`**: Dense points of `X` give strongly dense points of `CoverSpaceLocale X`.

#### Locale to Cover Space

- **`LocalePrecoverSpace`**: The precover space on `CompleteFilter L` whose cauchy covers are families `C` such that `top ≤ ⋁ points_* s` over `s ∈ C`.
- **`points_<=<`**: The map `points^*` sends locale `<=<` to cover-space `s<=<`.
- **`cover-cauchy`**: A locale-theoretic cover `top ≤ SJoin C` translates to a cauchy cover by the sets `points^* a` for `a ∈ C`.
- **`single_<=<-char`**: Characterizes `single x <=< U` by the existence of `a` with `x a` and `points^* a ⊆ U`.
- **`hasWeaklyDensePoints`**: Locale-level dense points yield weakly dense points of the associated precover space.
- **`hasDensePoints`**: Strongly dense locale points yield dense points of the associated precover space.
- **`LocaleCoverSpace`**: From a regular locale `L`, builds a `StronglyRegularCoverSpace` on top of `LocalePrecoverSpace L`.

#### Functoriality and Fully Faithfulness

- **`LocaleCoverSpaceFunctor`**: The functor `LocaleCat → PrecoverSpaceCat` sending `L` to `LocalePrecoverSpace L` and a frame homomorphism to its filter-pullback as a `PrecoverMap`.
- **`LocaleCoverSpaceFunctor-fullyFaithful`**: When the source has dense points and the target is a regular locale, the action of the functor on hom-sets is an equivalence; injectivity uses a meet/regularity argument and surjectivity reconstructs a frame homomorphism via `frameHom`.
- **`frameHom`**: Given a `PrecoverMap` between locale-induced cover spaces, builds the corresponding `FrameHom M L` by `a ↦ ⋁_{b <=< a} points_* (f ^-1 points^* b)`.

#### Unit of the Adjunction

- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit`**: For a strongly regular cover space `X` with weakly dense points, the canonical `PrecoverMap` from `X` into `LocaleCoverSpace (CoverSpaceLocale X)`, sending `x` to the framePres-point determined by `U ↦ single x <=< U`.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isDense`**: The unit is weakly dense.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isEmbedding`**: The unit is an embedding of cover spaces.
- **`CoverSpaceLocale-unit.isDenseEmbedding`**: Combines the above into a weakly dense embedding.

#### Completeness

- **`LocaleCompleteCoverSpace`**: Strengthens `LocaleCoverSpace` on a regular locale to a `StronglyCompleteCoverSpace`, witnessing that locales give complete and separated cover spaces.
- **`LocaleCompleteCoverSpace.makeFilter`**: From a weakly cauchy filter on `LocaleCoverSpace Lr`, constructs a `CompleteFilter L` by `a ↦ ∃ b, b <=< a ∧ F (points^* b)`, which serves as the limit point used to establish strong completeness.
