### Topology.CoverSpace.Subspace

Cover space structures on subsets: open subspaces, closed subspaces, and lifting of Cauchy filters.

This module constructs cover spaces on subsets of a given cover space `X`. For an open subset `S`, the cover space structure is built via a closure construction over "basic covers" — covers that are either restrictions of Cauchy covers of `X` or arise from the regular closure of the singleton `{S}`. The `RegularClosure` operator iteratively refines a family of covers by replacing each member with sets that are `<=<`-below it, capturing the topology induced by the open inclusion. For closed subsets, the simpler `CoverTransfer` along the inclusion suffices, and completeness transfers in both cases via filter extension along the inclusion.

#### Open Subspace

- **`OpenCoverSpace`**: Cover space structure on `Total S` for an open subset `S ⊆ X`, defined as the closure cover space generated by `isBasicCover`.
- **`isBasicCover`**: A cover of `Total S` is basic if it is the restriction `λ U. ∃ V ∈ D. U = restrict V` of a cover `D` of `X` that is either Cauchy or in the regular closure of `{{S}}`.
- **`basicCover-cover`**: Every basic cover covers every point of `Total S` (covering axiom).
- **`basicCover-regular`**: Basic covers satisfy the regularity axiom in the closure precover space.
- **`makeBasicCover`**: Builds a closure-cover from a cover `D` of `X` that is Cauchy or in the regular closure of `{{S}}`.

#### Regular Closure

- **`RegularClosure`**: Inductive predicate on covers of `X`, closed under starting from `A` and replacing a cover `D` with `{V | ∃ U ∈ D. V <=< U}`. Used to generate the "neighborhood-refined" covers around the open set `S`.
- **`regular-closure`**: Base constructor: any `C` with `A C` is in the regular closure.
- **`regular-closure-extends`**: Inductive constructor: if `D` is in the regular closure, so is `{V | ∃ U ∈ D. V <=< U}`.
- **`closure-neighborhood`**: If every cover in `A` has a member that is a `<=<`-neighborhood of `x`, then so does every cover in the regular closure of `A`.

#### Inclusion Map

- **`func`**: The first projection `Total S -> X` as a `PrecoverMap` from `OpenCoverSpace X So` to `X`.
- **`<=<-conv`**: Converts a `<=<`-neighborhood relation in the open subspace to one in `X` via the `extend` operation.

#### Filter Extension

- **`filter-extend`**: Pushes a `SetFilter (Total U)` forward to a `SetFilter X` by `V ↦ F (λ s. V s.1)`.
- **`proper-filter-extend`**: Properness lifts: a `ProperFilter` on `Total U` extends to a `ProperFilter` on `X`.
- **`cauchy-filter-extend`**: Cauchy property lifts: a `CauchyFilter` on `OpenCoverSpace X So` extends to a `CauchyFilter` on `X`.

#### Complete Subspaces

- **`OpenCompleteCoverSpace`**: An open subset of a `CompleteCoverSpace` is itself a `CompleteCoverSpace`. Separation is established via `Separated-char` using the inclusion map; completeness uses `cauchy-filter-extend` to transfer Cauchy filters back to `X`.
- **`ClosedCompleteCoverSpace`**: A closed subset of a `CompleteCoverSpace` is itself a `CompleteCoverSpace`, with cover structure given by `CoverTransfer` along the inclusion.
- **`ClosedCompleteCoverSpace.conv`**: Converse: if `CoverTransfer` along the inclusion of `S ⊆ X` (with `X` separated) is complete, then `S` is closed in `X`. Characterizes closedness via completeness of the subspace.
