### Topology.MetricSpace.Compact

Compactness properties for metric spaces, specifically that the Manhattan product preserves total boundedness of balls.

#### Total Boundedness of Products

- **`product-balls-tb`**: Given pseudo-metric spaces `X` and `Y` whose balls are totally bounded (`BallsTotallyBounded X` and `BallsTotallyBounded Y`), the Manhattan product pseudo-metric space `ManhattanProductPseudoMetricSpace X Y` also has totally bounded balls. Establishes that ball total boundedness is preserved under Manhattan products of metric spaces.
