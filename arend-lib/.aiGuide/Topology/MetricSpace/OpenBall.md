### Topology.MetricSpace.OpenBall

Open balls with a lower-real radius in an extended pseudometric space, and their cover-space structure.

For an extended pseudometric space `X`, a center `c` and a radius `r : LowerReal` (possibly infinite), `LBall r c` is the set of points whose distance from `c` is strictly below `r`. The module shows that it is open and contains the rational balls `OBall r' c` for `r'` below `r` in a rather-below sense. It then makes the subtype of the ball into a `CoverSpace` (`OpenBallCoverSpace`) generated by restrictions of Cauchy covers of `X` and the cover by smaller balls, and shows that for a complete metric space this cover space is complete (`OpenBallCompleteCoverSpace`).

#### Open balls

- **`LBall`**: The open ball `{x | dist c x <LU r}` of radius `r : LowerReal` around `c`, as a set of points of `X`.
- **`LBall-open`**: `LBall r c` is open.
- **`LBall_<=*`**: For a rational `r'` with `r.L r'`, the rational ball `OBall r' c` is uniformly below `LBall r c` (`<=*`: its star with respect to some uniform cover lies in `LBall r c`).
- **`LBall_<=<`**: For a rational `r'` with `r.L r'`, the rational ball `OBall r' c` is rather below `LBall r c` (relation `<=<`).

#### Cover space of an open ball

- **`OpenBallCoverSpace`**: The `CoverSpace` on the points of `LBall r c` whose covers are generated by restrictions of Cauchy covers of `X` and by the cover of the ball by the rational balls `OBall r' c` with `r.L r'`; its topology is the subspace topology. Its `\where` block contains the generating covers `OpenBallCoverSpace.isBasicCover`, `OpenBallCoverSpace.basicCover-cover`, `OpenBallCoverSpace.basicCover-regular`, `OpenBallCoverSpace.makeBasicCover1`, `OpenBallCoverSpace.makeBasicCover2`, the rather-below transfer lemmas `OpenBallCoverSpace.<=<-dir` and `OpenBallCoverSpace.<=<-conv`, and the inclusion `OpenBallCoverSpace.func` as a `CoverMap` into `X`.
- **`OpenBallCoverSpace.infinity-inv`** / **`OpenBallCoverSpace.infinity-inv-cover`**: For a distance-bounded `X` and the infinite radius `r = LowerReal.infinity`, the identification of `X` with the ball, and the proof that it is a `CoverMap` from `X` to the ball.

#### Completeness

- **`OpenBallCompleteCoverSpace`**: For a `CompleteExMetricSpace` `X`, the cover space of the open ball is a `CompleteCoverSpace` (separated and complete). Its `\where` block has `OpenBallCompleteCoverSpace.cauchy-filter-extend`, extending a Cauchy filter on the ball to one on `X`.
