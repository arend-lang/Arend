### Topology.Locale.HausdorffLocale

Hausdorff and weakly Hausdorff conditions on locales, stated through the diagonal into the product locale.

- **`IsHausdorffLocale`**: Hausdorff condition phrased as a closure property of the diagonal.
- **`IsRegular_IsHausdorff`**: Every regular locale (`IsRegularLocale`) is Hausdorff.
- **`IsWeaklyHausdorff`**: Weak Hausdorff condition: the diagonal is weakly closed.
- **`wregular_wHausdorff`**: Weakly regular locales are weakly Hausdorff.
