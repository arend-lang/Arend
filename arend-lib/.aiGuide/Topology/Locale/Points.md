### Topology.Locale.Points

The points–opens adjunction between locales and topological spaces, with conditions on when points are "dense enough" to recover the locale.

This module formalizes the classical adjunction `Loc ⇄ Top` between locales and topological spaces. The space of points of a locale `L` is the set of completely prime filters on `L`, with the topology generated by sets of the form `{x | x a}` for `a : L`; conversely, the open sets of a space form a frame. The frame homomorphism `points : L → Opens(Pts L)` along with its set-theoretic adjoint `points_*` exhibits this adjunction. Density conditions `HasDensePoints` and `HasStronglyDensePoints` characterize when a locale has enough points to be recoverable from its space, and constructive criteria are provided for locales given by frame presentations.

#### The Opens Frame

- **`OpensFrame`**: Builds a `Locale` whose carrier is the set of opens of a topological space `S`, with order being subset inclusion, joins as unions of opens (using `open-IUnion`), and meets as binary intersections (using `open-inter`).

#### The Points Space

- **`PointsSpace`**: The topological space of points of a locale `L`, defined as `CompleteFilter L`, where opens are subsets of the form `{x | x a}` for some `a : L`.
- **`PointsSpaceFunctor`**: The functor `LocaleCat → TopCat` sending `L ↦ PointsSpace L` and a frame homomorphism `f : M → L` to the continuous map between point spaces given by precomposition.

#### The Adjunction Maps

- **`points-map`**: The map on points induced by a locale homomorphism `f : LocaleHom L M`: a completely prime filter `x` of `L` is sent to the filter `{b | x (f b)}` of `M`.
- **`points`**: The unit-style frame homomorphism `L → OpensFrame (PointsSpace L)` sending `a` to the open `{x | x a}`. This is the locale side of the adjunction.
- **`points^*`**: The underlying set map `a ↦ {x | x a} : L → Set(CompleteFilter L)`, the carrier of `points` without the frame-hom structure.
- **`points^*-mono`**: Monotonicity of `points^*` with respect to `≤` and `⊆`.
- **`points^*_top>=`**, **`points^*_top`**: `points^* top = top` (i.e., the full set of points).
- **`points^*_meet>=`**, **`points^*_meet`**: `points^*` preserves binary meets.
- **`points_*`**: The right adjoint at the set level: `points_* U = ⋁ {a | points^* a ⊆ U}`, the largest locale element whose set of points lies in `U`.
- **`points_*-mono`**: Monotonicity of `points_*` in the subset order.
- **`points_*_meet>=`**, **`points_*_meet`**: `points_*` preserves binary meets.
- **`points^*-points_*`**: Adjunction property: if `points^* a ⊆ U`, then `a ≤ points_* U`.
- **`points-unit`**: Unit of the adjunction: `a ≤ points_* (points^* a)`.
- **`points-counit`**: Counit of the adjunction: `points^* (points_* U) ⊆ U`.

#### Points from Frame Presentations

- **`SitePoint`**: A point of a `PreorderSite P`: a predicate `F : P -> \Prop` that is upward closed, inhabited, closed under meets (two elements of `F` have a common refinement `z` in `F` covered by each), and such that whenever `x` has a basic cover by `U` and `F x`, some element of `U` satisfies `F`.
- **`site-point`**: Turns a `SitePoint P` into a completely prime filter of `SiteLocale P`: an open `U` belongs to it iff some element of `U` satisfies `p`.

#### Dense Points

- **`HasDensePoints`**: The property that `points_* bottom ≤ bottom`, i.e., the only locale element with no points is `bottom`. Equivalently, points are jointly "dense enough" to detect non-triviality.
- **`hasDensePoints-site`**: For a `PreorderSite P`, if every `a : P` with no `SitePoint` satisfying it is covered by the empty family, then `SiteLocale P` has dense points.
- **`hasDensePoints-char`**: Equivalent reformulation: `HasDensePoints L` iff every `a : L` with no point satisfying it must be `≤ bottom`.

#### Strongly Dense Points

- **`HasStronglyDensePoints`**: The stronger property that every `a : L` satisfies `a ≤ pHat (∃ x. x a)`, i.e., `a` is below the "propositional truncation hat" of the existence of a point witnessing it.
- **`densePoints_cover`**: Consequence of strong density: `a ≤ L.pHat (∃ (x : CompleteFilter L) (x a))`, the same bound stated as a lemma for a given `d : HasStronglyDensePoints L`.
- **`hasStronglyDensePoints-site`**: For a `PreorderSite P`, if every `a : P` is covered by the family of those `a` that are satisfied by some `SitePoint P`, then `SiteLocale P` has strongly dense points.
