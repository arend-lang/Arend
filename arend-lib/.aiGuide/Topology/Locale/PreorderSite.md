### Topology.Locale.PreorderSite

Preorder sites (preorders with basic covers, called flat sites by Vickers), the locale `SiteLocale P` of cover-closed subsets they present, the converse construction `LocaleSite` of a site from a locale, site morphisms, and the transfer of positivity, overtness, way-below, local compactness, algebraicity and regularity between a site and its locale.

- **`PreorderSite`**: Class extending `Preorder` and `Site`, given by a basic cover relation `isBasicCover x U` (for `x : E` and `U : Set E`), stable in the sense of `basic-cover-stable`: if `a <= b` and `U` is a basic cover of `b`, then `a` has a basic cover `V` refining `U` all of whose elements lie below `a`. The general `isCover` and `cover-stable` of `Site` are derived from it.
- **`PreorderSite.IsSubcanonical`**: Property of a site: whenever `U` is a basic cover of `x` and every member of `U` is below `y`, then `x <= y`.
- **`PreorderSite.subcanonical-cover`**: In a subcanonical site, if `x` is covered (in the inductive sense of `Cover`) by `U` and every member of `U` is below `y`, then `x <= y`.
- **`PreorderSite.subcanonical-cover1`**: In a subcanonical site, `Cover x (single y)` implies `x <= y`.
- **`PreorderSite.IsPositive`**: An element `a` is positive if every proposition `P` such that `a` is covered by the constant set `P` holds.
- **`PreorderSite.positive-site`**: `a` is positive in the site iff its generator `embed a` is positive in `SiteLocale`.
- **`PreorderSite.positive-locale`**: If an open `U` of `SiteLocale` contains a positive element `a`, then `U` is positive in the locale.
- **`PreorderSite.IsOvert`**: The site is overt if every element is covered by the set of positive elements.
- **`PreorderSite.overt-single`**: Overtness is equivalent to every `a` being covered by the set of those `x` equal to `a` for which `a` is positive.
- **`PreorderSite.overt-locale`**: The site is overt iff `SiteLocale` is an overt locale.
- **`PreorderSite.<<`**: Way-below relation on a `PreorderSite`: `x << y` iff for every cover `U` of `y` there is a finite list of elements of `U` that covers `x`.
- **`PreorderSite.<<-site`**: `x << y` in the site iff `embed x` is way below `embed y` in `SiteLocale`.
- **`PreorderSite.IsLocallyCompact`**: Every element `x` is covered by the elements way below it.
- **`PreorderSite.locallyCompact-locale`**: The site is locally compact iff `SiteLocale` is a locally compact locale.
- **`PreorderSite.IsAlgebraic`**: Every basic cover of `a` contains a finite list of elements that still covers `a`.
- **`PreorderSite.algebraic-char`**: A site is algebraic iff `a << a` for every element `a`.
- **`Cover`**: Inductively defined cover relation between an element and a set of elements of a `PreorderSite`, generated by `cover-inj` (below a member) and `cover-trans` (through a basic cover).
- **`Cover1`**: Cover of `x` by the single element `y`, i.e. `Cover x (single y)`.
- **`SiteLocale`**: The locale presented by a `PreorderSite P`: its frame of opens is `SiteLocale.Opens P`, ordered by inclusion, with top the whole set, meet the intersection and joins given by `SiteLocale.closure` of the union.
- **`SiteLocale.Opens`**: Carrier of `SiteLocale P`: subsets of `P` closed under covers.
- **`SiteLocale.closure`**: The open (cover-closed subset) generated by a subset of `P`.
- **`SiteLocale.embed`**: Inclusion `P -> Opens P` of generators.
- **`SiteLocale.func-equality`**, **`SiteLocale.func-equality_ext`**: Frame homomorphisms into `SiteLocale P` are determined by their values on generators.
- **`LocaleSite`**: The site of a `Locale L`: the elements of `L` with their order, where `U` is a basic cover of `x` iff `x <= Join U`.
- **`LocaleSite.locale_cover`**: Translates a `Cover` in `LocaleSite L` into an inequality `x <= Join U` in `L`.
- **`LocaleSite.adjointMap`**: Frame homomorphism `SiteLocale X -> Y` induced by a site morphism `X -> LocaleSite Y`.
- **`LocaleSite.adjointMap_embed`**: `adjointMap f` extends `f` on generators.
- **`PreorderSiteHom`**: Record of morphisms between preorder sites: order-preserving maps (`PreorderHom`) that preserve basic covers (`func-basicCover`) and satisfy the flatness conditions `func-flat-top` and `func-flat-meet`, making them site morphisms (`SiteHom`).
- **`PreorderSiteHom.func-Cover`**: A morphism of preorder sites preserves the inductive `Cover` relation: `Cover x U` gives `Cover (func x) (SetIm func U)`.
- **`PreorderSitePredenseHom`**: A `PreorderSiteHom` that is also a dense prehomomorphism: every `u` in the codomain is covered by the images lying below it (`func-dense-image`), and `func a <= func b` implies `Cover1 a b` (`func-dense-map`).
- **`sdense-site`**: Criterion for strong density of `LocaleSite.adjointMap f`, for a site morphism `f` into `LocaleSite L`: it suffices that `f x <= L.pHat Q` implies `Cover x (\lam _ => Q)` for every proposition `Q`.
- **`sdense-comp`**: Strong density of frame homomorphisms is closed under composition.
- **`regular-site`**: If every element `x` of a site is covered by the elements rather-below it (`Locale.<=<`) in `SiteLocale`, then `SiteLocale P` is a regular locale.
- **`<<-char`**: Criterion for way-below in a site: a relation `R` contained in `<=` such that whenever `R a b` and `U` is a basic cover of `b`, `a` is covered by a finite list of elements each `R`-related to a member of `U`, implies `R x y -> x << y`.
