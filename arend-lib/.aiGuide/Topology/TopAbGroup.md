### Topology.TopAbGroup

Topological abelian groups: abelian groups equipped with a compatible topology in which addition and negation are continuous.

A `TopAbGroup` simultaneously extends `TopSpace`, `AbGroup`, and `UniformSpace`, with the uniform structure derived from the topology via translation-invariant neighborhoods of `0`. The key idea is that uniformity is generated by sets of the form `UBall U x = {y | U (x - y)}` for opens `U` containing `0`; this links the algebraic and topological structure so that continuity at a point reduces to control of differences. The `<=<ta` relation captures the topological-group analog of `<=<` (a basic-near relation defined by `∀ x y ∈ V, U (x - y)`), and is used to prove standard shrinking and refinement lemmas needed for completeness, Hausdorff separation, and uniform maps.

#### Main Class

- **`TopAbGroup`**: Topological abelian group, extending `TopSpace`, `AbGroup`, and `UniformSpace`.
  - **`+-cont`**: Addition is continuous as a map from the product space.
  - **`negative-cont`**: Negation is continuous.
  - **`neighborhood-uniform`**: Characterizes uniform covers via open neighborhoods of `0`: `C` is uniform iff there is an open `U ∋ 0` such that for every `x` some `V ∈ C` contains all `y` with `U (x - y)`.
  - Provides the uniform space axioms (`uniform-cover`, `uniform-top`, `uniform-refine`, `uniform-inter`, `uniform-star`) and the `cauchy-open` compatibility of the cover space topology with the underlying topology.

#### Uniformity from Neighborhoods

- **`topAbProperUniform`**: The induced uniform structure is proper.
- **`topAbUniform`**: Given an open `U ∋ 0`, the family `{UBall U x | x ∈ E}` is uniform.
- **`shrink`**: For any open `U ∋ 0` there exists an open `V ∋ 0` with `V <=<ta U`.
- **`shrink'`** (in `\where`): Generalized shrinking lemma for any topological additive group with continuous addition and negation.

#### UBall Constructions

- **`UBall`**: `UBall U x = \lam x' => U (x - x')`, the translate of `U` centered at `x`.
- **`UBall-open`**: `UBall U x` is open whenever `U` is.
- **`UBall-center`**: `x ∈ UBall U x` whenever `0 ∈ U`.
- **`UBall-point`**: `0 ∈ UBall U x` whenever `x ∈ U`.

#### Derived Continuity

- **`subtract-cont`**: Subtraction `(s.1, s.2) ↦ s.1 - s.2` is continuous on the product space.

#### The `<=<ta` Relation

- **`<=<ta`**: `V <=<ta U` means `∀ x y ∈ V, U (x - y)` — the topological-group analog of the basic-near relation.
- **`<=<ta-left`**: From `V (x - z)` and `V (y - z)` deduce `U (x - y)`.
- **`<=<ta-right`**: From `V (x - y)` and `V (x - z)` deduce `U (y - z)`.
- **`<=<ta_<=`**: If `0 ∈ V` and `V <=<ta U` then `V ⊆ U`.
- **`<=<ta_negative`**: `<=<ta` is preserved by preimage along negation.
- **`<=<ta_<=*-shifted`**: Two-step shrinking gives `UBall W x <=* UBall U x`.
- **`<=<ta_<=*`**: Two-step shrinking gives `W <=* U` directly.

#### Continuity Characterizations

- **`topAb-contAt`**: Continuity of `f : X -> Y` at `x` iff for every open `V ∋ 0` in `Y` there is an open `U ∋ 0` in `X` with `U (x - x') -> V (f x - f x')`.
- **`topAb-sub-contAt`**: Same characterization for maps from a subspace `Set.Total S`.

#### Hausdorff Variant

- **`HausdorffTopAbGroup`**: Extends `TopAbGroup`, `HausdorffTopSpace`, and `SeparatedCoverSpace`.
  - **`equals`**: If `U (x - y)` holds for every open `U ∋ 0`, then `x = y`.

#### Morphisms

- **`TopAbGroupMap`**: Continuous group homomorphism between topological abelian groups; extends `ContMap`, `AddGroupHom`, and `UniformMap`. Automatically uniform via translation invariance.
  - **`func-uniform`**: Uniform-cover preservation derived from continuity at `0` plus homomorphism property.
  - **`embedding->uniformEmbedding`**: A dense topological embedding between topological abelian groups is automatically a dense uniform embedding.
