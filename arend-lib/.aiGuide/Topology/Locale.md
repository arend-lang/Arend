### Topology.Locale

Locales (point-free topologies) presented as complete distributive lattices satisfying the infinite distributive law, together with frame homomorphisms, nuclei, frame presentations, and the categories of locales and frame presentations.

#### Core Classes

- **`Locale`**: A locale, extending `CompleteLattice`, `BoundedDistributiveLattice`, and `SiteWithBasis`. Joins are constructed from binary `if` decisions; the infinite distributive law `Join-ldistr>=` (`e ∧ Join f <= Join (e ∧ f j)`) is the key axiom. Meets over arbitrary sets are derived from joins of lower bounds; basic covers are `x <= Join g`.
- **`FrameHom`**: A homomorphism of locales, extending `SetHom`. Preserves top, finite meets, and arbitrary joins; default implementations derive monotonicity and equalities from the corresponding `>=` inequalities.
- **`FrameHom.direct_o`**: The right adjoint (direct image) of a composition equals the composition of right adjoints.

#### Nuclei

- **`Nucleus`**: A nucleus on a locale `L`: an inflationary, idempotent, meet-preserving operator `L -> L`. Encodes a sublocale of `L`.
- **`Nucleus.exp`**: Exponential nucleus `j ⇒ j'`, defined as a meet over sufficient indices.
- **`Nucleus.exp-univ1`**, **`Nucleus.exp-univ2`**: Universal property of `exp` characterizing it as right adjoint to `meet` of nuclei.
- **`NucleusFrame`**: The locale of nuclei on `L`, ordered pointwise. Top is the constant-top nucleus; joins are constructed via the meet over upper bounds.
- **`NucleusFrame.<=-map`**: For nuclei `j <= j'`, the corresponding frame homomorphism between their associated locales.
- **`NucleusFrame.<=-map.surjective`**: That map is surjective.
- **`NucleusFrame.double-nucleus-left`** / **`double-nucleus-right`**: For `j <= k`, `k (j x) = k x` and `j (k x) = k x`.
- **`NucleusFrame.wclosure_<=`**, **`wclosure-sdense`**, **`wclosure-inclusion`**: Properties relating the weakly-closed image of a nucleus map to the nucleus itself.
- **`NucleusFrame.nucleus>=open`** (and `.conv`): Characterizes when a nucleus dominates `open a` by `top <= j a`.
- **`NucleusFrame.open_<=`** (and `.conv`): Antitone correspondence between elements `a` and the open nucleus `open a`.
- **`NucleusFrame.intersection-pullback`**: The locale of `j ∨ k` is the pullback in `LocaleCat` of the inclusions of `j` and `k`.

#### Open/Closed Sublocales

- **`closed>=open`** (and `.conv`): `closed c >= open a` iff `top <= c ∨ a`.
- **`open<=closed`** (and `.conv`): `open a >= closed c` iff `c ∧ a <= bottom`.
- **`closed-compact`**: A closed sublocale with `a << top` and `N >= open a` is compact.

#### Frame Category

- **`FrameCat`**: The category of locales with frame homomorphisms (covariant direction).
- **`FrameCat.equiv_iso`**: A frame homomorphism whose underlying function is an equivalence is an iso in `FrameCat`.

#### Frame Presentations

- **`FramePres`**: A frame presentation: a set with binary `conj` and an arbitrary basic-cover relation `BasicCover x g`.
- **`FramePres.Indexing`**: Reindexing predicate for an `I`-indexed family of basic-cover shapes.
- **`FramePres.indexing-make`**, **`indexing-transport`**: Helpers to build/transport indexed properties.
- **`Cover`**: Inductive cover relation generated by basic covers, reflexivity (`cover-inj`), transitivity, and the conjunction laws (projection, idempotence, commutativity, left-distributivity).
- **`Cover.cover-trans1`**, **`cover-proj2`**, **`cover-index`**, **`cover-ldistr'`**, **`cover-rdistr`**, **`cover-rdistr'`**, **`cover-conj`**, **`cover-conj1`**, **`cover-prod`**, **`map`**, **`cover_top`**, **`cover_<=`**, **`cover-inj_<=`**: Derived cover lemmas — including transport along `Cover1`, indexing, distributivity, conjunction-pairing, and lifting along homs.
- **`Cover1`**: Single-target cover `Cover x (\lam _ => y)`; the preorder underlying a presentation.
- **`Cover'`**: Alternative inductive presentation of cover, with explicit conjunction-projection and product constructors. Equivalent to `Cover` (`cover-cover'`, `cover'-cover`).
- **`framePresPreorder`**: Preorder on a presentation given by `Cover1`.
- **`framePresSite`**: Site structure on a presentation: pullbacks via `conj`; basic covers stable under reindexing along `Cover1`.

#### Frame Presentation Morphisms

- **`FramePresPrehom`**: A function preserving `conj` and basic covers, but not necessarily surjective onto the target.
- **`FramePresHom`**: A `FramePresPrehom` whose image covers the target (`Cover y func`).
- **`FramePresCat`**: The category of frame presentations.

#### Presented Locale

- **`PresentedFrame`**: The locale freely generated by a presentation `P`. Opens are downward-closed-under-cover predicates on `P`; meet uses `closure` of pairs.
- **`PresentedFrame.Opens`**: The carrier type of opens.
- **`PresentedFrame.closure`**: Closure of an indexed family into an open.
- **`PresentedFrame.<=`**: Pointwise inclusion of opens.
- **`PresentedFrame.closure<=`**, **`embed<=`**: Universal property of closure/embedding.
- **`PresentedFrame.embed`**: The canonical inclusion `P -> PresentedFrame P`.
- **`PresentedFrame.element_join`**: An open is the join of embeddings of its members.
- **`PresentedFrame.surj-map`**: Lifts a surjection-by-generators into an explicit preimage.
- **`PresentedFrame.Cover_embed`** / **`embed_Cover`**: `Cover1 a b` iff `embed a <= embed b`.
- **`PresentedFrame.embed_meet`**: `embed a ∧ embed b = embed (conj a b)`.
- **`PresentedFrame.func-equality`**, **`func-equality_ext`**: Two frame homs out of a presented frame agree iff they agree on generators.

#### Unital Frame Presentations

- **`FrameUPres`**: Frame presentation with a distinguished unit covering everything.
- **`FrameUPresHom`**: A `FramePresHom` preserving the unit; `func-image` is then automatic.
- **`FrameUPresCat`**: Category of unital frame presentations.
- **`FrameUPresCocompleteCat`**: Cocompleteness of `FrameUPresCat`. Colimits are constructed from a syntactic term type:
  - **`FrameUPresCocompleteCat.FTerm`**: HIT representing terms over the colimit's generators, with conjunction, unit, functorial-action, and equational constructors.
  - **`FrameUPresCocompleteCat.colimit-obj`**, **`colimitMap`**, **`colimit-cone`**, **`colimit-func`**, **`colimit-univ`**, **`colimit-univ-eq`**: The colimit object, the inclusions, and its universal property (induction on `FTerm`).
  - **`FrameUPresCocompleteCat.fpair`**: Pair of generators in the colimit of a binary product diagram.
  - **`FrameUPresCocompleteCat.term-product`**: Every term in a binary-product colimit is `Cover1`-equivalent to a pair `fpair a b`.

#### Subcategories and Reflections

- **`FrameUnitalSubcat`**: Fully faithful inclusion `FrameCat -> FrameUPresCat` (every locale viewed as a unital presentation).
- **`FrameUnitalSubcat.Func-inverse`**: Inverse-image formula.
- **`FrameReflectiveSubcat`**: Reflective inclusion `FrameCat -> FramePresCat` with reflector `PresentedFrame`.
- **`FrameReflectiveSubcat.locale_cover`**: A `Cover` in a locale-as-presentation entails the join inequality.
- **`FrameReflectiveSubcat.adjointMap`**, **`adjointMap_embed`**: The adjoint frame hom `PresentedFrame X -> Y` and its action on generators.
- **`FrameUnitalReflectiveSubcat`**: Reflective inclusion of `FrameCat` into `FrameUPresCat`.

#### Limits and Colimits of Locales

- **`FrameBicat`**: `FrameCat` is bicomplete.
  - **`FrameBicat.limit-obj`**: Limit in `FrameCat` as a locale of compatible tuples.
  - **`FrameBicat.pullback-obj`**, **`proj1`**, **`proj2`**: Pullbacks of locales as triples `(x,y,fx=gy)`.
  - **`FrameBicat.coneMap_finj`**: Identifies a cone map of a `FrameCat`-colimit with `embed ∘ finj`.
- **`LocaleCat`**: Opposite category of `FrameBicat`; the geometric category of locales. Bicomplete; terminal object is `discrete (\Sigma)`.
- **`LocaleCat.terminal-direct`**: The right adjoint of the unique map `L -> 1` is `\lam _ => top <= x`.

#### Discrete Locales

- **`discrete`**: Discrete locale on a set `X` (powerset locale).
- **`discrete.functor`**: Functor `SetCat -> LocaleCat`.
- **`discrete.exponent`**: Exponential `P --> Q` in a discrete locale is pointwise implication.

#### Open and Surjective Maps

- **`pullback_open`**: Open maps are stable under pullback in `LocaleCat`.
- **`pullback_sdense`**: Strongly dense maps pull back along open maps.
- **`overt=open`**: A locale is overt iff its terminal map is open.
- **`open-comp`**: Open maps compose.
- **`regular_surj`** (with helper **`regular_surj-lemma`**): Regular monos in `LocaleCat` are surjective on opens.
- **`surj_regular`**: Surjective frame homs are regular monos in `LocaleCat`.
- **`surj_equiv`** and **`surj_equiv.map-comm`**: A surjection induces a frame iso between the image and codomain, compatibly with the image factorization.

#### Hausdorff and Regular Locales

- **`isHausdorffLocale`**: Hausdorffness via the diagonal: a generalized separation condition over `(L, L×L, π1, π2)`.
- **`isHausdorffLocale.generalized`**, **`extend`**: Auxiliary predicate and "extension" used in the definition.
- **`isHausdorffLocale.diagonal_func`**, **`diagonal_direct`**, **`diagonal-isClosed`**: Characterizations of the diagonal map and the fact that the diagonal of a Hausdorff locale is closed.
- **`regular_Hausdorff`** (with **`generalized`** helper): Every regular locale is Hausdorff.
- **`isWeaklyHausdorff`**: Weakly Hausdorff = the diagonal is weakly closed.
- **`wregular_wHausdorff`**: Weakly regular implies weakly Hausdorff.

#### Orthogonal Factorization Systems

- **`dense_closed_ofs`**: OFS on `LocaleCat` with dense maps on the left and closed-image surjections on the right.
- **`sdense_wclosed_ofs`**: OFS with strongly dense on the left and weakly closed on the right.

#### Overtness, Positivity, Density, and Way-Below

- **`overt-fromPres`**: Overtness lifts from a presentation to its presented frame.
- **`sdense_positive`**, **`func_positive`**: Positivity is preserved by strongly dense maps and reflected along any frame hom.
- **`sdense_overt`**: Strongly dense map into an overt codomain forces the domain to be overt.
- **`sdense-fromPres`**: Strong density of `adjointMap f` from a generator-level criterion.
- **`sdense-comp`**: Strongly dense maps compose.
- **`<<-fromPres`**: Way-below at the generator level lifts to the presented frame.
- **`<<_<=`**, **`<<-left`**, **`<<-right`**: Standard properties of way-below.
- **`locallyCompact-fromPres`**: Local compactness lifts from a presentation.
- **`<=<_<=`**: `<=<` implies `<=`.
- **`LocaleRatherBelow`**: Instance making `<=<` a `RatherBelow` relation on any locale.
- **`regular-fromPres`**: Sufficient generator-level condition for the presented frame to be regular.

#### Indexed Cover Reasoning

- **`wayBelowPredicate`**: Closure principle for a relation `R` behaving like way-below: from `R x y` and `Cover y g` together with hypotheses on basic covers, monotonicity, density, and a commutativity condition, one extracts a finite subarray of indices that already covers `x`.
- **`wayBelowPredicate.comm-lem`**: Inductive helper isolating the first `k` elements of an array cover under `R`.
- **`wayBelowPredicate.++_singleton`**, **`split_++`**, **`split`**, **`unsplit`**, **`unsplit2`**: Combinatorial helpers for flattening/indexing concatenated arrays of arrays used in the proof above.
- **`wayBelowPredicate.indexing-basic`**: Lifts a per-index basic-cover finiteness statement through `Indexing`.
