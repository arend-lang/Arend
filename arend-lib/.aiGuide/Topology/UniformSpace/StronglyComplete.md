### Topology.UniformSpace.StronglyComplete

Strongly complete uniform spaces and the strong completion of a strongly regular uniform space.

This module combines `StronglyRegularUniformSpace`, `CompleteUniformSpace`, and `StronglyCompleteCoverSpace` into a single notion of a strongly complete uniform space, and constructs the strong completion functor for strongly regular uniform spaces. The strong completion equips `StrongCompletion X` with a uniformity generated by lifting uniform covers `C` of `X` to covers of the form `mkSet U` (open sets in the completion induced by `U : C`). A weakly dense uniform embedding into a strongly complete target admits a unique uniform extension, providing the universal property that characterizes the strong completion.

#### Main Class

- **`StronglyCompleteUniformSpace`**: Class extending `StronglyRegularUniformSpace`, `CompleteUniformSpace`, and `StronglyCompleteCoverSpace`. Captures uniform spaces that are strongly regular and strongly complete with respect to their cover structure.

#### Universal Property

- **`weaklyDense-uniform-lift`**: Given a weakly dense uniform embedding `f : X -> Y` (with `Y` strongly regular) and a uniform map `g : X -> Z` into a strongly complete uniform space, produces the uniform extension `Y -> Z`. This is the universal property of weakly dense embeddings into strongly complete targets.

#### Strong Completion

- **`UniformStrongCompletion`**: Instance turning a strongly regular uniform space `X` into a `StronglyCompleteUniformSpace`. Its underlying cover space is `StrongCompletion X`, and a cover `D` is uniform iff it is refined by `{mkSet U | U ∈ C}` for some uniform cover `C` of `X`.
- **`UniformStrongCompletion.properUniform`**: If `X` is proper uniform, so is its strong completion.
- **`UniformStrongCompletion.makeUniform`**: Lifts a uniform cover `C` of `X` to the uniform cover `{mkSet U | U ∈ C}` of `UniformStrongCompletion X`.
- **`uniform-strongCompletion`**: The canonical uniform map `X -> UniformStrongCompletion X` (lifting `strongCompletion` from cover spaces).
- **`uniform-strongCompletion.isDenseEmbedding`**: The canonical map is a weakly dense uniform embedding, enabling use of `weaklyDense-uniform-lift`.

#### Filter Characterizations

- **`cauchyFilter-uniform-char`**: A set filter on a preuniform space meets every Cauchy cover iff it meets every uniform cover.
- **`regularFilter-uniform-char`**: For a Cauchy filter on a regular preuniform space, having a `<=<`-refinement inside the filter for each member is equivalent to having a `<=*`-refinement, linking the regular and strongly regular notions of refinement.
