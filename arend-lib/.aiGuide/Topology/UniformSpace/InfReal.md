### Topology.UniformSpace.InfReal

The strongly complete uniform space structure on the extended/infinite reals `InfReal`.

This module equips `InfReal` (real numbers with `+∞`) with a strongly regular, separated, strongly complete uniform space structure. The uniformity is generated by two kinds of basic covers: standard `eps`-balls around finite real points (using `RealAbGroup.abs`) together with half-infinite tails of the form `B < ·` that capture neighborhoods of `+∞`. The Cauchy completion is identified with `InfReal` itself by extracting lower/upper Dedekind cuts from a Cauchy filter, with the half-line cover handling the case where the filter converges to infinity.

#### Main Instance

- **`InfRealUniformSpace`** (instance): The `StronglyCompleteUniformSpace` structure on `InfReal`, combining the underlying uniform space, separation, and strong completeness.

#### Uniform Space Structure

- **`InfRealUniformSpace`** (in `\where`): The underlying `StronglyRegularUniformSpace` on `InfReal`. A cover `C` is uniform iff there is some `eps > 0` and bound `B` such that the half-line `(B <)` is in `C`, and every real `x` has a `C`-element containing all reals within `eps` of `x`.
- **`makeUniform`**: Constructs the canonical uniform cover at scale `eps` and bound `B`, consisting of the half-line `(B <)` together with `eps`-balls around every real point.

#### Cauchy Filters and Neighborhoods

- **`NFilter`**: The neighborhood `SetFilter` at a point `x : InfReal` — sets that either contain an `eps`-ball around the finite representative of `x`, or contain a half-line `(B <)` for some `B < x` (when `x` is near `+∞`).
- **`cauchy-ball`**: For any uniform cauchy cover `C` and point `x : InfReal`, produces a member `U : C` that is an `eps`-ball around a finite representative of `x`, or a half-line below `x`.
- **`<=<-ball`**: Characterizes the strong inclusion `single x <=< U`: any uniform neighborhood of `x` contains either an `eps`-ball or a half-line below `x`, depending on whether `x` is finite or infinite.

#### Completion Map

- **`fromCF`**: Reconstructs an `InfReal` from a `StronglyRegularCauchyFilter` on `InfRealUniformSpace` by defining its lower cut (`a` such that some filter set lies above some `a1 > a`) and upper cut (`b` such that some filter set lies below some `b1 < b`). This witnesses strong completeness.

#### Open Sets

- **`infReal-ball-open`**: The open `eps`-ball `{y | ∃ y' : Real, y' = y, |y' − x| < eps}` around a real point `x` is open in the `InfReal` uniform topology.
- **`infReal-half-open`**: The half-line `(B <) ⊆ InfReal` is open for every real bound `B`, giving the standard neighborhood basis at `+∞`.
