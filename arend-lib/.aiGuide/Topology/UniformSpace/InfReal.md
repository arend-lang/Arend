### Topology.UniformSpace.InfReal

Equips the extended non-negative reals `InfReal` with a strongly complete uniform space structure, where neighborhoods are generated by Real-centered ε-balls together with upper half-rays `(B, ∞]`.

#### Uniform Space Instance

- **`InfRealUniformSpace`** (instance): The `StronglyCompleteUniformSpace` structure on `InfReal`, built from the strongly regular uniform space below together with separation and strong completeness.
- **`InfRealUniformSpace.InfRealUniformSpace`**: The underlying `StronglyRegularUniformSpace InfReal`, whose uniform covers are generated by `makeUniform`.
- **`InfRealUniformSpace.makeUniform`**: Witnesses that for any bound `B : Real` and `eps > 0`, the family consisting of the half-ray `(B, ∞]` together with the Real-centered open ε-balls forms a uniform cover of `InfReal`.

#### Neighborhood Filter

- **`InfRealUniformSpace.NFilter`**: The neighborhood `SetFilter` of a point `x : InfReal`. A set `U` is a neighborhood iff either `x` is finite (some `x1 : Real` equals `x`) and `U` contains a Real ε-ball around `x1`, or `x = ∞` and `U` contains some upper half-ray `(B, ∞]` with `B < x`.

#### Cauchy / Cover Lemmas

- **`InfRealUniformSpace.cauchy-ball`**: For any uniform Cauchy cover `C` and point `x : InfReal`, there exists `U ∈ C` that is itself a Real-centered ε-ball around `x` (when `x` is finite) or an upper half-ray below `x` (when `x = ∞`).
- **`InfRealUniformSpace.<=<-ball`**: If `single x <=< U` in the uniform space, then `U` contains a ball/half-ray neighborhood of `x` of the form described above. Used to extract concrete neighborhoods from the strong refinement relation.

#### Completion

- **`InfRealUniformSpace.fromCF`**: Builds an `InfReal` from a strongly regular Cauchy filter `F` on `InfRealUniformSpace`, defining its lower cut `L a` as "some member of `F` lies strictly above some `a1 > a`" and its upper cut `U b` dually. This realizes strong completeness by exhibiting limits of Cauchy filters.

#### Open Sets

- **`infReal-ball-open`**: For `eps x : Real`, the set of `y : InfReal` such that `y` is a real number within distance `eps` of `x` is open in `InfRealUniformSpace`.
- **`infReal-half-open`**: For `B : Real`, the upper half-ray `{y : InfReal | B < y}` is open in `InfRealUniformSpace`.
