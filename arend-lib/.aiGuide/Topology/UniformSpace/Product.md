### Topology.UniformSpace.Product

Binary products in the categories of regular preuniform spaces and uniform spaces.

The product uniform structure on `\Sigma X Y` is generated by rectangular covers `Prod U V` where `U` ranges over a uniform cover of `X` and `V` over a uniform cover of `Y`. The underlying cover space agrees with the topological product `ProductCoverSpace X Y`, ensuring the product construction is compatible across the topology hierarchy. The module also packages the standard categorical apparatus — projections, tupling, and functoriality — exhibiting `⨯` as a categorical product in the category of uniform maps.

#### Product Instances

- **`RegularPreuniformSpaceHasProduct`**: `HasProduct` instance for `RegularPreuniformSpace`, dispatching to `ProductRegularPreuniformSpace`.
- **`ProductRegularPreuniformSpace`**: The product `\Sigma X Y` of two regular preuniform spaces. A cover `E` is uniform iff it is refined by some rectangular cover `{Prod U V | U ∈ C, V ∈ D}` built from uniform covers `C` of `X` and `D` of `Y`. Extends `ProductCoverSpace X Y`.
- **`UniformSpaceHasProduct`**: `HasProduct` instance for `UniformSpace`, dispatching to `ProductUniformSpace`.
- **`ProductUniformSpace`**: The product `\Sigma X Y` of two uniform spaces. Extends `ProductRegularPreuniformSpace X Y` and additionally satisfies the star-refinement axiom `uniform-star`.

#### Properness and Categorical Maps

- **`ProductUniformSpace.properUniform`**: If `X` and `Y` are properly uniform (`IsProperUniform`), then so is `X ⨯ Y`.
- **`ProductUniformSpace.proj1`**: First projection `UniformMap (X ⨯ Y) X` sending `s ↦ s.1`.
- **`ProductUniformSpace.proj2`**: Second projection `UniformMap (X ⨯ Y) Y` sending `s ↦ s.2`.
- **`ProductUniformSpace.tuple`**: Universal tupling: given uniform maps `f : Z → X` and `g : Z → Y`, produces the uniform map `Z → X ⨯ Y` with components `(f z, g z)`.
- **`ProductUniformSpace.prod`**: Functoriality of `⨯`: lifts uniform maps `f : X → Y` and `f' : X' → Y'` to a uniform map `X ⨯ X' → Y ⨯ Y'`, defined as `tuple (f ∘ proj1) (f' ∘ proj2)`.

#### Cover Construction

- **`ProductUniformSpace.prodCover`**: Given uniform covers `C` of `X` and `D` of `Y`, the family of rectangular sets `{(s.1, s.2) | U s.1 ∧ V s.2}` for `U ∈ C, V ∈ D` is a uniform cover of `X ⨯ Y`. The basic tool for transferring uniformity across the product.
