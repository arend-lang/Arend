### Algebra.Ring.Integral

Integral elements, integral extensions, and integral closures of commutative rings.

This module formalizes the theory of integral ring extensions: an element `a` is integral over `R` (via `f : R → E`) iff it satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in `R`. The key structural tool is `polyImage f a` — the subring of `E` generated by the image of `f` together with `a`, identified with the image of the polynomial evaluation map. Integrality is characterized in three equivalent ways (monic-polynomial witness, finite-generation as a module, membership in a finitely-generated submodule), and the equivalence is used to prove that integral elements form a subring (the integral closure) and that integrality is preserved under composition. The module also covers integrally closed extensions, behavior under surjections, lifting through factor rings of `R[x]/(p)`, and stability under directed colimits.

#### Polynomial Image Subrings

- **`polyImage`**: The subring of `E` consisting of values `polyEval (polyMap f p) a` for `p : Poly R` — i.e., `R[a] ⊆ E`.
- **`polyImage-closed`**: `polyImage f a` is closed under multiplication by `a`.
- **`polyImage-ext`**: Every `f b` lies in `polyImage f a` (the base ring embeds).
- **`polyImage-element`**: `a` itself lies in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImage-pow`**: Every power `a^n` lies in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImageHom`**: The corestriction `R → polyImage f a` of `f`.
- **`polyImageHomPoly`**: The surjection `R[x] → polyImage f a` sending `p ↦ polyEval (polyMap f p) a`.
- **`mPolyImage`**: Multivariate analogue: subring `R[l₀,…,l_{n-1}] ⊆ E` for an array `l : Array E`.
- **`mPolyImage-ext`**, **`mPolyImage-element`**: The base ring and each `l j` lie in `mPolyImage f l`.
- **`mPolyImageHom`**: Corestriction `R → mPolyImage f l`.
- **`mPolyImage-finGen`**: Exhibits `l` as algebra generators of `mPolyImage f l` over `R`.

#### Integrality Predicate

- **`isIntegral`**: `∃ p, isMonic p ∧ polyEval (polyMap f p) x = 0` — `x` satisfies a monic polynomial over `f.Dom`.
- **`isIntegral.fromInv`**: Integrality from a polynomial whose top coefficient is invertible (rescale to monic).
- **`isIntegral.fromPoly`**: Over a discrete field, any nonzero polynomial annihilator yields integrality.
- **`isIntegralExt`**: Every element of `f.Cod` is integral over `f.Dom`.
- **`isIntegrallyClosed`**: Every integral element of the codomain comes from the domain (i.e., `f` is surjective onto its integral closure).

#### Module-Theoretic Characterization

- **`monic-generated`**: A monic annihilating polynomial of degree `n` makes `1, a, …, a^{n−1}` generate `polyImage f a` as an `R`-module.
- **`module-integral`**: If `a` lies in a subring `S ⊇ f(R)` that is finitely generated as an `R`-module, then `a` is integral over `R`.
- **`module-integral.module-monic`**: Constructs the explicit monic annihilator from a finite generating set (Cayley–Hamilton style determinant trick).
- **`finite-integralExt`**: A finitely-generated module structure on `f` makes `f` an integral extension.
- **`integral-generated`**: If `a` is integral, then `polyImage f a` is finitely generated as an `R`-module.
- **`integralExt-generated`**: An algebra generated by finitely many integral elements is finitely generated as a module.
- **`integralExt-finite-char`**: TFAE — `f` integral / each generator integral / `f.Cod` finitely generated as `f.Dom`-module (for finitely-generated algebras).

#### Closure Properties

- **`poly_integral`**: `polyEval (polyMap f q) a` is integral whenever `a` is.
- **`poly_integral.aux`**: Refined version producing an annihilator of a controlled degree.
- **`polyImage_integral`**: Every element of `polyImage f a` is integral over `R` when `a` is.
- **`polyImage_integralExt`**: `polyImageHom f a` is an integral extension.
- **`integral_image-comp`**: Integrality is preserved by post-composition with a ring homomorphism.
- **`integralExt-surj`**: A surjective ring homomorphism is an integral extension.
- **`integralExt_id`**: The identity is an integral extension.
- **`integral_image`**: Each `f a` is integral over `f.Dom` (trivially).
- **`integralExt_polyDiv`**: Over an integral extension, every monic polynomial in `E[x]` divides a monic polynomial coming from `R[x]`.
- **`integralExt-left`**: If `g ∘ f` is integral and `g` is injective, then `f` is integral.
- **`integral-right`**, **`integralExt-right`**: Integrality of `g ∘ f` (point/extension-wise) implies integrality of `g`.
- **`integral-comp`**: An integral element over `S` becomes integral over `R` whenever `R → S` is integral (transitivity at a single element).
- **`integralExt-comp`**: Composition of integral extensions is integral (transitivity).

#### Integral Closure

- **`integralClosure-subring`**: The subring of `E` consisting of all elements integral over `R`.
- **`integralClosure-subring.integral-combined`**: Tower lemma — `R[a]` and `R[a][b]` are both integral, used to prove the closure is closed under `+` and `*`.
- **`integralClosure`**: The integral closure as a `CRing`.
- **`integralClosure-left`**: The map `R → integralClosure f`.
- **`integralClosure-right`**: The inclusion `integralClosure f ↪ E`.
- **`integralClosure-integral`**: `integralClosure-left` is an integral extension.
- **`integralClosure-closed`**: `integralClosure-right` is integrally closed (the closure is its own closure).

#### Factor Rings and Colimits

- **`integral_factor`**: Lifts the polynomial evaluation `R[x]/(p) → polyImage f a` through the factor-ring construction when `p` annihilates `a`.
- **`poly-integralExt`**: Quotients `R[x]/I` by ideals containing a monic `p` give integral extensions of `R`.
- **`integralExt-directColimit`**: A directed colimit of integral extensions in `RingCat` is integral — each colimit-injection from `j` is an integral extension.
