### Algebra.Ring.Integral

Integral elements, integral ring extensions, and integral closures: an element `a` is integral over `f : R → E` if it is a root of a monic polynomial with coefficients in `R`, with the equivalence between integrality and module-finiteness, and the construction of the integral closure as a subring.

#### Polynomial Image Subrings

- **`polyImage`**: The subring of `E` generated by the image of `f : R → E` together with an element `a : E`, defined as the image of the evaluation `R[X] → E` sending `X ↦ a`.
- **`polyImage-closed`**: Multiplication by `a` preserves membership in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImage-ext`**: Every element of the form `f b` lies in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImage-element`**: The element `a` itself lies in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImage-pow`**: Every power `a^n` lies in `polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImageHom`**: The corestriction of `f` to a ring homomorphism `R → polyImage f a`.
- **`polyImageHomPoly`**: The induced homomorphism `R[X] → polyImage f a` sending a polynomial to its evaluation at `a`.
- **`mPolyImage`**: Multivariate analogue: the subring generated by `f(R)` together with a finite array `l` of elements of `E`.
- **`mPolyImage-ext`**, **`mPolyImage-element`**, **`mPolyImageHom`**: Multivariate analogues of the above.
- **`mPolyImage-finGen`**: `mPolyImage f l` is algebra-generated over `R` by the elements of `l`.

#### Integrality Predicate

- **`isIntegral`**: `isIntegral f x` holds iff `x` is a root of some monic polynomial with coefficients in the domain of `f`.
- **`isIntegral.fromInv`**: Integrality from a polynomial whose leading coefficient (at degree `n`) is invertible.
- **`isIntegral.fromPoly`**: Over a discrete field, any nonzero polynomial annihilating `a` witnesses integrality.
- **`isIntegralExt`**: A ring extension `f` is integral if every element of its codomain is integral over `f`.

#### Module-Theoretic Characterizations

- **`monic-generated`**: If `a` satisfies a monic polynomial of degree `n`, then `polyImage f a` is generated as an `R`-module by `1, a, ..., a^(n-1)`.
- **`module-integral`**: If `a` lies in a subring `S ⊇ f(R)` that is finitely generated as an `R`-module, then `a` is integral over `f`.
- **`module-integral.module-monic`**: Constructs an explicit monic annihilating polynomial of degree `≤ |l|` from a generating array `l`.
- **`finite-integralExt`**: If the codomain of `f` is finitely generated as an `R`-module, then `f` is an integral extension.
- **`integral-generated`**: If `a` is integral over `f`, then `polyImage f a` is finitely generated as an `R`-module.

#### Closure Properties

- **`poly_integral`**: The image `polyEval (polyMap f q) a` of any polynomial `q` at an integral element `a` is again integral.
- **`poly_integral.aux`**: Constructs a monic annihilating polynomial of the same degree for `polyEval (polyMap f q) a`.
- **`polyImage_integral`**: Every element of `polyImage f a` is integral over `f` when `a` is.
- **`polyImage_integralExt`**: `polyImageHom f a` is an integral extension whenever `a` is integral.

#### Functoriality and Composition

- **`integral_image-comp`**: Integrality is preserved under postcomposition: if `a` is integral over `f`, then `g a` is integral over `g ∘ f`.
- **`integralExt-surj`**: Every surjective ring homomorphism is an integral extension.
- **`integralExt_id`**: The identity is an integral extension.
- **`integral_image`**: Every element of the form `f a` is integral over `f`.
- **`integralExt_polyDiv`**: Over an integral extension, every monic polynomial in `E[X]` divides a monic polynomial that is the image of a monic polynomial in `R[X]`.
- **`integralExt-left`**: If `g ∘ f` is integral and `g` is injective, then `f` is integral.
- **`integral-right`**, **`integralExt-right`**: If `g ∘ f` is integral, then so is `g` (elementwise / extension).
- **`integral-comp`**: If `f` is an integral extension and `a` is integral over `g`, then `a` is integral over `g ∘ f`.
- **`integralExt-comp`**: Composition of integral extensions is integral.

#### Finitely Generated Algebras

- **`integralExt-generated`**: If `f` is algebra-generated by elements that are each integral, then the codomain is a finitely generated `R`-module.
- **`integralExt-finite-char`**: TFAE for a finitely generated algebra `f`: (1) `f` is an integral extension, (2) every generator is integral, (3) the codomain is module-finite over `R`.

#### Quotient Rings

- **`poly-integralExt`**: For a monic polynomial `p` in an ideal `I ⊆ R[X]`, the composite `R → R[X]/I` is an integral extension.
- **`integral_factor`**: The factor lift `R[X]/(p) → polyImage f a` induced by an annihilator `p` of `a`.

#### Integral Closure

- **`integralClosure-subring`**: The subring of `E` consisting of all elements integral over `f`, with proofs of closure under `0`, `+`, `1`, `*`, and negation.
- **`integralClosure-subring.integral-combined`**: Auxiliary lemma showing that the iterated polynomial-image construction over two integral elements yields an integral extension.
- **`integralClosure`**: The integral closure of `R` in `E` as a commutative ring.
- **`integralClosure-left`**: The corestriction `R → integralClosure f`.
- **`integralClosure-right`**: The inclusion `integralClosure f → E`.
- **`isIntegrallyClosed`**: A homomorphism `f` is integrally closed if every element of its codomain that is integral over `f` lies in the image of `f`.
- **`integralClosure-integral`**: The map `R → integralClosure f` is an integral extension.
- **`integralClosure-closed`**: The inclusion `integralClosure f → E` is integrally closed.

#### Colimits

- **`integralExt-directColimit`**: A directed colimit of integral extensions (in `RingCat` over a bottom-join semilattice) is an integral extension into the colimit.
