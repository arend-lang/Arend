### Algebra.Ring.Localization

Localization of commutative rings at a multiplicative subset, including the universal property and the field of fractions construction.

This module specializes the monoid localization of `Algebra.Monoid.Localization` to rings. `RingLocalization` extends the monoid-level `Localization` record to the case where the base `B` and the localization `L` are commutative rings and `inL` is a ring homomorphism, and adds ring-specific consequences of the universal property. `LocRing` is the concrete construction on the quotient `LocType S` of triples `(numerator, denominator, proof denominator ∈ S)`, with `locR` the canonical map `R → LocRing S`. Specializing to a decidable integral domain with `S` the non-zero elements yields the field of fractions.

#### Abstract Localization

- **`RingLocalization`**: Extends the monoid `Localization` with `B` and `L` being `CRing`s and `inL` a `RingHom`. Describes a ring `L` obtained from `B` by inverting the submonoid `S`.
- **`RingLocalization.liftHom`**: Extends a ring homomorphism `f : B → T` that sends every element of `S` to an invertible to a ring homomorphism `L → T`.
- **`RingLocalization.fromLocalization`**: Upgrades a monoid-level `Localization` between commutative rings with a ring homomorphism `f` to a `RingLocalization`.
- **`RingLocalization.liftHom1`**: Version of `liftHom` for localization at the powers of a single element `a`: it only requires `f a` to be invertible.
- **`RingLocalization.zro-char`**: `inL x = 0` iff there is `c ∈ S` with `x * c = 0`.
- **`RingLocalization.loc-trivial-char`**: The localization is trivial (`0 = 1` in `L`) iff `0 ∈ S`.
- **`RingLocalization.nilpotent-trivial`**: If a nilpotent element lies in `S`, then `0 = 1` in `L`.
- **`RingLocalization.isLocal-char`**: `L` satisfies `IsLocal` iff for all `x`, `y` with `x + y ∈ S` there is `r` such that `x * r ∈ S` or `y * r ∈ S`.
- **`RingLocalization.isEpiHom`**: Two ring homomorphisms out of `L` that agree on the image of `inL` are equal (`inL` is an epimorphism of rings).

#### Concrete Construction

- **`LocRing`**: Concrete `CRing` instance on `LocType S` (fractions `r/s` with `s ∈ S` modulo the localization equivalence), for a submonoid `S` of a commutative ring `R`. Defines zero, addition, negation and the natural-number coefficients on equivalence classes; multiplication comes from `LocMonoid S`.
- **`loc_unequals_domain`**: Over an integral domain with `0 ∉ S`, equality of fractions `inl~ a = inl~ b` reduces to plain cross-multiplication: `a.1 * b.2 = b.1 * a.2`.

#### Canonical Map

- **`locR`**: The canonical ring homomorphism `R → LocRing S`.
- **`locR-isEpi`**: Two ring homomorphisms out of `LocRing S` that agree after composing with `locR` are equal.
- **`localization-zro-char`**: A fraction `inl~ a` is zero iff there is `c ∈ S` with `a.1 * c = 0`.

#### Localization of Ideals

- **`LocalizationIdeal`**: Given an ideal `I` of the ring `I.S`, a ring homomorphism `f : I.S → R` and a localization `L` at a submonoid `S` of `I.S`, the ideal of `R` consisting of those `a` with `a * f b = f c` for some `b ∈ S` and `c ∈ I`.
- **`LocalizationIdeal.unit-char`**: The localized ideal contains `1` iff `I` meets `S`.
- **`LocalizationIdeal.sclosure-char`**: The localization of the ideal generated by `U` is the ideal generated by the image of `U` under `f`.

#### Field of Fractions

- **`FieldOfQuotients`**: For a decidable integral domain `D`, the localization `LocRing D.subMonoid` at its non-zero elements is a `DiscreteField`, with decidable equality and explicit inverses for non-zero elements.
