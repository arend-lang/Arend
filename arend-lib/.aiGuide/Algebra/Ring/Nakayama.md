### Algebra.Ring.Nakayama

Nakayama's lemma and its consequences for finitely generated modules over commutative rings.

#### Nakayama's Lemma

- **`nakayama`**: For a finitely generated module `M` over a commutative ring `R` with ideal `I`, if `M ⊆ I·M`, then there exists `e : R` with `1 - e ∈ I` such that `e` annihilates every element of `M` (i.e. `e *c m = 0` for all `m`).
- **`nakayama'`**: Dual formulation: under the same hypotheses, there exists `e ∈ I` acting as the identity on `M` (`e *c m = m` for all `m`).
- **`nakayama-ideal`**: Idempotent form for ideals: if `I` is finitely generated and `I ⊆ I²`, then `I` is generated by a single idempotent `u` (with `u * u = u` and `I = (u)`).

#### Consequences

- **`linear-surj-equiv`**: A surjective endomorphism of a finitely generated module over a commutative ring is an equivalence (i.e. automatically injective, hence an isomorphism).
