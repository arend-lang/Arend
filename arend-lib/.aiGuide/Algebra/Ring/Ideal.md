### Algebra.Ring.Ideal

Ideals of (commutative) rings, their lattice structure, multiplication, and constructions of generated and maximal ideals.

The module layers ideal notions on top of `SubAddGroup`: left, right, two-sided, and (for commutative rings) plain ideals are records adding the appropriate absorption axioms. For a `CRing` an ideal is automatically two-sided and carries an `LModule` structure on its underlying subgroup. Generation is handled by an explicit `closure` construction whose elements are finite linear combinations, giving uniform definitions of principal, finitely generated, sum, and product ideals. The `IdealLattice` and `IdealMonoid` instances assemble these into a bounded lattice (with intersection as meet, closure of the union as join) and a commutative monoid under ideal multiplication, while `countable-maximal` shows that over a countable ring with decidable membership every proper finitely generated ideal extends to a maximal one.

#### Ideal Records

- **`LIdeal`**: Left ideal of a `Ring`; extends `SubAddGroup` with `ideal-left : contains a -> contains (r * a)`. Negative-closure is derived.
- **`RIdeal`**: Right ideal; symmetric `ideal-right`. Provides `ldiv`: divisibility transports membership.
- **`TIdeal`**: Two-sided ideal, extending both `LIdeal` and `RIdeal`.
- **`Ideal`**: Ideal of a `CRing`; extends `TIdeal` with right-absorption derived from commutativity. The main abstraction used throughout the rest of the module.

#### Derived Structures on an Ideal

- **`Ideal.radical`**: The radical ideal `√I = { a | ∃ n, aⁿ ∈ I }`, packaged as an `Ideal S`.
- **`Ideal.module`**: The underlying `LModule S` of the ideal, with scalar action by ring multiplication.
- **`Ideal.module.finitelyGenerated`**: A finitely generated ideal yields a finitely generated module.
- **`radical-superset`**: `I ⊆ √I`.
- **`radical_pow`**: `xⁿ ∈ √I ⇒ x ∈ √I`.
- **`radical-univ`**: Universal property of the radical with respect to ideals containing `I`.

#### Sums and Membership Properties

- **`bigSum`**: Closure of an ideal under finite array sums.
- **`finSum`**: Closure under sums indexed by a `FinSet`.

#### Generation Predicates

- **`IsGeneratedBy`**: An array `l` generates the ideal: each `l i` lies in it and every element is a linear combination of `l`.
- **`IsFinitelyGenerated`**: Existence of some generating array.
- **`IsGeneratedBy1`**: Generation by a single element `a`, equivalent to `a` left-dividing every member.
- **`IsPrincipal`**: Existence of a single generator.
- **`principal->finitelyGenerated`**: Principal ideals are finitely generated.
- **`IsMaximal`**: `1 ∉ I` and for every `x` either `x ∈ I` or `1 - y·x ∈ I` for some `y` (constructive maximality).

#### Closure Constructions (in `\where` of `Ideal`)

- **`closure`**: Ideal generated by a family `g : J -> R`; elements are `∑ pᵢ · g(jᵢ)`.
- **`sclosure`**: Closure of a subset `U : R -> \Prop`, viewing `U` as an indexed family.
- **`closure-superset`**, **`sclosure-superset`**: Generators belong to their closure.
- **`closure-univ`**, **`sclosure-univ`**: Universal property — any ideal containing the generators contains the closure.
- **`closure1`**: Principal ideal generated by `a`, i.e. closure over the unit type at `a`.
- **`closure1-lem`**: `closure1 a b ↔ ∃ c, b = a · c`.
- **`closure1_LDiv`**: `closure1 a b ↔ a ∣ b` (truncated).
- **`closure1-principal`** (with `generated`): `closure1 a` is principal, generated by `a`.
- **`closure2-lem`**: Membership in a closure indexed by `Bool` is a two-term combination.
- **`fin-closure-lem`**: Membership for `FinSet`-indexed closures via `FinSum`.
- **`closureN-lem`**: Membership for closures of an array of generators via `BigSum`. Uses helpers `singleton-lem` (indicator-sum collapses to one term) and `suc-eq` (decidable `Nat` equality on successors).
- **`closure-generated`**, **`closure-finGenerated`**: Closures of arrays are (finitely) generated by them.

#### Chain Conditions and Unions

- **`ChainCondition`**: For an ascending chain `I : Nat -> Ideal R`, eventually `I (suc n) ⊆ I n`.
- **`fromMonoidChainCondition`**: Derives the ideal chain condition for chains of principal ideals from a divisibility chain condition on the `DivQuotient` monoid.
- **`union`**: Ideal `⋃ Iₙ` of an ascending chain (with `inc` witnessing inclusion); membership is existence of an `n`.
  - **`union.include`**: Membership is preserved when bumping the index by any `m`.

#### Maximal Ideal Existence

- **`countable-maximal`**: For a countable `CRing` with decidable membership in finitely generated ideals, every proper finitely generated ideal `I` extends to a maximal ideal `M ⊇ I`. Built by enumerating ring elements and inductively adjoining each one when it does not produce `1`. Internal helpers:
  - **`next`**: Given an FG ideal `I` and ring element indexed by `n`, the FG ideal `I + (eₙ)`.
  - **`next-fg`**: `next` preserves finite generation.
  - **`ideal`**: The inductively defined chain of FG ideals.
  - **`ideal-proper`**: Each stage avoids containing `1`.

#### Lattice Structure

- **`IdealLattice`**: `BoundedLattice (Ideal R)` with:
  - **`<=`**: Pointwise inclusion of containment predicates.
  - **`meet`**: Intersection — `(I ∧ J) x = I x × J x`.
  - **`top`**: The unit ideal (contains everything).
  - **`join`**: Closure of the disjoint union of `I` and `J`'s elements.
  - **`bottom`**: Zero ideal `{0}`.

#### Multiplicative Monoid Structure

- **`IdealMonoid`**: `CMonoid (Ideal R)` under ideal multiplication, with `ide = top`.
  - **`func`**: The family `(a, b, _, _) ↦ a · b` over pairs from `I × J`.
  - **`product`**: `I · J = closure (func I J)`.
  - **`product-left`**, **`product-right`**: `I · J ⊆ I` and `I · J ⊆ J`.
  - **`product_meet`**: `I · J ⊆ I ∧ J`.
