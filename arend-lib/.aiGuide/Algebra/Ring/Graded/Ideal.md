### Algebra.Ring.Graded.Ideal

Homogeneous ideals in graded commutative rings: ideals closed under taking homogeneous components.

#### Homogeneous Ideals

- **`HomogenIdeal`**: Class extending `Ideal` over a `GradedCRing`. Adds the axiom `isHomogenIdeal`: if a homogeneous array `l` has its big sum in the ideal, then every element of `l` is in the ideal. Equivalently, the ideal is closed under decomposition into homogeneous parts.

#### Generation by Homogeneous Elements

- **`HomogenIdeal.hasHomogenGen`**: Predicate stating that an ideal `I` has a generating set `U` of homogeneous elements, i.e. every `x` in `U` is homogeneous of some degree, and `I` equals the ideal closure of `U`.
- **`HomogenIdeal.toGen`**: Every homogeneous ideal admits a homogeneous generating set, constructed from the homogeneous decompositions of its elements.
- **`HomogenIdeal.fromGen`**: Conversely, any ideal that (truncatedly) has a homogeneous generating set is a homogeneous ideal. Uses `homogen-similar` and `sum-decomp` to extract homogeneous components.

#### Examples

- **`irrelevantIdeal`**: The irrelevant ideal of a graded commutative ring `R`, generated by all homogeneous elements of strictly positive degree. Built via `HomogenIdeal.fromGen` from the predicate `∃ (n : Nat) (isHomogen x (suc n))`.
