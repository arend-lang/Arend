### Algebra.Ring.Boolean.SubBoolean

- **`SubBooleanPseudoRing`**: Class extending `SubPseudoRing`, with the ambient ring `S` overridden to be a `BooleanRing`. Represents a sub-pseudo-ring of a Boolean ring.
- **`IBooleanPseudoRing`**: Instance giving the underlying sub-pseudo-ring `IPseudoRing S` of a `SubBooleanPseudoRing` the structure of a `BooleanRing`; the idempotence axiom is inherited from the ambient Boolean ring.
