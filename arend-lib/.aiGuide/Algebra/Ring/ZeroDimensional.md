### Algebra.Ring.ZeroDimensional

Characterizations of zero-dimensional commutative rings (Krull dimension ≤ 0), von Neumann regular rings, and their relationship to fields and Bezout rings.

#### Zero-Dimensional Ring Characterizations

- **`zeroDimensional-char`**: TFAE statement equating `R.IsZeroDimensional`, `R.Dim<= 0`, and the property that for every `a : R` there exist `b : R` and `k : Nat` with `k /= 0`, `b` idempotent, and `b` mutually dividing `pow a k`.
- **`zeroDimensional-char.aux`**: Helper lemma showing that `x = x * c` implies `x = x * pow c n` for any `n : Nat`.
- **`zeroDimensional_local-char`**: TFAE statement for local zero-dimensional rings, equating: zero-dimensionality plus the property that every `a` or `a + 1` is invertible; zero-dimensionality plus connectedness; and the property that every element is either invertible or nilpotent.

#### Von Neumann Regular Rings

- **`vonNeumann_idempotent`**: For a von Neumann regular ring `R` and any `a : R`, produces an idempotent `u` such that `a` divides `u` and `a = u * a`.
- **`vonNeumann-char`**: TFAE characterization of von Neumann regular rings: `R.IsVonNeumannRegular`; zero-dimensional and reduced; every finitely generated ideal is generated by an idempotent; every ideal is idempotent (`I * I = I`); and the meet of any two ideals equals their product (`I ∧ J = I * J`).
- **`vonNeumann_Bezout`**: A von Neumann regular ring is Bezout (every finitely generated ideal is principal).

#### Zero-Dimensional Impotent Rings and Fields

- **`zeroDim->inv`**: In a zero-dimensional impotent commutative ring, every element is either zero or invertible.
- **`zeroDim->field`**: Constructs a `DiscreteField` structure on a zero-dimensional impotent commutative ring, using `zeroDim->inv` to decide whether each element is zero or invertible.
