### Algebra.Ring.MonoidRing

Construction of monoid rings (and monoid semirings/algebras) `R[M]` as quotients of arrays of coefficient-monomial pairs, with evaluation and universal-property machinery.

#### Underlying Set and Equivalence

- **`MonoidSet`**: The underlying set `R[M]` of a monoid ring, defined as a quotient of `Array (\Sigma R M)` by the relation `~`.
- **`~`**: Equivalence relation generated by permutation, symmetry, dropping zero coefficients, and combining like terms `(a + b, m) ~ (a, m) :: (b, m)`.
- **`inMS~`**, **`inMS`**: Inject an array of pairs into the quotient as a `Quotient` or `MonoidSet` element.
- **`~-msequiv`**: A single `~`-step yields equality in `MonoidSet`.
- **`~_++-left`**, **`~_++-right`**, **`~_++`**, **`~_Big++`**: Closures of `~` are compatible with array concatenation (left, right, both, big-concat).
- **`~_map`**: `~` is preserved under componentwise mapping by an `AddMonoidHom` on coefficients and a function on monomials.
- **`toClosure`**, **`fromClosure`**: Converting between `inMS l = inMS l'` and the transitive closure `Closure ~ l l'`.
- **`unique-sum`**: An array all of whose monomials equal `m` collapses to a single term `(BigSum coefs, m)` in the closure.

#### Monomials and Construction

- **`msMonomial`**: Build a single-term element `r·m` of `MonoidSet M R` from `r : R` and `m : M`.
- **`monoidSet-ext`**: Lift equality in `Quotient` to equality in `MonoidSet`.

#### Coefficient Extraction

- **`monoidSet-coefs`**: Sum the coefficients of all monomials satisfying a decidable predicate `P : M -> \Prop`.
- **`msCoef`**: For `M : DecSet`, the coefficient of monomial `m` in `p`, defined via `monoidSet-coefs (decideEq m)`.
- **`msCoef_monomial`**: `msCoef (msMonomial r m) m = r`.
- **`msCoef_map`**: Coefficient extraction commutes with mapping by an injective monomial map and an `AddMonoidHom`.
- **`msCoef/=0_Index`**: A nonzero coefficient witnesses that `m` appears among the monomials of a representative array.

#### Algebraic Structure Instances

- **`MonoidAbMonoid`**: `MonoidSet M R` is an `AbMonoid` (additive structure: zero is `nil`, addition is concatenation).
- **`MonoidAbGroup`**: For `R : AddGroup`, `MonoidSet M R` is an `AbGroup`; negation negates each coefficient.
- **`MonoidSemiring`**: For `M : Monoid`, `R : Semiring`, gives `MonoidSet M R` the semiring structure with multiplication via the `pairs` combinator (convolution `(a,m)·(b,n) = (a*b, m*n)`).
- **`MonoidRing`**: Combines `MonoidSemiring` and `MonoidAbGroup` to give the ring `R[M]` for `M : Monoid`, `R : Ring`.
- **`MonoidCSemiring`**: Commutative-semiring version when `M : CMonoid` and `R : CSemiring`.
- **`MonoidAlgebra`**: For `M : CMonoid`, `R : CRing`, equips `R[M]` with a `CAlgebra R` structure via `monoidRingHom`.

#### Ring Homomorphisms

- **`monoidRingHom`**: The canonical ring map `R -> R[M]` sending `a` to `(a, 1)`.
- **`monoidSet-map`**: Functorial action: maps `R[M] -> S[N]` from a function `M -> N` and an `AddMonoidHom R -> S`.
- **`monoidSet-hom`**: The map `monoidSet-map f g` is an `AddMonoidHom`.
- **`monoidSet-semiringHom`**, **`monoidSet-ringHom`**: Upgrade to a `SemiringHom`/`RingHom` from a `MonoidHom` and `SemiringHom`/`RingHom` on coefficients.

#### Monomial Algebra Lemmas

- **`msMonomial_+`**: `msMonomial (a+b) m = msMonomial a m + msMonomial b m`.
- **`msMonomial_BigSum`**: Distributes `msMonomial __ m` over a sum.
- **`msMonomial_0`**: `msMonomial 0 m = 0`.
- **`msMonomial_*`**: `msMonomial (a*b) 1 = msMonomial a 1 * msMonomial b 1`.
- **`msMonomial_pow`**: `pow (msMonomial r m) n = msMonomial (pow r n) (pow m n)`.
- **`msMonomial-split`**: Factorization `msMonomial a m = msMonomial a 1 * msMonomial 1 m`.
- **`msMonomial_BigSum-split`**: Any element `inMS l` equals the sum of its monomials.
- **`msMonomial_BigProd`**, **`msMonomial_BigProd_ide`**: Distribute `BigProd` over `msMonomial`.

#### Coefficient Lemmas

- **`monoidSet-coefs_+`**: `monoidSet-coefs` is additive.
- **`monoidSet-coefs_BigSum`**: `monoidSet-coefs` distributes over `BigSum`.
- **`msCoef-split`**: Every `p : MonoidSet M R` is a finite sum `∑ msMonomial (msCoef p m) m` over a duplicate-free list of monomials.
- **`msCoefs-fin`**: For decidable equality on `R`, the support `{m | msCoef p m /= 0}` is a `FinSet`.
- **`msCoef/=0`**: A nonzero element has at least one monomial with nonzero coefficient.

#### Decidable Normal Form

- **`DecMonoidSet`**: An alternative quotient where representatives are arrays with distinct monomials and nonzero coefficients, modulo permutation only.
- **`DecMonoidSet.Type`**: The carrier `\Sigma (l : Array ...) (injectivity of __.2) (∀ j, (l j).1 /= 0)`.
- **`DecMonoidSet.inDMS`**: Inject into the quotient.
- **`DecMonoidSet.~-dmsequiv`**, **`DecMonoidSet.unext`**: Equality in `DecMonoidSet` corresponds to permutation of representatives.
- **`decToMonoidSet`**: The forgetful map `DecMonoidSet M R -> MonoidSet M R`.
- **`monoidSetDec-equiv`**: For `M`, `R` with decidable equality, `decToMonoidSet` is an `Equiv` — i.e. `MonoidSet` admits unique normal forms.
  - Auxiliaries: **`msNormVars`**, **`msNorm`**, **`msNorm'`** (compute the support and normalize), **`+_~`**, **`remove_~`**, **`msNorm=msNorm'`**, **`msNorm'-norm`**, **`msNormVars-norm`**, **`msNorm_~`**, **`msNormVars_~`**, **`msNormVars_~'`** (normalization is well-defined and respects `~`).

#### Evaluation (Universal Property)

- **`evalMS`**: Evaluate `x : MonoidSet M R` at a function `f : M -> R` by computing `∑ s.1 * f s.2`.
- **`evalMSMonoidHom`**: `evalMS __ f` is an `AddMonoidHom`.
- **`evalMSSemiringHom`**: For a `MonoidHom f : M -> R` (with `R : CSemiring`), evaluation is a `SemiringHom R[M] -> R`.
- **`evalMSRingHom`**: Same for `R : CRing` — the universal map out of `MonoidRing M R`.
- **`evalMS_monomial`**: `evalMS (msMonomial a 0) f = a`.
- **`evalMS_map`**, **`evalMS_map2`**: Evaluation commutes with `monoidSet-map` (in the monomial map, and in the coefficient ring map).

#### Polynomial Embedding

- **`polyToMonoidSet`**: Send a polynomial `p : Poly R` to `R[M]` by substituting a fixed `m : M` for the variable.
- **`polyToMonoidSetHom`**: The substitution is an `AddMonoidHom PolyRing -> MonoidRing`.
- **`polyToMonoidSetRingHom`**: For `M : CMonoid`, `R : CRing`, upgrades to a `RingHom`. Auxiliary **`func-*c`** handles scalar multiplication.
- **`polyToMonoidSet-eval`**: `evalMS (polyToMonoidSet m p) f = polyEval p (f m)` — substitution then evaluation equals direct polynomial evaluation.

#### Uniqueness of Ring Maps Out

- **`monoidRingHom-unique`**: Two ring maps `f, g : R[M] -> S` agreeing on constants `msMonomial r 1` and on monomials `msMonomial 1 m` are equal — the universal property of `R[M]`.
- **`monoidSet-list`**: Helper expressing `inMS l` as `BigSum` of monomials, used in the uniqueness proof.
