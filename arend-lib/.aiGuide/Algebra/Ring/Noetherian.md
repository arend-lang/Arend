### Algebra.Ring.Noetherian

Noetherian commutative rings, defined via the ascending chain condition on finitely generated ideals.

This module formalizes Noetherianity constructively. Rather than asserting that every ideal is finitely generated (which is classically equivalent but constructively stronger), the definition requires that every chain of finitely generated ideals stabilizes. This phrasing makes the property accessible without relying on classical logic, while still capturing the essential finiteness behavior of Noetherian rings.

#### Classes

- **`WeaklyNoetherianCRing`**: Extends `CRing`. A commutative ring in which every chain of finitely generated ideals stabilizes.
  - **`isWeaklyNoetherian`**: Given a sequence of ideals `I : Nat -> Ideal \this`, if each `I n` is finitely generated (`Ideal.IsFinitelyGenerated`), then the chain `I` satisfies `Ideal.ChainCondition` (i.e., it eventually stabilizes).
- **`NoetherianCRing`**: Extends `WeaklyNoetherianCRing`. A commutative ring satisfying the stronger, bar-induction-style Noetherian condition `IsNoetherian`; the weak chain condition `isWeaklyNoetherian` is derived from it.
  - **`isNoetherian`**: The ring satisfies `IsNoetherian \this`.

#### Noetherian condition

- **`IsNoetherian`**: `IsNoetherian R` holds when `Bar PausesElem nil`, i.e. every sequence of elements of `R` eventually has an element lying in the ideal generated by the later ones.
- **`Pauses`**: A `\Prop`-valued predicate on arrays: `Pauses R l` holds when some entry `l j` is related by `R` to the tail `drop (suc j) l`. Its `toIndex` and `fromIndex` convert to and from an explicit index `j`, and `isBarMonotone` shows it is bar-monotone.
- **`PausesElem`**: `Pauses` for arrays of ring elements, where an element is related to the tail when it lies in the ideal generated by the tail (`lclosure`). Comes with lemmas for inserting into, extending and mapping along ring homomorphisms, and `lincomb`.
- **`PausesIdeal`**: `Pauses` for arrays of finitely generated ideals, where an ideal is related to the tail when it is contained in the ideal generated by the tail.
- **`idealToElem`**: Transfers a bar for `PausesIdeal` on the principal ideals of an array `l` to a bar for `PausesElem` on `l`.
- **`elemToIdeal`**: From a bar for `PausesElem` on `nil`, produces a bar for `PausesIdeal` on `nil` (via the fan theorem).
