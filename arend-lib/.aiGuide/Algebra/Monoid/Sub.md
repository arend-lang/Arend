### Algebra.Monoid.Sub

Sub-semigroup, sub-monoid, and sub-additive-monoid structures, presented as predicates closed under the relevant operations, together with the induced algebraic structure on the subset.

This module bundles a sub-structure as a `SubSet` enriched with closure conditions (closure under `*`, `+`, identity, etc.) and derives the carrier structure on `\Sigma (x : S) (contains x)`. The pattern is uniform: each class extends the corresponding `SubPointed`/`SubSet` and adds a closure axiom, then exposes an `I...`-named cowith that realizes the inherited algebraic interface. Commutative variants are produced via `cStruct`/`abStruct` `\where`-helpers rather than separate classes. The free sub-monoid generated by a subset is built explicitly via an iterated "apply one product step" operator `apl`, with `closure` as its colimit, giving an inductive characterization plus a universal property.

#### Sub-semigroups

- **`SubSemigroup`**: Class extending `SubSet` over a `Semigroup`, adding `contains_*` (closure under multiplication). Exposes `ISemigroup`, the induced semigroup on the subset.
- **`SubSemigroup.cStruct`**: Promotes a `SubSemigroup` of a `CSemigroup` to a `CSemigroup` on the subset, by inheriting commutativity.

#### Sub-monoids

- **`SubMonoid`**: Class extending `SubPointed` and `SubSemigroup` over a `Monoid`. Carries `IMonoid` (the induced monoid structure) and a canonical embedding into the ambient monoid.
- **`SubMonoid.contains_pow`**: Closure under powers: if `contains x`, then `contains (Monoid.pow x n)` for every `n : Nat`.
- **`SubMonoid.sub-pow`**: Compatibility of powers with the embedding: `(IMonoid.pow x n).1 = Monoid.pow x.1 n`.
- **`SubMonoid.embedMonoid`**: The first-projection `MonoidHom IMonoid S` exhibiting the sub-monoid inclusion.
- **`SubMonoid.cStruct`**: Lifts a `SubMonoid` of a `CMonoid` to a `CMonoid`, combining `IMonoid` with the commutative sub-semigroup structure.
- **`SubMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-monoid (everything is contained), built from `SubPointed.max`.
- **`SubMonoid.powers`**: The sub-monoid `{a^n | n : Nat}` generated by a single element `a : M`, given by an existential over exponents.

#### Closure of a subset

- **`SubMonoid.closure`**: The sub-monoid generated by an arbitrary `SubSet M`, defined as `∃ (n : Nat) (iterr apl n S x)` — elements reachable in finitely many `apl` steps.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl`**: One step of the closure construction: a new `SubSet` containing the original elements, the identity `1`, and all products `y * z` with `y, z` already contained.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.ext`**: Inclusion `S <= closure S` of a subset into its closure.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.univ`**: Universal property: any sub-monoid `S'` containing `S` also contains `closure S`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl-inc`**: Monotonicity of iteration: if `n <= m`, then `iterr apl n S x` implies `iterr apl m S x`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl-inc.alt`**: Reformulation in terms of `n + m`, used to align iteration depths.

#### Decidable sub-monoids

- **`DecSubMonoid`**: Class combining `SubMonoid` with `DecSubPointed`, giving decidable membership.

#### Sub-additive-monoids

- **`SubAddMonoid`**: Additive analogue extending `SubAddPointed`, with closure axiom `contains_+`. Exposes `IAddMonoid`, the induced additive monoid on the subset.
- **`SubAddMonoid.sub-BigSum`**: Compatibility of finite sums with the embedding: `(IAddMonoid.BigSum l).1 = AddMonoid.BigSum (map __.1 l)`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.abStruct`**: Promotes a `SubAddMonoid` of an `AbMonoid` to an `AbMonoid`, inheriting `+-comm`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-additive-monoid, built from `SubAddPointed.max`.
