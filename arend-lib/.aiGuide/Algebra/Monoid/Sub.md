### Algebra.Monoid.Sub

Sub-semigroups, sub-monoids, and sub-additive-monoids: subsets closed under the relevant algebraic operations, together with their induced structures and constructions.

#### Sub-Semigroups

- **`SubSemigroup`**: Class extending `SubSet` over a `Semigroup`, with closure under multiplication via `contains_*`.
- **`SubSemigroup.cStruct`**: Induces a `CSemigroup` structure on a sub-semigroup of a commutative semigroup.

#### Sub-Monoids

- **`SubMonoid`**: Class extending `SubPointed` and `SubSemigroup` over a `Monoid` (closed under `1` and `*`).
- **`SubMonoid.cStruct`**: Induces a `CMonoid` structure on a sub-monoid of a commutative monoid.
- **`SubMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-monoid containing every element of `A`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure`**: The sub-monoid generated by an arbitrary `SubSet S` of `M`, defined as elements reachable by finitely many iterations of `apl`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.ext`**: The original set `S` embeds into its closure: `S <= closure S`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.univ`**: Universal property — any sub-monoid `S'` containing `S` also contains `closure S`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl`**: One-step closure operator, adding `1` and all products `y * z` of existing elements.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl-inc`**: Monotonicity of iterated `apl`: more iterations only enlarge the set.
- **`SubMonoid.powers`**: The sub-monoid `{aⁿ | n : Nat}` of powers of a fixed element `a : M`.

#### Decidable Sub-Monoids

- **`DecSubMonoid`**: Sub-monoid with decidable membership, extending `SubMonoid` and `DecSubPointed`.

#### Sub-Additive-Monoids

- **`SubAddMonoid`**: Class extending `SubAddPointed` over an `AddMonoid`, with closure under addition via `contains_+`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.abStruct`**: Induces an `AbMonoid` structure on a sub-additive-monoid of an abelian additive monoid.
- **`SubAddMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-additive-monoid containing every element of `A`.
