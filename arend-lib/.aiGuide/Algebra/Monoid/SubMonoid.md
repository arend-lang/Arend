### Algebra.Monoid.SubMonoid

Sub-semigroup, sub-monoid, and sub-additive-monoid structures, presented as predicates closed under the relevant operations, together with the induced algebraic structure on the subset.

This module bundles a sub-structure as a `SubSet` enriched with closure conditions (closure under `*`, `+`, identity, etc.) and derives the carrier structure on `\Sigma (x : S) (contains x)`. The pattern is uniform: each class extends the corresponding `SubPointed`/`SubSet` and adds a closure axiom, then provides an `I...`-named instance that realizes the inherited algebraic interface. Commutative variants are provided by separate instances (`ICSemigroup`, `ICMonoid`, `IAbMonoid`) rather than separate classes. The free sub-monoid generated by a subset is built explicitly via an iterated "apply one product step" operator `apl`, with `closure` as its colimit, giving an inductive characterization plus a universal property.

#### Sub-semigroups

- **`SubSemigroup`**: Class extending `SubSet` over a `Semigroup`, adding `contains_*` (closure under multiplication). The induced semigroup on the subset is `ISemigroup`.
- **`ISemigroup`**: The semigroup structure on the elements of a `SubSemigroup`, with multiplication `(x.1 * y.1, contains_* x.2 y.2)`.
- **`ICSemigroup`**: The induced semigroup of a sub-semigroup of a `CSemigroup` is commutative.

#### Sub-monoids

- **`SubMonoid`**: Class extending `SubPointed` and `SubSemigroup` over a `Monoid`. The induced monoid on the subset is `IMonoid`, which has a canonical embedding `IMonoid.embed` into the ambient monoid.
- **`SubMonoid.contains_pow`**: Closure under powers: if `contains x`, then `contains (Monoid.pow x n)` for every `n : Nat`.
- **`SubMonoid.sub-pow`**: Compatibility of powers with the embedding: `((IMonoid \this).pow x n).1 = Monoid.pow x.1 n`.
- **`SubMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-monoid (everything is contained), built from `SubPointed.max`.
- **`SubMonoid.powers`**: The sub-monoid `{a^n | n : Nat}` generated by a single element `a : M`, given by an existential over exponents.
- **`IMonoid`**: The monoid structure on the elements of a `SubMonoid`; `IMonoid.embed` is the inclusion into the ambient monoid.
- **`ICMonoid`**: The induced monoid of a sub-monoid of a `CMonoid` is commutative.
- **`KernelMonoid`**: The kernel of a `MonoidHom` `f` as a sub-monoid of `f.Dom`.
- **`ImageMonoid`**: The image of a `MonoidHom` `f` as a sub-monoid of `f.Cod`.
- **`ImMonoid`**: The monoid induced on the image of `f`, i.e. `IMonoid (ImageMonoid f)`.
- **`ImMonoidLeftHom`**: The corestriction `f.Dom -> ImMonoid f` of `f` to its image, as a `MonoidHom`.

#### Closure of a subset

- **`SubMonoid.closure`**: The sub-monoid generated by an arbitrary `SubSet M`, defined as `∃ (n : Nat) (iterr apl n S x)` — elements reachable in finitely many `apl` steps.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl`**: One step of the closure construction: a new `SubSet` containing the original elements, the identity `1`, and all products `y * z` with `y, z` already contained.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.ext`**: Inclusion `S <= closure S` of a subset into its closure.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.univ`**: Universal property: any sub-monoid `S'` containing `S` also contains `closure S`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl-inc`**: Monotonicity of iteration: if `n <= m`, then `iterr apl n S x` implies `iterr apl m S x`.
- **`SubMonoid.closure.apl-inc.alt`**: Reformulation in terms of `n + m`, used to align iteration depths.

#### Decidable sub-monoids

- **`DecSubMonoid`**: Class combining `SubMonoid` with `DecSubPointed`, giving decidable membership.

#### Sub-additive-monoids

- **`SubAddMonoid`**: Additive analogue extending `SubAddPointed`, with closure axiom `contains_+`. The induced additive monoid on the subset is `IAddMonoid`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.sub-BigSum`**: Compatibility of finite sums with the embedding: `((IAddMonoid \this).BigSum l).1 = AddMonoid.BigSum (map __.1 l)`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.max`**: The maximal sub-additive-monoid, built from `SubAddPointed.max`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.contains_*n`**: Closure under multiples: if `contains x`, then `contains (n AddMonoid.*n x)` for every `n : Nat`.
- **`SubAddMonoid.contains_BigSum`**: If every entry of an array `l` is contained, then so is `AddMonoid.BigSum l`.
- **`IAddMonoid`**: The additive monoid structure on the elements of a `SubAddMonoid`; `IAddMonoid.embed` is the inclusion into the ambient monoid.
- **`IAbMonoid`**: The induced additive monoid of a sub-additive-monoid of an `AbMonoid` is commutative.
- **`KernelAddMonoid`**: The kernel of an `AddMonoidHom` `f` as a sub-additive-monoid of `f.Dom`.
- **`ImageAddMonoid`**: The image of an `AddMonoidHom` `f` as a sub-additive-monoid of `f.Cod`.
- **`ImAddMonoid`**: The additive monoid induced on the image of `f`, i.e. `IAddMonoid (ImageAddMonoid f)`.
- **`ImAddMonoidLeftHom`**: The corestriction `f.Dom -> ImAddMonoid f` of `f` to its image, as an `AddMonoidHom`.
