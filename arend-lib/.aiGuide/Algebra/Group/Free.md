### Algebra.Group.Free

Construction of the free group on a set, with its universal property and (decidable) reduction to normal form.

The free group on a set `S` is built as a quotient of the list monoid over `S × Bool` (where `Bool` indicates whether a generator appears or its inverse), modulo an equivalence generated by inserting/removing adjacent pairs `(x, p) :: (x, not p)`. The construction is split in two layers: the raw word monoid `W` with a manually defined `inv` and an "adjacency" relation, and the quotient group obtained by closing the relation under reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity. For a `DecSet` `S`, words are reducible to a canonical irreducible form by repeated cancellation, which yields decidable equality on the free group.

#### Free Group Construction

- **`FreeGroup`**: Free group on a set `S`, as `Group` instance. Carrier is `Quotient ~` over the word monoid; multiplication, inverse, and units lift from list concatenation.
- **`FreeGroup.can`**: Canonical inclusion `S -> FreeGroup S`, sending `s` to the singleton word `(s, true) :: nil`.
- **`FreeGroup.W`**: Underlying word monoid `ListMonoid {Σ S Bool}` of formal generators tagged with a sign.
- **`FreeGroup.inv`**: Word-level inverse: reverses the list and flips each Bool tag.
- **`FreeGroup.AreAdjacent`**: Single-step rewrite relation: `w_1` and `w_2` differ by inserting `(x, p) :: (x, not p)` at some position.
- **`FreeGroup.equivWords`**: Equivalence relation `~` on `W`, the reflexive/symmetric/transitive closure of `AreAdjacent`.

#### Lemmas on Words and Equivalence

- **`FreeGroup.inv_*`**: `inv (x * y) = inv y * inv x` for words.
- **`FreeGroup.adjacent_cons`**: Equivalence is preserved by prepending a generator.
- **`FreeGroup.adjacent_snoc`**: Equivalence is preserved by appending a generator.
- **`FreeGroup.equivMulRight`**, **`FreeGroup.equivMulLeft`**: Equivalence is a congruence for word concatenation on either side.
- **`FreeGroup.equiv_inv`**: Equivalence is preserved by `inv`.
- **`FreeGroup.inv_mul`**: `inv a * a ~ nil`, witnessing left inverses.

#### Universal Property

- **`FreeGroup.univ_map`**: Lifts `i : S -> G` to a map `List (Σ S Bool) -> G` by sending `(s, true)` to `i s`, `(s, false)` to `inverse (i s)`, and concatenation to product.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_map_++`**: `univ_map` is a monoid homomorphism: `univ_map i (l1 ++ l2) = univ_map i l1 * univ_map i l2`.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_equiv`**: `univ_map` respects the equivalence relation, so it descends to the quotient.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_map'`**: The induced map `FreeGroup S -> G` on the quotient.
- **`FreeGroupUniversalProperty`**: The universal property: for any group `G` and `i : S -> G`, there exists a `GroupHom (FreeGroup S) G` extending `i` along `FreeGroup.can`.

#### Reduction to Normal Form (Decidable Case)

- **`reduce`**: Normalizes a word by recursively cancelling adjacent inverse pairs `(x, b) :: (x, not b)`, requiring `S : DecSet`.
- **`reduce_equiv`**: Every word is equivalent to its reduction: `x ~ reduce x`.
- **`isIrreducible`**: Boolean check that no two adjacent positions cancel.
- **`isIrreducibleCons`**: If `a :: x` is irreducible, so is `x`.
- **`reduceIsIrreducible`**: `reduce x` is always irreducible.
- **`reduce_inP`**: Inserting an inverse pair `(s, b) :: (s, not b)` between two segments does not change `reduce`.
- **`reduce_equivWords`**: Equivalent words have equal reductions, the key lemma showing `reduce` is well-defined on the quotient.
- **`reduceHom`**: The induced map `FreeGroup S -> List (Σ S Bool)` returning the unique reduced representative.

#### Decidable Equality

- **`DecWordMonoid`**: `DecSet` instance on `List (Σ S Bool)` for `S : DecSet`, providing decidable equality of words.
- **`FreeDecGroup`**: `Group.Dec` instance on `FreeGroup S` for `S : DecSet`, deriving decidable equality from the unique normal form via `reduceHom`.
