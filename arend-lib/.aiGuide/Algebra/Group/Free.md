### Algebra.Group.Free

Construction of free groups over a set, the universal property, and decidable equality on free groups over a decidable set via word reduction.

#### Free Group Construction

- **`FreeGroup`**: Instance `(S : \Set) : Group`. The free group on `S`, defined as the quotient of words over `\Sigma S Bool` (letters paired with a polarity bit) by the equivalence generated by adjacent inverse-cancellation. Group operations descend from list concatenation, with the inverse reversing the list and flipping each polarity.
- **`FreeGroup.can`**: Canonical inclusion `S -> FreeGroup S` sending `s` to the singleton word `[(s, true)]`.

#### Internal Word Structure

- **`FreeGroup.W`**: The monoid of words `ListMonoid {\Sigma S Bool}` underlying the free group.
- **`FreeGroup.inv`**: Word-level inverse: reverses a word and negates each polarity bit.
- **`FreeGroup.inv_*`**: `inv (x * y) = inv y * inv x`, the antihomomorphism property of word inversion.
- **`FreeGroup.AreAdjacent`**: Relation `w_1 ~ w_2` witnessing that `w_2` is obtained from `w_1` by inserting a cancelling pair `(x, p) :: (x, not p)` at some position.
- **`FreeGroup.equivWords`**: The equivalence relation on words generated by `AreAdjacent` (its reflexive-symmetric-transitive closure), used to define the quotient.

#### Congruence Lemmas for the Word Equivalence

- **`FreeGroup.adjacent_cons`**: Word equivalence is preserved under prepending a generator.
- **`FreeGroup.adjacent_snoc`**: Word equivalence is preserved under appending a generator.
- **`FreeGroup.equivMulRight`**: Right multiplication preserves word equivalence: `a ~ b -> a * c ~ b * c`.
- **`FreeGroup.equivMulLeft`**: Left multiplication preserves word equivalence: `a ~ b -> c * a ~ c * b`.
- **`FreeGroup.equiv_inv`**: Word inversion preserves word equivalence.
- **`FreeGroup.inv_mul`**: `inv a * a ~ nil`, witnessing the group inverse on words.

#### Universal Property

- **`FreeGroup.univ_map`**: For a group `G` and map `i : S -> G`, extends `i` to a map on raw words `List (\Sigma S Bool) -> G` by sending each `(s, true)` to `i s` and `(s, false)` to its inverse.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_map_++`**: `univ_map` is a monoid homomorphism w.r.t. word concatenation.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_equiv`**: `univ_map` factors through word equivalence: equivalent words map to equal group elements.
- **`FreeGroup.univ_map'`**: The induced map `FreeGroup S -> G` on the quotient.
- **`FreeGroupUniversalProperty`**: Universal property of the free group: for any group `G` and map `i : S -> G`, produces a unique group homomorphism `f : FreeGroup S -> G` extending `i` along `FreeGroup.can`.

#### Word Reduction

- **`reduce`**: Normalizes a word over a `DecSet` by repeatedly cancelling adjacent inverse pairs (using `decideEq` and `xor` on polarities) from the right.
- **`reduce_equiv`**: Every word is `equivWords`-equivalent to its reduction.
- **`isIrreducible`**: Decidable predicate testing that a word contains no adjacent cancelling pair.
- **`isIrreducibleCons`**: Irreducibility is preserved under taking the tail.
- **`reduceIsIrreducible`**: The output of `reduce` is always irreducible.
- **`reduce_inP`**: Inserting a cancelling pair `(s, b) :: (s, not b)` between two words does not change the reduction.
- **`reduce_equivWords`**: Equivalent words have equal reductions, i.e. `reduce` is invariant under `FreeGroup.equivWords`.
- **`reduceHom`**: Lifts `reduce` to a function `FreeGroup S -> List (\Sigma S Bool)` on the quotient.

#### Decidable Equality

- **`DecWordMonoid`**: Instance `(S : DecSet) : DecSet` giving decidable equality on words `List (\Sigma S Bool)` via componentwise comparison.
- **`DecWordMonoid.first`**: Helper returning the head of a list with a default fallback.
- **`DecWordMonoid.word-dec`**: Decision procedure for equality of two words.
- **`FreeDecGroup`**: Instance `(S : DecSet) : Group.Dec`. The free group on a decidable set has decidable equality, decided by comparing the reductions of two words via `reduceHom`.
