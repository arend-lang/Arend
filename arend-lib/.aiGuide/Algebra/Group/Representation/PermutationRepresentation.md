### Algebra.Group.Representation.PermutationRepresentation

Constructs the permutation representation of a group on a free module generated by a G-set, and states a reducibility hypothesis.

#### Permutation Representation

- **`PermRepr`**: Given a ring `R`, group `G`, and `G`-set `X`, builds the linear representation `LinRepres R G` whose underlying module is the free `R`-module on `X` (functions `X -> R`), with `G` acting by permuting basis elements via `g ** f = \lam j => f (inverse g ** j)`. The inverse is needed to make the action a left action compatible with function composition.
- **`PermRepr.inverse_prod`**: Auxiliary lemma `inverse (m * n) = inverse n * inverse m`, the antihomomorphism property of group inversion, used to verify associativity of the action.
- **`PermRepr.R_m`**: The ring `R` viewed as a left module over itself (`RingLModule R`).
- **`PermRepr.Module`**: The underlying `R`-module of the permutation representation, defined as `PowerLModule X R_m` — the `X`-indexed power of `R_m`, i.e. functions `X -> R` with pointwise structure.

#### Reducibility Hypothesis

- **`PermutationRepresReducible`**: A class bundling the data needed to argue that a permutation representation is reducible. Fields:
  - `R : Ring`, `G : Group`, `X : GroupAction G` — the ambient ring, group, and `G`-set.
  - `somepoint`, `somepoint' : X` with `different-points : somepoint' /= somepoint` — two distinct points of `X`.
  - `not-trivial-ring : R.zro /= R.ide` — the ring is nontrivial.
  - `decideEq-somepoint (x : X) : Dec (x = somepoint)` — decidable equality with the chosen base point, used to construct a nontrivial invariant subspace.
