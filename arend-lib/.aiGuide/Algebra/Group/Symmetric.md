### Algebra.Group.Symmetric

The symmetric group of permutations of `Fin n`, with cycle/transposition decomposition and the sign homomorphism.

#### Symmetric Group Type

- **`Sym`**: The type of permutations of `Fin n`, defined as `Equiv {Fin n} {Fin n}`.
- **`Sym.fromEquiv`**: Coerces an `Equiv` on `Fin n` into a `Sym n`.
- **`Sym.equals`**: Extensionality: two permutations are equal if they agree pointwise.
- **`Sym.reduce`**: Reduces a permutation of `Fin (suc n)` to one of `Fin n` by skipping `e 0`.
- **`Sym.reduce_id`**: Reducing the identity yields the identity.
- **`SymmetricGroup`**: `Group` instance on `Sym n` with composition `transEquiv`, identity `idEquiv`, and inversion `symQEquiv`.
- **`SymmetricGroup.inv-isEquiv`**: The inversion map is itself an equivalence on `Sym n`.

#### Support of Permutations

- **`Sym.Support`**: The support predicate `e i /= i`, the set of points moved by `e`.
- **`Sym.SupportFin`**: `FinSet` instance for the support of a permutation.
- **`Sym.Support_inverse`**: Support is preserved under inversion.
- **`Sym.Support_ret`**: If `i` is in the support, so is `e.ret i`.
- **`Sym.Support_Not`**: If `i` is not in the support, then `e i = i`.
- **`isCycle`**: A permutation is a cycle if its support is generated by iterated application starting from one point.

#### Independent Permutations

- **`independent_*`**: Permutations with disjoint supports commute: `e * e' = e' * e`.
- **`independent_Support-right`**, **`independent_Support-left`**: Disjoint supports merge into the support of the product.
- **`independent_Support-conv`**: Converse: support of a product splits into supports of the factors.
- **`independent_Support_BigProd`**: Support of a big product of pairwise-independent permutations contains each factor's support.
- **`independent_Support_BigProd.conv`**: Converse for big products: any point in the product's support lies in some factor's support.

#### Cycles

- **`cycle`**: Builds a cycle from an injective array `l : Array (Fin n) k` mapping `l i` to `l (i+1 mod k)`.
- **`cycle.func`**: The underlying function of `cycle`, sending `l i` to the next element and fixing other points.
- **`cycle.func-inj`**: Injectivity of `cycle.func`.
- **`cycle.step`**: One step of a cycle: `cycle l inj (l i) = l (suc i mod k)`.
- **`cycle.steps`**: Iterated steps: `pow (cycle l inj) m (l 0) = l m`.
- **`Support_cycle`**: Any element in the support of `cycle l inj` is `l j` for some `j`.
- **`cycle_Support`**: Each `l j` lies in the support of `cycle l inj` when `k > 1`.
- **`cycle-isCycle`**: A `cycle` of length `> 1` satisfies `isCycle`.

#### Transpositions

- **`transposition`**: The transposition swapping `a` and `b` (when `a /= b`), built as a 2-element cycle.
- **`transposition.transposition-left`**, **`transposition.transposition-right`**: Swap behavior on `a` and `b`.
- **`transposition.transposition_/=`**: Fixes points distinct from `a` and `b`.
- **`transposition.transposition-isInv`**: Transpositions are involutions.
- **`transposition1`**: The adjacent transposition swapping `a` and `suc a` in `Sym (suc n)`.
- **`transposition1.transposition1-left`**, **`transposition1.transposition1-right`**: Behavior on `a` and `suc a`.
- **`transposition1.transposition1_/=`**, **`transposition1_<`**, **`transposition1_>`**: Fixes points outside `{a, suc a}`.
- **`transposition1.transposition1-isInv`**: Adjacent transpositions are involutions.

#### Recursive Structure and Cardinality

- **`symmetric-rec`**: The equivalence `Sym (suc n) ≃ Fin (suc n) × Sym n` via `(e 0, reduce e)`.
- **`cyclePerm`**: The cyclic permutation of `Fin n` shifting `0` to `k`.
- **`SymFin`**: `FinSet` instance for `Sym n` of cardinality `fac n`.
- **`SymFin.fin_equiv`**: Explicit equivalence `Fin (fac n) ≃ Sym n`.
- **`EquivFin`**: `FinSet` instance for `Equiv {A} {B}` between two finite sets (empty if cardinalities differ).

#### Lifting Permutations

- **`lift`**: Embeds `Sym n` into `Sym (suc n)` by fixing `0`.
- **`lift.aux`**: General version: lifts an `Equiv {Fin n} {Fin m}` to `Equiv {Fin (suc n)} {Fin (suc m)}`.
- **`lift_ide`**: `lift` preserves the identity.
- **`lift_cycle`**: `lift` of a cycle is a cycle on shifted indices.
- **`lift_transposition`**, **`lift_transposition1`**: `lift` commutes with both kinds of transpositions.
- **`lift_*`**: `lift` is multiplicative.
- **`lift_BigProd`**: `lift` distributes over big products.

#### Decomposition Theorems

- **`transposition1-decomp`**: Every permutation of `Fin (suc n)` is a product of adjacent transpositions, returning the explicit list.
- **`transposition1-decomp.BigProd-fixed`**: Big product of permutations all fixing `i` also fixes `i`.
- **`transposition1-decomp.BigProd-unique`**: Computes a big product at `x` when only one factor moves it.
- **`transposition1-decomp.aux`**, **`aux1`**: Inductive helpers expressing `e` in terms of `lift (reduce e)` and a product of `transposition1`s.
- **`cycle-decomp`**: Every permutation decomposes into a product of pairwise-disjoint cycles.
- **`cycle-decomp.aux`**: Recursive engine for `cycle-decomp`, peeling off one cycle at a time using the support's finiteness.
- **`cycle-decomp.pow-lem`**: If two iterates of `e` agree on `x`, the difference is a period.
- **`cycle-decomp.pow_mod`**: Iteration is periodic modulo the period of `x`.
- **`cycle-decomp.fixPoint-iter`**: Fixed points are fixed under all iterates.
- **`cycle-decomp.pow-rotate`**: Iteration commutes with `e`: `e (pow e k i) = pow e k (e i)`.

#### Sign Homomorphism

- **`sign`**: The sign of a permutation, `(-1)^(inversions e)` valued in any ring.
- **`sign.inversions`**: The number of inversions of `e`, recursively `e 0 + inversions (reduce e)`.
- **`sign.inversions_ide`**: The identity has zero inversions.
- **`sign.Inversions`**: The set of pairs `(i, j)` with `i < j` and `e j < e i`.
- **`sign.InversionsFin`**: `FinSet` instance showing this set has cardinality `inversions e`.
- **`sign.sign_hom`**: Ring homomorphisms preserve `sign`.
- **`sign_ide`**: `sign ide = ide`.
- **`sign_*`**: Multiplicativity of the sign.
- **`sign_*.Inverstions_transposition_>`**, **`Inverstions_transposition_<`**: Equivalences relating inversion sets of `e` and `transposition1 a * e` depending on whether `e a < e (suc a)`.
- **`sign_*.inverstions_transposition_>`**, **`inverstions_transposition_<`**: Numerical versions: multiplying by an adjacent transposition changes the inversion count by `±1`.
- **`sign_*.inversions_transposition`**: Parity of inversions flips under multiplication by `transposition1 a`.
- **`sign_*.inversions_*`**: Parity of inversions is additive under composition.
- **`sign_inverse`**: `sign (inverse e) = sign e`.
- **`signHom`**: Packages `sign` as a `MonoidHom (SymmetricGroup n) R` for any ring `R`.
