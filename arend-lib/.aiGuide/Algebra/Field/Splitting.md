### Algebra.Field.Splitting

Construction of splitting fields for polynomials over discrete fields.

A splitting field of a polynomial `p` over a field `k` is a field extension `K` in which `p` factors into linear factors and which is generated as an algebra by the roots. This module defines the property `IsSplittingField` and provides a generic recursive construction that adjoins one root at a time via a `RootFieldData` interface, where each step builds a maximal-ideal quotient `k[x]/(q)` for an irreducible factor `q` of `p`. The construction is parameterized by a predicate `P` on fields (preserved under the construction) and a polynomial property `S`, and is instantiated to produce countable splitting fields when the base field is countable.

#### Splitting Field Property

- **`IsSplittingField`**: Property of a ring homomorphism `f : k -> K` stating that `p` factors over `K` as a constant times a product of linear terms `x - aᵢ`, with `K` algebra-generated by the roots `aᵢ`.
- **`splitting-integral`**: A splitting field extension is an integral extension.
- **`splitting-monic`**: For a monic polynomial, the leading constant in the splitting factorization is `1`, so `polyMap f p` equals the bare product of linear factors.

#### Countable Splitting Fields

- **`countableSplittingField`**: Given a countable discrete field `k` and a polynomial `p : Poly k`, produces a countable discrete field `K`, a ring homomorphism `k -> K`, and a proof that this is a splitting field of `p`.

#### Generic Construction Framework

- **`RootFieldData`**: Record packaging a one-step root extension: a field `RootField` extending `k` via `rootFieldHom`, a `root` element that satisfies `polyMapEval p root = 0`, and a witness `RootField-gen` that every element of `RootField` is the evaluation at `root` of some polynomial over `k`. The predicate `P` is required to hold of `RootField`.
- **`SplittingFieldData`**: Class abstracting the data needed to iterate the construction: a field predicate `P`, a polynomial property `S` closed under ring homomorphisms (`S-hom`) and division (`S-factor`), and a `nextField` operation producing a `RootFieldData` for any nonzero non-invertible polynomial satisfying `P` and `S`.
  - **`makeSplittingField`**: Builds a splitting field for `p` by induction on `degree p`. The recursive helper `aux` adjoins a root via `nextField`, divides `p` by `(x - root)` using `rootDiv_*`, and recurses on the quotient.
- **`CountableSplittingFieldData`**: Instance of `SplittingFieldData` with `P = Countable` and trivial `S`. The `nextField` step constructs the new field as the factor ring `k[x]/m`, where `m` is a maximal ideal containing `(p)` (from `poly-maximal-ideal`), with `root` the class of `x` and countability transferred via `factor-countable`.
- **`poly-eval`**: Lemma that evaluating `polyMap polyHom p` at the canonical indeterminate `padd 1 0` recovers `p`; used to verify that the constructed root is indeed a root of `p` in the factor ring.
