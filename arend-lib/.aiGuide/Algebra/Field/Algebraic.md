### Algebra.Field.Algebraic

Algebraic and integral extensions of discrete fields, and their relationship to zero-dimensionality.

This module connects integrality, finite generation, and zero-dimensionality for ring extensions over a discrete field. Its central technical device is a Kronecker-style "change of variables" substitution that turns a non-zero multivariate polynomial into a univariate polynomial with an invertible leading coefficient, allowing one to extract an integral generator from any algebraic relation. From this it derives that integral extensions of discrete fields are zero-dimensional, that polynomial images of integral elements over a field form a discrete field, and that finitely generated algebra extensions are integral exactly when the target ring is zero-dimensional.

#### Integrality and Zero-Dimensionality

- **`integralExt-zeroDim`**: Any integral extension `f : K → R` of a discrete field is zero-dimensional, i.e. every element `a : R` satisfies `pow a n = pow a (suc n) * b` for some `b`. Reduces via the integral relation to a univariate `poly-zeroDim` lemma.
- **`integral_dom-zeroDim`**: Zero-dimensionality descends along an injective integral extension `f : R → S`: if `S` is zero-dimensional, so is `R`. The auxiliary `aux` performs induction on the degree of the integrality witness, reducing the problem one coefficient at a time.

#### Polynomial Image as a Field

- **`polyImage_field`**: For a discrete field `K`, an impotent commutative ring `E`, a homomorphism `f : K → E`, and an integral element `a : E`, the subring `polyImage f a` (the image of the evaluation map `K[X] → E`) is itself a discrete field. Combines `integralExt-zeroDim` with `zeroDim->field`.

#### Reduction of Multivariate Algebraic Relations

- **`mPoly_integral`**: Given `f : K → S` and a non-zero multivariate polynomial `p` vanishing on `(a, l)`, constructs an intermediate ring `E` with maps `K → E → S` such that `E` is generated by shifted variables `l j - pow a (d j)`, the embedding `E → S` is injective, every `l j` is a polynomial over `E` evaluated at `a`, and `a` is integral over `E`. This implements the Kronecker substitution that converts simultaneous algebraicity into univariate integrality.
- **`mPoly_integralExt`**: Extends `mPoly_integral` to a full integral extension when `f : K → S` is finitely generated by `l`. Produces an `E` with `S` integral over the image of `E`, using `integralExt-finite-char` to lift integrality of the leading variable to the whole algebra.

#### Change of Variables (Kronecker substitution)

- **`change-vars`**: Substitution `Xⱼ ↦ lⱼ + Y^(d^j)` that turns an `n`-variable polynomial into a univariate polynomial in `Y`. The exponent gap `d^j` ensures distinct monomials produce distinct degrees.
- **`change-vars_map`**: Naturality of `change-vars` under a ring homomorphism.
- **`change-vars_eval`**: Evaluating the substituted polynomial at `a` recovers a multivariate evaluation at `lⱼ + pow a (d^j)`.
- **`change-vars_BigSum`**, **`change-vars_*c`**, **`change-vars_monomial`**: `change-vars` distributes over sums, scalar multiplication, and on monomials reduces to a `permSet-univ` product of shifted linear factors.
- **`permSet-univ_monic`**: A `permSet-univ` product of monic polynomials is monic, with combined degree the sum of degrees.

#### Leading Coefficients and Degree Bookkeeping

- **`MPoly_poly`**: Given a non-zero multivariate polynomial in canonical form `BigSum (msMonomial (pcⱼ) (plⱼ))` with invertible coefficients `pcⱼ`, distinct monomials `plⱼ`, and gap `d` larger than every `permSet-length (plⱼ)`, the substituted polynomial has some degree `m` whose coefficient is invertible. This is the key lemma producing an integral relation from a multivariate relation.
- **`MPoly_poly-field`**: Specialization of `MPoly_poly` to the discrete-field case, where any non-zero polynomial canonicalizes with invertible coefficients.
- **`group`**: Builds an `EPerm` rearranging an array of `Fin n` into runs of equal indices via `count_EPerm`.
- **`groupSum`**: Rewrites `BigSum (map f l)` as a weighted sum `BigSum (natCoef (count l j) * f j)`.
- **`leadCoef_BigSum`** / **`leadCoef_BigSum_/=0`**: For a sum of polynomials with strictly distinct degree bounds `pd`, the leading coefficient of the sum equals the leading coefficient of the term with the maximum bound. The `_/=0` variant handles the non-empty case explicitly.
- **`poly-degrees`**: Combines the above to show that a sum `∑ pcⱼ *c lⱼ` of `*c`-scaled monic polynomials with distinct degrees and invertible scalars has an invertible leading coefficient at the maximum degree.

#### Characterization of Integral Extensions

- **`finGenExt-char`**: For a finitely generated algebra `f : K → E` over a discrete field, `f` is an integral extension iff `E` is zero-dimensional. Forward direction is `integralExt-zeroDim`; backward is `aux`.
- **`finGenExt-char.aux`**: The non-trivial direction — given zero-dimensionality of `S` and a finite generating set `l`, produces integrality of every element. Proceeds by induction on the number of generators, using `mPoly_integralExt` to peel off one generator at a time.
