### Algebra.Field.Algebraic

Algebraic and integral extensions over discrete fields, connecting integrality to zero-dimensionality and finite generation.

#### Integrality and Zero-Dimensionality

- **`integralExt-zeroDim`**: An integral extension `f : K -> R` from a discrete field forces `R` to be zero-dimensional (every element satisfies `pow a n = pow a (suc n) * b` for some `b`).
- **`integralExt-zeroDim.poly-zeroDim`**: Internal lemma extracting zero-dimensionality witnesses from a monic polynomial annihilating an element.
- **`integral_dom-zeroDim`**: Descent of zero-dimensionality along an injective integral extension `f : R -> S`: if `S` is zero-dimensional, so is `R`.
- **`integral_dom-zeroDim.aux`**: Inductive helper that walks the polynomial degree to produce the descent witness.

#### Field Structure on Polynomial Image

- **`polyImage_field`**: Given an integral element `a` over a discrete field `K` inside an impotent commutative ring `E`, the subring `polyImage f a` is itself a discrete field. Built by combining `integralExt-zeroDim` with `zeroDim->field`.

#### Multivariate Polynomial Integrality

- **`mPoly_integral`**: Core construction for algebraic dependence. Given a nonzero multivariate polynomial `p : MPoly (Fin (suc l.len)) K` vanishing at `(a :: l)`, produces an intermediate ring `E` with a generating tuple `l'`, an injective embedding `h : E -> S`, and exhibits `a` as integral over `E` while expressing each `l j` as a polynomial in `a` over `E`. This is the key step for reducing a transcendence-style argument to a single integral element.
- **`mPoly_integralExt`**: Extends `mPoly_integral` to an integral extension result: under a finite algebra generation hypothesis, produces an intermediate `E` over which `S` is integral and which is generated by one fewer element. Uses `integralExt-finite-char` to assemble the integrality conclusion.

#### Change of Variables (helpers for `mPoly_integral`)

- **`change-vars`**: Substitutes each variable `X_j` by `l_j + X^(d^j)` to convert a multivariate polynomial into a univariate polynomial over `R`, isolating a leading-coefficient structure.
- **`change-vars_map`**: Naturality of `change-vars` with respect to ring homomorphisms.
- **`change-vars_eval`**: Evaluating `change-vars p d l` at `a` equals the multivariate evaluation of `p` at `l_j + a^(d^j)`.
- **`change-vars_BigSum`**: `change-vars` distributes over finite sums.
- **`change-vars_*c`**: `change-vars` commutes with scalar multiplication.
- **`change-vars_monomial`**: Computes `change-vars` on a unit-coefficient monomial as a product of factors `padd pzero (a j) + (padd 1 0)^(d^j)`.

#### Leading Coefficient Analysis

- **`permSet-univ_monic`**: A product `permSet-univ ps c` of monic polynomials with prescribed degrees is again monic with degree given by the corresponding sum.
- **`MPoly_poly`**: Given a sum of monomials whose permutation-set exponents are all distinct, `change-vars` produces a polynomial with an invertible leading coefficient at a determinable degree. This is the geometric heart of the algebraic-independence-to-integrality reduction.
- **`MPoly_poly-field`**: Specialization of `MPoly_poly` over a discrete field: any nonzero `p : MPoly (Fin n) K` becomes (after `change-vars`) a polynomial with an invertible leading coefficient.

#### Sum and Index Combinatorics (inside `MPoly_poly`)

- **`MPoly_poly.group`**: Reorganizes an array of indices into runs of repeated values via an `EPerm` (extensional permutation).
- **`MPoly_poly.groupSum`**: Rewrites `BigSum (map f l)` as a sum weighted by occurrence counts: `BigSum (\lam j => natCoef (count l j) * f j)`.
- **`MPoly_poly.leadCoef_BigSum`**: For a nonempty array of polynomials with distinct prescribed degrees, identifies the maximum degree and shows the leading coefficient of the sum equals the leading coefficient of one summand.
- **`MPoly_poly.leadCoef_BigSum_/=0`**: Same statement for length-non-zero arrays without the explicit `suc n` length.
- **`MPoly_poly.poly-degrees`**: From a sum `p = BigSum (pc j *c l j)` of scalar multiples of monic polynomials with distinct degrees and invertible scalars, extracts a degree at which `p` has an invertible leading coefficient.

#### Characterization of Finite Algebras

- **`finGenExt-char`**: For a finitely generated `K`-algebra `E` over a discrete field, `f` is an integral extension iff `E` is zero-dimensional. The forward direction is `integralExt-zeroDim`; the reverse is bootstrapped via `mPoly_integralExt`.
- **`finGenExt-char.aux`**: Helper proving that zero-dimensionality plus algebra generation by a finite tuple implies integrality, by induction on the number of generators.
