### Algebra.Domain.IntegrallyClosed

Defines integrally closed domains — integral domains that are integrally closed in their field of fractions.

#### Classes

- **`IntegrallyClosedDomain`**: Extends `IntegralDomain` with the property `isIntegrallyClosedDomain`, asserting that the localization map `locMap` into the field of fractions (localization at the submonoid of nonzero elements) is integrally closed. Captures the standard notion of an integrally closed domain.
