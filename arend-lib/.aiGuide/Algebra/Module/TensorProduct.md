### Algebra.Module.TensorProduct

The tensor product of two modules over a commutative ring, defined as a truncated higher inductive type, together with its universal property.

For modules `A` and `B` over a commutative ring `R`, `TensorProduct A B` is the set of formal finite sums of pairs `(a,b)` (`tensorSum`) modulo permutation, zero, additivity in each argument, and the balancing relation `(r a, b) ~ (a, r b)`. It is an `R`-module (`TensorProductModule`) generated by the pure tensors `tensor a b`, and `tensor` is linear in each argument. The universal property of the tensor product is proved: every bilinear map `A × B -> C` factors through a linear map out of the tensor product (`tensorLift`) agreeing with it on pure tensors, and this factorization is unique (`tensorLift-unique`). The module also shows that `tensorLift` is additive and `R`-homogeneous in the bilinear map.

#### Construction

- **`TensorProduct`**: The tensor product `A ⊗ B` of `R`-modules, a truncated `\Set`-level data type with constructor `tensorSum` on arrays of pairs and path constructors for permutation, zero pairs, additivity in each argument and moving scalars between factors.
- **`tensor`**: The pure tensor `a ⊗ b`, the one-element sum `tensorSum ((a,b) :: nil)`.
- **`tensorSum_zro-left`**: Dropping a summand `(0,b)` from a `tensorSum` does not change it.
- **`tensorSum_zro-right`**: Dropping a summand `(a,0)` from a `tensorSum` does not change it.
- **`TensorProductModule`**: Instance making `TensorProduct A B` an `LModule R`: addition concatenates the arrays, zero is the empty sum, and scalars and negation act on the first components.

#### Pure Tensors

- **`tensor_+-left`**: `tensor` is additive in the first argument.
- **`tensor_+-right`**: `tensor` is additive in the second argument.
- **`tensor_*c-right`**: Scalars pass through the second argument: `a ⊗ (r b) = r (a ⊗ b)`.
- **`tensor-linear-left`**: For fixed `b`, `a ↦ a ⊗ b` is a linear map `A -> TensorProduct A B`.
- **`tensor-linear-right`**: For fixed `a`, `b ↦ a ⊗ b` is a linear map `B -> TensorProduct A B`.
- **`tensorSum_BigSum`**: A `tensorSum l` equals the big sum in the module of the pure tensors of the elements of `l`.
- **`tensor-repr`**: Every element of the tensor product is (merely) a finite sum of pure tensors.

#### Universal Property

- **`tensorLift`**: The linear map `TensorProduct A B -> C` induced by a bilinear map `f : BilinearMap A B C`, sending a sum to the sum of the values of `f`.
- **`tensorLift_tensor`**: `tensorLift f` sends `a ⊗ b` to `f a b`.
- **`tensorLift-unique`**: Two linear maps out of the tensor product that agree on all pure tensors are equal.
- **`tensorLift_+`**: `tensorLift` is additive in the bilinear map: `tensorLift (f + g) x = tensorLift f x + tensorLift g x`.
- **`tensorLift_*c`**: `tensorLift` commutes with scalar multiplication of the bilinear map: `tensorLift (r *c f) x = r *c tensorLift f x`.
