### Algebra.Module.Sub

Submodules of left modules over a ring, equipped with their lattice structure and constructions for ideal-scaling and cyclic submodules.

#### Submodule Structure

- **`SubLModule`**: Class extending `SubAddGroup` for submodules of an `LModule R`. Adds closure under scalar multiplication via `contains_*c : contains b -> contains (a *c b)`.
- **`topSubLModule`**: The improper (top) submodule containing every element of `M`.

#### Lattice of Submodules

- **`SubLModuleSemilattice`**: Instance of `TopMeetSemilattice` on `SubLModule` for a fixed ring `R` and module `M`. Order is pointwise inclusion, meet is pointwise intersection, top is `topSubLModule`.

#### Constructions

- **`*m`**: Product of a left ideal `I` and a submodule `S`, yielding the submodule of finite sums `Σ xᵢ *c sᵢ` with `xᵢ ∈ I` and `sᵢ ∈ S`. Closure under `+`, negation, and scalar multiplication is witnessed via list concatenation, negation of coefficients, and left-multiplication of coefficients (using `ideal-left`).
- **`OneDimSubmod`**: The cyclic submodule generated by a single element `v : L`, consisting of all scalar multiples `r *c v` for `r : R`.
