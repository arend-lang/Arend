### Algebra.Module.SubLModule

Submodules of left modules over a ring, with the lattice of submodules and ideal-submodule multiplication.

A `SubLModule` is a subset of an `LModule` closed under the additive group operations and scalar multiplication, extending `SubAddGroup` with the `contains_*c` closure condition. Each submodule yields an induced `LModule` structure (`IModule`) on the carrier subset; kernels and images of linear maps are submodules. Submodules form a top-meet-semilattice ordered by inclusion, with intersections as meets and the whole module as top. The product `I *m S` of an ideal and a submodule produces the submodule of finite sums of elements `r *c v` with `r ∈ I` and `v ∈ S`, generalizing ideal-module multiplication.

#### Submodule Class

- **`SubLModule`**: Extends `SubAddGroup (R : Ring)` with `S : LModule R` and the closure axiom `contains_*c : contains b -> contains (a *c b)` for any scalar `a : R`.
- **`IModule`**: Instance giving the induced `LModule S.R` on the subset of a submodule `S`, with addition and scalar multiplication inherited componentwise from the ambient module; `IModule.embed` is the inclusion as a `LinearMap`.

#### Constructions

- **`topSubLModule`**: The improper submodule containing every element of `M`; serves as the top of the submodule lattice.
- **`OneDimSubmod`**: The cyclic submodule `R · v` generated by a single vector `v : L`, defined as `{u | ∃ r : R, r *c v = u}`.

#### Lattice of Submodules

- **`SubLModuleSemilattice`**: `TopMeetSemilattice` instance on `SubLModule` over a fixed ring `R` and module `M`: order is pointwise inclusion of the underlying predicates, `meet` is the intersection submodule (`contains a := Σ (U a) (V a)`, closure proved componentwise), and `top` is `topSubLModule M`.

#### Kernels and Images

- **`KernelLModule`**: The kernel of a linear map `f`, as a submodule of `f.Dom` (extends `KernelAddGroup f`, closed under scalars since `f (a *c b) = a *c f b`).
- **`ImageLModule`**: The image of a linear map `f`, as a submodule of `f.Cod`.
- **`ImLModule`**: The image of `f` as a module in its own right, i.e. `IModule (ImageLModule f)`.
- **`ImLModuleLeftHom`**: The corestriction of `f` to its image, a `LinearMap f.Dom (ImLModule f)`.

#### Ideal-Submodule Product

- **`*m`**: Given a left ideal `I : LIdeal R` and a submodule `S` of `M`, produces the submodule whose elements are finite sums `Σ rᵢ *c vᵢ` with `rᵢ ∈ I` and `vᵢ ∈ M`, expressed via existence of an array `l` such that `a = M.BigSum (map (λ s => s.1 *c s.3) l)`.
