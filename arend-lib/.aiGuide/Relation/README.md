### Relation

This directory formalizes binary relations on sets: their basic propositional form, classical closure operations, and the theory of equivalence relations and set-quotients.

#### Relations and Closures

- **`Truncated.md`** — Defines `Rel A` as `\Prop`-valued binary relations and provides the transitive, transitive-reflexive, symmetric, and reflexive closures as minimal inductive datatypes.

#### Equivalence and Quotients

- **`Equivalence.md`** — Classes for transitive and equivalence relations with their free closures, the set-quotient HIT, and the standard toolkit for lifting functions, choice, and decidability through quotients.
